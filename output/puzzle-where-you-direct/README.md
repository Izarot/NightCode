# ⚡ Electric Circuit Puzzle

Light up the room. Wire by wire. Don't short yourself.

## How to Play
- **Move** with Arrow Keys / WASD
- **Right-click** to snap a wire segment (max 120px)
- **Space** to toggle Power Mode (up to 10 wires)
- Route power from Wall Outlets, Ceiling Panels, or the Battery Box to all 12 lights
- Avoid crossing wires (short circuit!) and don't overload the battery

## Win / Lose
- **Win:** All 12 lights ON — bonus for under 90 seconds 🏆
- **Lose:** Time runs out or too many broken wires block every path 💀
