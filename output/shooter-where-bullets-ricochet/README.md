# Welcome to Ricochet Rift – Where Every Shot Bounces Back!  

## How to Play  
Dive into **Ricochet Rift**, a neon-drenched 2D top-down shooter where your bullets *ricochet* off walls, enemies, and everything in between!  

- **Move**: Use **Arrow Keys** or **WASD** to navigate your avatar through a chaotic arena.  
- **Aim & Shoot**: Point your mouse or tap on mobile to fire bullets that bounce off surfaces with realistic physics.  
- **Survive**: Dodge enemy fire, blast foes, and master the art of ricochets to rack up points.  
- **Power Up**: Fill your **Heat Meter** by taking damage or chaining kills to unlock temporary boosts.  

### Key Features  
- **Ricochet Chaos**: Every bullet bounces up to 3 times, retaining speed and changing direction based on wall angles.  
- **Enemy Variety**: Face off against Basic Grunts, Patrollers, and Seekers with unique behaviors.  
- **Neon Retro Vibes**: Glowy silhouettes, synthwave soundtrack, and particle explosions light up the arena.  
- **Mobile Ready**: Seamless touch controls with auto-aim assist for on-the-go action.
