# Physics Catapult – Ball Edition

## 🎯 Overview
Operate a catapult, launch balls, and destroy target structures in this 2D physics-based puzzle/action game. Each level challenges your timing, aiming skills, and physics intuition to clear obstacles, break blocks, and reach goals.

## 🕹️ How to Play
1. **Aim** – Drag the catapult arm to set launch angle (0°-180°) and pull back (0-300px) to determine power.
2. **Release** – Click to fire the ball. Watch the real-time parabolic trajectory preview.
3. **Destroy** – Hit breakable targets (blocks with 3 health) for points and chain reactions.
4. **Complete** – Clear all targets or reach the goal zone before running out of balls or time.

## 📱 Platform & Controls
- **Platform**: HTML5 Canvas (WebGL fallback) – works on desktop & mobile browsers
- **Controls**:
  - **Mouse/Touch** – Drag to aim, release to launch
  - **Pointer deadzone** – Ignores accidental micro-movements
  - **Optional mouse wheel** – Fine angle adjustments

## 🌟 Features
- ⚡ Realistic ball physics (gravity, air drag, elastic bounces)
- 🎮 Trajectory preview showing the ball's flight path
- ⭐ Three-star rating system based on accuracy and efficiency
- 🔥 Unlockable ball skins & catapult upgrades through stars
- 📊 Score tracking, timer, and level progression

## 🚀 Quick Start
Open the game in any modern browser and start Level 1! Drag, release, and watch your ball fly.
