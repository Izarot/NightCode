# 🦠 VIRAL.exe

*A cyberpunk tower-defense where YOU are the infection.*

---

## How to Play

**You're a digital virus.** Spread through the network. Corrupt every node. Capture the server. Don't get caught.

### Controls
| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| WASD / Arrows | Move |
| Spacebar | Pulse Burst (3x capture speed) |
| R | Restart |
| Esc | Pause |

### Node Types
- 🟢 **Data Nodes** — Easy prey. Just touch and hold.
- 🔴 **Firewall Nodes** — Active hunters. They shoot scan beams. Avoid or die.
- 🟡 **Router Nodes** — Quick capture. Reveals hidden paths.
- ⚪ **Server Nodes** — Your goal. Protected. Worth 1000 points.

### Tips
- Move **along connection lines** for +50% speed
- Use **Pulse Burst** when surrounded by firewalls
- Watch the **screen edges** — red pulses mean danger incoming
- The **minimap** shows paths you haven't explored
