# 🌌 Neon Labyrinth

> A glowing, synthwave-fueled dash through shifting mazes of light and danger.

---

## 🎮 What is this?

**Neon Labyrinth** is a bite-sized 2D arcade-puzzle game built in pure HTML5 Canvas. You spawn in a corner of a freshly generated maze, the exit pulses in the opposite corner, and the corridors are patrolled by relentless red **Sentinels**. Your job: stay alive, move fast, and reach the gold.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

### Controls
| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| **Move** | `W` `A` `S` `D` *or* Arrow Keys |
| **Restart Level** | `R` |
| **New Maze** | `N` |
| **Start / Retry** | Any Key / `Space` |

### The Rules
1. **Find the Exit** — a pulsing gold square hidden in the bottom-right of the maze.
2. **Avoid the Sentinels** — diamond-shaped red enforcers that patrol or orbit the corridors. Touch one and it's **Game Over**.
3. **Beat the Clock** — every millisecond counts. Your timer only stops when you quit.
4. **Stay Smooth** — your little green avatar has momentum. Brake, drift, and slide along walls. Don't slam into corners.

### Tips for Survival
- 👂 Listen for the **high-pitched beep** — it means a Sentinel is dangerously close.
- 🏃 Outrunning patrols is possible: you're slightly faster than them.
- 🧭 If a maze feels unfair, mash `N` for a fresh roll.
- 🎯 Wider corridors = more Sentinel traffic. Plan your route.
