# 🏗️ Tower Tetris

**Clear lines. Watch them rise. Survive the tower.**

A Tetris variant where completed lines don't disappear—they become rising obstacles! Place tetrominoes, clear lines, and dodge the growing tower before it crushes you.

## How to Play
- Move, rotate, and drop pieces to fill horizontal lines
- Completed lines transform into **Riser Lines** that shoot upward
- Dodge the rising tower while keeping the spawn area clear
- Game over when you collide with a riser or the top gets blocked

## Controls
- **Move:** A/D or ←/→
- **Rotate:** W/↑ or Z
- **Soft Drop:** S/↓
- **Hard Drop:** Space
- **Hold:** Shift
- **Pause:** Esc

## The Twist
Every line you clear builds the tower. The higher it climbs, the tighter the squeeze. Can you keep the tower from reaching the top?
