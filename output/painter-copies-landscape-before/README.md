# 🎨 CHROMA DRIFT - Painter's Race

Race the colors before they drift away!

You are a painter in a living landscape. The hills, lake, and flowers are constantly shifting hue. Run to each color zone, copy its *current* color onto your canvas, and finish your painting before time runs out — or the colors fade too far from the target!

## How to Play

1. **Move** with WASD / Arrow Keys
2. **Sprint** with Shift and **Dash** with Space
3. Stand near a glowing color zone and **hold E** to paint
4. Watch the Target vs. Sampled swatches — paint when the colors match!
5. Complete all zones with **>70% accuracy** before the timer ends to win

Beware: colors drift faster every level, and painting a color that's drifted too far will make a muddy stroke and hurt your accuracy.

## Controls

- **WASD / Arrows:** Move
- **Shift:** Sprint
- **Space:** Dash
- **E (Hold):** Paint / Copy Color
- **Esc:** Pause

## Features

- Drifting HSV color zones with real-time swatches
- 5 levels with faster drift, more zones, and tighter timers
- Painterly procedural Canvas art, particles, and dash trails
- Perfect / Good / Poor stroke scoring with time bonuses
