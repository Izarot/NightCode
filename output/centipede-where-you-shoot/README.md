# 🐛 Centipede Reimagined 🐛

A high-octane, top-down arcade shooter where survival is a winding, segmented nightmare! Defend your base from the relentless, wiggling descent of the neon centipede. Can you handle the speed as it ramps up?

## 🕹️ How to Play

**The Goal:** Destroy as many segments as possible without letting the centipede reach the bottom of your screen!

*   **Move:** Use your **Mouse**, **Touch**, or **Arrow Keys** to slide your cannon left and right.
*   **Shoot:** Click, Tap, or hit the **Space Bar** to fire cyan projectiles.
*   **Strategy:** 
    *   Destroying segments breaks the chain!
    *   If you kill the **Head**, a new one spawns, but the speed might just increase!
    *   Watch the **Speed Meter**—the longer you survive, the faster they come.
