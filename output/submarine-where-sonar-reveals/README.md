# 🌊 ECHO DEPTHS

*Your sonar shows you the world four seconds from now. It will never show you the world as it is.*

## How to Play

You're a heavy, drifty submarine in a pitch-black trench. You can barely see what's around you *now*—but your sonar reveals what will be there in **4 seconds**.

1. **Ping** (`SPACE`) to cast a ghostly snapshot of the future.
2. **Read the ghosts**: cyan wireframes show where hazards—and *you*—will be.
3. **Maneuver** (`WASD` / arrows) so future-you doesn't collide with future-danger.
4. **Survive** until the echo collapses into reality. Avert it, or take hull damage.
5. Reach the extraction beacon before your oxygen runs out.

### Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `W` / `↑` | Thrust forward |
| `S` / `↓` | Reverse / brake |
| `A` / `D` | Rotate |
| `Shift` | Boost |
| `SPACE` | Sonar ping |
| `F` | Toggle headlamp |
| `Esc` | Pause |

## The Catch

The present is almost invisible. You have a tiny headlamp and your ears. The future is gorgeous, cyan, and deadly. You aren't dodging what you see—you're dodging what you *saw*.
