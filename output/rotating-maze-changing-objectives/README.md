# 🌀 Rotating Labyrinth

*A dizzying puzzle-action game where the maze spins, but your wits must stay sharp.*

---

## 🎮 How to Play

**Goal:** Complete objectives (collect keys, reach exits, avoid hazards) before the timer runs out. The catch? **The entire maze rotates continuously**, shifting paths and turning dead-ends into highways.

### Controls
| Platform | Move | Pause |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Desktop** | `WASD` / `Arrow Keys` | `Esc` / `P` |
| **Mobile/Tablet** | On-screen Joystick | Top-Right Button |

### Core Loop
1.  **Spawn** at the entrance.
2.  **Read the Objective** (top-left HUD): *Collect 3 Keys*, *Reach the Exit*, *Survive the Laser*.
3.  **Navigate** the twisting corridors. Your controls stay relative to *you*, not the maze—up is always up.
4.  **Complete** the objective to score points and spawn the next challenge.
5.  **Survive** the difficulty ramp: rotation speeds up, timers shrink, hazards get meaner.

### Pro Tips
- **Watch the Mini-Map** (bottom center) for a non-rotated bird's-eye view.
- **Momentum is key**: Accelerate smoothly, don't tap keys.
- **Hazards stun, they don't kill** (usually). Losing lives costs score.
