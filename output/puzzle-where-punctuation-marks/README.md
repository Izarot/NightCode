# Punctuations

A word‑puzzle game where punctuation marks steer your word across a vibrant canvas.

## How to Play
- Move your word using the arrow keys (or swipe on mobile).  
- Tap/click punctuation marks to activate their special effects:  
  - **`.`** – slows the word down.  
  - **`!`** – speeds it up with a jitter.  
  - **`?`** – changes direction.  
  - **`,`** – pauses briefly.  
  - **`:`** – splits the word into two.  
  - **`–`** – merges nearby words.  
  - **`—`** – reverses direction for a short burst.  
- Guide the word to the target zone (bottom‑right corner) before the timer runs out.

## Controls
- **Keyboard:** Arrow keys to move.  
- **Touch:** Swipe to move; tap punctuation icons to activate.

## Features
- 5 progressively challenging levels.  
- Smooth physics, particle effects, and a retro‑futuristic visual style.  
- Real‑time score and timer display.  
- Simple pause/resume and restart options.
