# PhaseShift

**A neon-soaked 2D action-platformer where you build your own way up — one temporary platform at a time.**

Leap across floating islands, bridge bottomless pits, and dodge toxic hazards in a cyberpunk void. Your only tool? Pure phase energy.

## How to Play

**Move & Jump**
- **A / D or Arrow Keys:** Move left / right
- **Space / W:** Jump — tap for a short hop, hold for a full jump
- You get coyote time and jump buffering, so near-misses still feel fair

**Phase Platforms**
- **Hold Left Mouse Button:** Spawn a platform toward your cursor
- Hold longer for a longer platform (40px - 300px)
- Each platform costs **25 energy** and lasts **4 seconds** before fading
- **Shift:** Lock platform direction horizontal
- **Ctrl:** Lock platform direction vertical

**Energy**
- Max energy: **100**
- Regenerates at **8 energy / second**
- Watch the **PHASE ENERGY** bar bottom-left — no energy, no platform!

**Goal**
Reach the glowing portal at the end of each level. Avoid pits, spikes, and saw blades — checkpoints will respawn you with brief invincibility, but you'll pay an energy penalty.

**Pro Tips**
- Watch the red shrinking ring — a platform is about to expire!
- The crosshair turns pink when you don't have enough energy to spawn
- Chain platforms quickly, but don't get stranded mid-air

## Features

- Native HTML5 Canvas rendering with neon bloom and particles
- Frame-rate independent physics with `requestAnimationFrame`
- Procedurally generated floating islands with parallax backgrounds
- Custom crosshair, glassmorphism HUD, and stability timer
