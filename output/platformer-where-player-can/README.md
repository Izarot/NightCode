# INVERTIGO

A 2D HTML5 Canvas palette-inversion platformer. Toggle palettes to reveal hidden paths, solidify bridges, and dodge phase-specific hazards. Use A/D to move, Space to jump, Shift to invert—then collect all Prisms and reach the Exit Rift!

## How to Play

- **Move:** A / D or ← / → Arrow Keys  
- **Jump:** Space or W / ↑ Arrow  
- **Invert Palette:** Shift or E (0.25 s cooldown, shown as a ring)  
- **Restart:** R  
- **Pause:** Esc or P  

Collect all 3 Prisms per level and reach the glowing Exit Rift to advance. Palette inversion makes hidden geometry solid—watch for faint outlines that hint at what’s there when inactive. Toggle mid‑air, time your shifts with moving platforms, and avoid phase‑specific hazards!
