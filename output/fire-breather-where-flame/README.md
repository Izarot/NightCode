# Fire Breather

A 2D side‑scrolling action/platformer where you breathe fire, collect fuel, and survive as long as possible.

## How to Play
- **Move** left/right with **A/D** or the arrow keys.  
- **Jump** with **Space** (or **W**) or a tap on mobile.  
- **Breathe fire**: hold **Space** (or touch) to charge, then release to shoot a flame.  
- **Collect fuel** pellets and crystals to keep your breath strong.  
- **Defeat enemies** (Flame Imps, Dragon Riders, Stone Golems, Flying Phoenixes) and avoid spikes, lava, and moving platforms.  
- **Dash** by double‑tapping a direction (has a cooldown).  

## Controls
| Keyboard | Touch |
|----------|-------|
| ←/→ Move | Swipe left/right |
| ↑ Jump | Tap |
| Space Hold to breathe | Hold to breathe (direction follows finger) |
| Double‑tap ←/→ Dash | Double‑tap to dash |

## Tips
- Watch the fuel bar—low fuel means a shorter flame.  
- When fuel ≥ 50, unleash a charged fireball for double damage and an ember trail.  
- Some enemies drop extra fuel when defeated.  

Enjoy the blaze!
