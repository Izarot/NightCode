# 🌌 Void Weaver

> *A cosmic sorcerer stealing the magic of fractured entities.*

## How to Play

**Move** with `WASD` or Arrow Keys
**Aim** with your mouse
**Shoot** with `Left Click` or `Space`
**Absorb** patterns with `E`
**Cycle** stored patterns with `Q`

### The Core Loop

1. **Fight** enemies using your currently absorbed patterns
2. **Survive** their bullet hell attacks
3. **Weave** — when an enemy dies, time slows and their bullets freeze
4. **Absorb** the Pattern Shard to steal their attack
5. **Evolve** your arsenal with each kill

### Win Condition
Survive 5 escalating Chambers and absorb **5 unique patterns** to face The Archon and complete your Void Codex.

### Lose Condition
Your fractured core reaches zero HP. Retry and lose one absorbed pattern.
