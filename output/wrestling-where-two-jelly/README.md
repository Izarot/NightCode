

# Jelly Joust: Slippery Plate Showdown

Welcome to the wobbling, sliding, and slamming world of *Jelly Joust*! Battle your friend as two gelatinous cubes on a slippery metallic plate. The goal? Pin your opponent, knock them off, or deplete their health. It's a physics-based wrestling match where every collision counts!

## How to Play

### Controls
- **Player 1 (Red Jelly):**
  - **WASD:** Move (W = Jump, A/D = Strafe, S = Crouch)
  - **J/K:** Attack (Grab/Slam)
  - **L:** Block/Counter

- **Player 2 (Blue Jelly):**
  - **Arrow Keys:** Move (↑ = Jump, ←/→ = Strafe, ↓ = Crouch)
  - **N/M:** Attack (Grab/Slam)
  - **,** (Comma): Block/Counter

### Key Mechanics
- **Grab & Slam:** Get close to your opponent and press attack to grab. While holding, press attack again to slam them into the plate for damage.
- **Pin Attempt:** Hold grab and crouch for 3 seconds to pin your opponent in the center zone.
- **Dash:** Double-tap a direction for a quick burst of speed (3-second cooldown).
- **Slippery Surface:** The plate has low friction, so you'll slide and bounce! Stay on the plate—falling off means instant elimination.

### Victory Conditions
- Pin your opponent in the center for 3 seconds.
- Reduce their health to 0.
- Knock them off the plate.

## Game Features
- **Physics-Powered Chaos:** Soft-body jelly deformation, momentum, and slippery plate mechanics.
- **2-Player Local Multiplayer:** Challenge a friend on the same keyboard!
- **Multiple Modes:** Time Attack, Survival, and Training.
- **Dynamic Visuals:** Translucent jellies, particle effects, and a starry void background.
