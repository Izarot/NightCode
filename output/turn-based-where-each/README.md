# 🎮 TILE-STRAT: EXPANSION

> **Build. Place. Conquer.** A turn-based tile-placement strategy game where every square matters.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **Pick a Tile** — Choose from your tray of terrain types (Forest, Water, Mountain, Grass, and more).
2. **Place It** — Click a valid spot on the grid. Watch the ghost tile preview where you'll land.
3. **Watch It Resolve** — Adjacency combos kick in! Forest + Water = Oasis. Mountain + Forest = Fortress. Chain bonuses for massive resource gains.
4. **Manage Resources** — Gold, Mana, and Population are generated each turn based on your board layout. Spend wisely.
5. **Move Units** — Your units pathfind across terrain using A* (mountains cost 3×, grass costs 1×). Smooth Lerp interpolation keeps movement buttery.
6. **Win or Lose** — Reach the resource threshold or hold the Core tile to win. Run out of space or lose the Core? Game over.

### 🎯 Quick Tips
- **Combos are king** — adjacency effects stack, so plan clusters, not isolated tiles.
- **Check the Mini-Map** — bottom-left for the big picture.
- **Watch the Combat Log** — bottom-right panel tells you exactly what just happened.

---

## ⚙️ Under the Hood

- **Engine:** Entity-Component-System (ECS) with a Grid-Based Spatial Hash Map
- **Rendering:** HTML5 Canvas 2D with layered rendering (Background → Tiles → Entities → FX)
- **Visuals:** Glow & shadow shaders via `globalCompositeOperation`, minimalist vector aesthetic
- **Performance:** Targets 60 FPS with up to 500 tiles and 50 units
- **Input:** Mouse & touch mapped to grid coordinates via `Math.floor(mouseX / tileSize)`
