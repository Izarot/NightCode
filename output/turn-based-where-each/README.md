# Line-Turn

A tiny turn-based canvas game where every line you draw becomes a moving obstacle. Survive, score, and try not to get tangled in your own neon mess.

## How to Play

- Move with **Arrow Keys** or **swipe**.
- Press **Space** or **tap** to draw a line in your last movement direction.
- Each turn lasts **5 seconds**. If you don’t draw, a line appears anyway.
- Dodge your moving lines and survive as long as possible.
- Press **P** to pause and **M** to mute.

## Goal

Stay alive, rack up points, and reach higher levels. Perfect runs earn bonus points.
