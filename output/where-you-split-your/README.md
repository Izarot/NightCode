# 🌮 MITOSIS: Precision Puzzle-Platformer

## How to Play
- **Split**: Press `KEY_SPLIT` or `Gamepad West` to divide your primary fragment into two smaller ones (max 3 splits). Use them to solve puzzles, activate switches, or reach new areas!
- **Merge**: Hold `KEY_MERGE` or `Gamepad East` near a smaller fragment to combine them back into one. Bigger = Stronger! (Merge cooldown: 0.2s)
- **Switch**: Tap `KEY_TAB` or `Gamepad RB/LB` to cycle between your active fragments. Each has unique abilities based on size!
- **Idle States**: Right-click or press `KEY_Q` to set uncontrolled fragments to Stay, Follow, or Hold Ground (for pressure plates).
- **Puzzle Mechanics**: Use mass gates, laser receivers, and phase vents to progress. Heavy fragments block lasers, light ones phase through!
