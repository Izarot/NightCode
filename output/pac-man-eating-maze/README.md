# 🧱 WallCrunch Escape

**Pac-Man eats walls to carve his escape.** The more walls you crunch, the closer the exit opens — and the angrier the ghosts get.

## How to Play
- **Arrow keys / WASD** — move, swipe on mobile
- **Eat walls** (-8 energy, +3 refill) to shrink the maze
- **40% walls gone** → exit door appears (15s window!)
- **10+ walls eaten** → ghosts turn edible for 8s
- **Reach the exit** before time runs out to advance

## Quick Stats
- Energy regenerates +1/s • Max 100
- Score: Wall 10pts • Ghost 200–1600 • Exit 5000
- 3 lives • Indestructible border • Seeded mazes
