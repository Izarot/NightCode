# Wall‑Kick & Hook  

A 2‑D HTML5 canvas platformer where you **can’t jump** – you kick off walls and swing with a grappling hook to reach the goal.

## How to Play
- **Move**: ← / → arrows (or virtual joystick)  
- **Wall‑Kick**: Hold **Space** or **↑** to charge, then tap **Shift** to launch off a wall  
- **Grappling Hook**: Press **E** to shoot, **R** to retract or let go while swinging  
- **Goal**: Collect Echo Crystals, dodge spikes/enemies, and touch the exit flag with low vertical speed  
- **Lives**: 3 hearts – lose a life on spikes or enemy contact; game over when lives hit 0
