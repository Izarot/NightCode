# Rotating Gravity Match-Three

A 2D HTML5 Canvas puzzle game where the world spins every time you clear a match. Match tiles, ride the shifting gravity, and keep your combo streak alive!

## How to Play

- **Move:** Use Arrow Keys or WASD to guide your character across the grid.
- **Swap:** Click or tap a tile, then drag to an adjacent tile to swap them.
- **Match:** Line up 3 or more identical tiles horizontally or vertically to clear them.
- **Gravity Shift:** After every cascade, gravity rotates 90° clockwise — Down → Right → Up → Left → Down. Tiles and your character re-orient instantly.
- **Combo:** Chain cascades together to multiply your score. The combo resets if you go 5 seconds without a match.
- **Goal:** Reach the target score before you run out of moves!

## HUD
