# 🖋️ LINE PHYSICS

A minimalist physics sandbox where your doodles become walls. Sketch freely — then watch the chaos unfold.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Click and drag** anywhere on the canvas to draw lines.
2. Objects (circles and boxes) **spawn from the top** and fall under gravity.
3. They **bounce, roll, and collide** with whatever you've drawn.
4. Experiment — sketch ramps, walls, funnels, or abstract mazes.
5. Press **C** to clear your lines, or **R** to fully reset.

## ⌨️ Controls

| Action | Input |
|---|---|
| Draw line | Left-click + drag |
| Toggle spawning | Spacebar |
| Clear lines | C key |
| Full reset | R key |

## 💡 Tips

- Quick strokes = sharp obstacles; slow sweeping curves = gentle slides.
- Crossing lines create unpredictable ricochets — embrace the chaos.
- Watch the object counter climb as you build more elaborate traps.
