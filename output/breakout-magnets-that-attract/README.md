## 🎮 How to Play

Move your paddle, **flip your polarity**, and bend the ball's path through every brick!

| Control | Action |
|---------|--------|
| 🖱️ **Mouse** / **← →** | Move paddle |
| **Spacebar** | Toggle polarity (N ↔ S) |
| **Q / E** | Force NORTH (+) / SOUTH (−) |
| **Right-click** | Quick polarity swap |
| **Touch** | Drag to move on mobile |

### The Magnetic Rule

Master the curve. Chain combos. Clear all 8 levels without dropping a ball.
