HOW TO PLAY

Move: Mouse / ← →   Launch: Click / Space

Switch Polarity: Space   Force +: Q   Force −: E   Right-Click

Pause: P or Esc

Your paddle and ball carry NORTH (+) or SOUTH (−) polarity.

Opposite polarities attract; like polarities repel. Curve the ball around obstacles, pull it from danger, or rocket it through dense brick formations.

Sustain curved flight to build a combo multiplier. Clear levels without flipping polarity for a +500 style bonus!
