# 🎵 Note Jumper

*A rhythm-platformer where every jump makes music!*

## 🎮 How to Play

**Move:** `A` / `D` or `←` / `→`  
**Jump:** `Space`, `W`, or `↑`  
**Pause:** `P` or `Escape`  
**Restart:** `R`

### The Goal
Jump across floating musical notes! Land on them to play their tone and earn points. Hit notes in rhythm with the beat for **Perfect** scores (💯 points), or just keep the melody going for **Good** hits (✨ 50 points).

### Pro Tips
- 🎹 Notes glow different colors by pitch — the world is your instrument!
- ⏱️ Variable jump height: tap for short hops, hold for high leaps
- 🔥 Chain hits together to build your combo multiplier (up to 4x!)
- 🐑 Miss a note and your combo resets — stay in the flow

### The Soundtrack
Three levels of increasing tempo:
1. **First Steps** — 60 BPM (beginner-friendly)
2. **Night Walk** — 90 BPM (moderate challenge)
3. **Finale** — 120 BPM (expert territory)
