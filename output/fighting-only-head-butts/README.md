# 🎮 CRANIUM CRASH

## How to Play

Welcome to **Cranium Crash**, a high-intensity, physics-driven 2D arcade fighting game where all combat is executed exclusively through head-butts!

### Controls

- **WASD / Arrow Keys**: Move your character around the arena.
- **Space / J**: Initiate a head-butt attack. Press and hold to wind up, then release to strike!
- **Shift / K**: Dash for a high-velocity movement.

### Game Mechanics

- **Wind-up State**: Pressing the 'Attack' key initiates a 15-frame animation/physics state where your character's center of mass shifts forward, increasing potential kinetic energy.
- **Collision Layers**: 
  - `Layer 1 (Environment)`: Static boundaries and platforms.
  - `Layer 2 (Player)`: Dynamic circular hitboxes for heads; rectangular hitboxes for bodies.
  - `Layer 3 (Hitbox)`: Transient high-velocity circles generated during the "Head-butt" frame.
- **Head-Butt Physics (The "Impact" Mechanic)**:
  - **Momentum Transfer**: The force of the impact is calculated based on your character's velocity vector at the moment of collision.
  - **Recoil System**: Upon impact, both players receive a reciprocal impulse vector. If your character's velocity is below a threshold, they enter a `STUNNED` state (0.5s).
  - **Knockback Scaling**: Impact force scales with the "Wind-up" duration.
- **Combat Loop**:
  - **The Dash-Strike**: A rapid forward lunge. If the head-hitbox intersects the opponent's head-hitbox during the active frames, a "CRITICAL IMPACT" is triggered.
  - **Stun/Daze**: Continuous small-scale impacts reduce a "Daze Meter." When the meter fills, the player enters a "Wobble" state, making them unable to move for 1.5 seconds.
  - **Ring-Out**: The arena is a floating platform. If your character's Y-coordinate exceeds the platform bounds, they are eliminated.

### Visuals

- **Art Style**: High-contrast, chunky 2D Vector Art (Neo-Retro).
- **Character Design**: Exaggerated, oversized heads with expressive facial features (eyes widening on impact). Squash-and-stretch deformation applied to the head during the wind-up and impact frames.
- **Visual Effects (VFX)**:
  - **Impact Sparks**: Radial particle bursts (yellow/white) at the point of collision.
  - **Motion Blur**: Trailing "ghost" sprites behind players during high-velocity dashes.
  - **Screen Shake**: Dynamic camera shake intensity scaled to the `Impact Force` variable.
  - **Hit-Stop**: A 3-frame freeze of the entire game engine upon a "Critical Impact" to emphasize weight.

### UI Elements

- **Health/Daze Bars**: Two thick, segmented bars at the top-left and top-right.
  - `Health Bar`: Red, decreases on impact.
  - `Daze Bar`: Yellow, fills when taking hits; resets on successful head-butts.
- **Combo Counter**: Floating text (e.g., "3 HIT COMBO!") that scales up and fades out near the point of impact.
- **Timer**: Centralized digital clock (MM:SS) in a bold, monospaced font.
- **Round Overlay**: Large, stylized text ("FIGHT!", "K.O.!") using a heavy sans-serif font with a drop shadow, appearing mid-screen during state transitions.
