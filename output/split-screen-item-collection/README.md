
# ⚡ DUALITY DRIFT ⚡
### *A Split-Screen, Momentum-Based Reality Brawler*

Two realities. One screen. Zero mercy.

---

### 🎮 How to Play
You and your opponent occupy mirrored, vertically split universes. Collect **Resonance Shards**, bank them in your local **Anchor Zone**, and reach **100 Stability** before the 3-minute timer expires.

Every shard you collect spawns a dangerous **Echo** in your opponent's screen.

#### Core Loop:
Navigate Physics → Collect Shard → Trigger Cross-Screen Echo → Adapt & Bank → Win

---

### ⌨️ Controls

| Action | Player 1 | Player 2 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Move | `W` / `A` / `S` / `D` | `Arrow Keys` |
| Jump | `Space` or `W` | `Arrow Up` |
| Dash | `Left Shift` | `Numpad1` / `Right Shift` |
| Bank | `E` | `Enter` / `Numpad2` |

Bank: stand in your Anchor Zone (P1 left edge, P2 right edge) and hold Bank for 1.2s.

---

* Momentum physics with coyote time and jump buffering
* Cross-screen echoes
* Neon vector graphics and split-screen
