

# ⚡ DUALITY DRIFT ⚡
### *A Split-Screen, Momentum-Based Reality Brawler*

Two realities. One screen. Zero mercy. 

---

### 🎮 How to Play
You and your opponent occupy mirrored, vertically split universes. The core objective is simple: collect **Resonance Shards**, bank them in your local **Anchor Zone**, and reach **100 Stability** before the 3-minute timer expires. 

But reality is linked! Every shard you collect spawns a dangerous **Echo** (hazards, gravity wells, or reality fractures) in your opponent's screen. Use your momentum, master the drift, and destabilize your opponent before they destabilize you!

#### Core Gameplay Loop:
$$\text{Navigate Physics} \rightarrow \text{Collect Shard} \rightarrow \text{Trigger Cross-Screen Echo} \rightarrow \text{Adapt \& Bank} \rightarrow \text{Win}$$

### ✨ Key Features
*   **The "Drift" Physics Model:** Momentum-based movement with coyote time, jump buffering, and wall-slides for high-skill ceiling micro-adjustments.
*   **Cross-Screen Echoes:** Collecting shards spawns hazards like **Phantom Spikes** or gravity-warping **Gravity Wells** on the opponent's screen. Use them to block paths or force mistakes.
*   **Neon Vector Minimalism:** Vibrant vector graphics, bloom layer effects, screen-shake trauma system, and procedural dash trails.
*   **Adaptive Audio:** Dynamic synth layers that intensify as your stability climbs or as the clock runs down.

---

### 🏆 Game Modes
*   **Versus Mode:** Local split-screen 1v1 (or 1vAI). Rubber-banded spawn logic ensures the trailing player has a chance to catch up.
*   **Co-op Mode:** Shared stability pool. Requires grabbing the **Sync Core** simultaneously to trigger the win condition.
