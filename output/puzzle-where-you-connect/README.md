# Pipe-Flow Puzzle: Water Works Wonderland! 🌊

**Drip, Drop, Done!**  
Channel water from the source to blooming flowers by building and rotating pipes. Time’s ticking—make every drop count!

---

## How to Play 🛠️

1. **Select Your Pipes**  
   - Grab pieces from the palette on the right. Choose straights, corners, T-junctions, or sneaky leaks!

2. **Place & Pivot**  
   - Click/tap to drop pipes onto the grid.  
   - Right-click or swipe to rotate them 90° clockwise.  

3. **Flow the H2O**  
   - Hit the **Flow** button (or enable auto-flow) to watch water race through your network.  

4. **Satisfy the Flowers** 🌸  
   - Each flower needs a steady stream. Connect them all before the timer dries up!  

5. **Tweak & Triumph**  
   - Remove pieces with a long-press or right-click.  
   - Use pressure valves and power-ups to outsmart tricky layouts.  

---

## Pro Tips 💡
- **Leaks are bad news**—they drain your water meter!  
- **Pressure tokens** unlock splitters and T-junctions.  
- **Glow-up!** When a flower is happy, it shines bright.
