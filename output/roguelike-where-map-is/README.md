# Room Shuffle

**A procedurally-generated roguelike where the dungeon rearranges itself between levels!**

## How to Play

Navigate your hero through a grid of mysterious rooms, each containing unique layouts, traps, treasures, and enemies. Defeat all foes in a room to progress to the next level — but beware! The entire dungeon shuffles its rooms after each victory, making your previous knowledge obsolete.

### Controls
- **Arrow Keys / WASD**: Move your character
- **Spacebar**: Interact with chests, open doors, or attack enemies

### Core Mechanics
- Explore procedurally-generated rooms (8×8 grid)
- Battle 3 types of enemies: Goblins, Skeletons, and Shadows
- Collect up to 5 items from treasure chests
- Avoid deadly traps and navigate wall mazes
- Survive permadeath across increasingly complex levels

### Victory Conditions
- Defeat the boss in the final room
- Complete all levels without dying

### Death Means...
- Starting over from level 1 with fresh stats
- Permadeath is permanent — no continues!
