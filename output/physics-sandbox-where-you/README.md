# 🎨 InkBridge: Lemming Physics Sandbox

> *Draw. Solidify. Survive. Repeat.*

Welcome to **InkBridge**, a 2D physics puzzle sandbox where you play as a master architect wielding **magical liquid ink**. Your mission? Guide a horde of clumsy, physics-driven Lemmings across deadly chasms, spinning saws, and spike pits by drawing bridges that crystallize into rigid structures right before your eyes.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### **The Core Loop**
**Draw → Solidify → Simulate → Optimize**

1.  **🖌️ DRAW** — Hold **Left Click / Drag** to stream wet ink from your cursor. It flows like real paint—dripping, curving, and sagging under gravity.
2.  **⏳ WAIT** — Ink stays *liquid* for ~2 seconds. Lemmings **slide right off** wet ink! Watch it shimmer and crystallize into a solid, walkable polygon.
3.  **▶️ RELEASE** — Hit **Play** (or `Space`). Lemmings spawn and walk forward autonomously. They climb gentle slopes, slide down steep ones, and *splat* if they fall too far.
4.  **🔧 ITERATE** — Bridge snapped? Lemmings drowned? Hit **Reset**, adjust gravity, grab the **Eraser** (refunds 50% ink!), and try a truss design this time.
