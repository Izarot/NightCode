# GYRE — Rotational Puzzle Platformer

## How to Play
Become a kinetic entity trapped in a spinning chamber! By rotating the entire level and its gravity vector, you dictate which way is "down" and navigate neon platforms, dodge hazards, and collect energy orbs to unlock the exit.

### The Basics
1. **Rotate** the world to shift gravity and align your path.
2. **Move** left and right relative to the new "down".
3. **Jump** to leap over spikes, lasers, and crumbling blocks.
4. **Collect** all 3 energy orbs to activate the exit portal.
5. **Reach** the exit to complete the level!

### Controls
- **Desktop:** `Q`/`E` or `←`/`→` to Rotate, `A`/`D` or `←`/`→` to Move, `Space` to Jump.
- **Mobile:** Drag to Rotate, Tap to Jump.
- **Gamepad:** Left Stick to Move, `L1`/`R1` to Rotate, `A` to Jump.
