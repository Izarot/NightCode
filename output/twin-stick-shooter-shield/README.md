## 🎮 How to Play

Blast through waves of neon enemies in this fast-paced arcade shooter! Your spacecraft auto-fires plasma shots, but the real magic is your **reflective shield**.

### Controls
- **WASD / Left Stick** — Fly around with smooth inertia
- **Mouse / Right Stick** — Aim your weapons
- **Spacebar / Right Click (HOLD)** — Activate shield
- **ESC** — Pause the chaos

### The Shield Mechanic
Hold your shield button to reflect enemy projectiles right back at them! 🔄

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Shield active | Drains 15% energy/sec |
| Reflected shot | Deals **150% damage** 💥 |
| Each reflection | Costs 25% energy |
| Deactivate | 2-second cooldown |

Watch your **circular energy ring**:
- 🔵 **Blue** — Full power
- 🟡 **Yellow** — Half charged  
- 🔴 **Red** — DANGER (pulses warning!)

### Tips for Survival
- Time your shield activations to chain reflections
- Combo multipliers reward consecutive reflects
- Watch for **Swarm enemies** (spread shots) and **Heavies** (explosive rounds)
- Survive 5 minutes or take down the boss to win!
