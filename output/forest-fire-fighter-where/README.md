

# 🚒 Forest Fire Fighter

> Save the trees, one bucket at a time!

Welcome to **Forest Fire Fighter**, a high-octane 2D survival game where you pilot a trusty firefighting aircraft against spreading wildfires! Use your wits and your water tank to protect the forest before it turns into ash.

## 🎮 How to Play

*   **Fly:** Point and click to steer your aircraft, or use **WASD** for manual precision.
*   **Drop Water:** Click to auto-aim and release water, or hit **Spacebar** for an emergency dump!
*   **Refill:** Watch your tank! Land near lakes, rivers, or refill stations to top up your 500-unit capacity.
*   **Strategize:** Water doesn't just put out fires—it creates **firebreaks**! Drop water ahead of the flames to stop them in their tracks.
*   **Watch the Wind:** Fire spreads fast with the wind. Keep an eye on the wind indicator and your fuel gauge!

## ⚠️ Pro Tips

*   Don't let your fuel run out, or you'll have to make an emergency crash landing!
*   Create firebreaks *before* the fire reaches them to save water and score big.
