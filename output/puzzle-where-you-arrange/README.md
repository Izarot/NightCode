#  🎯 MirrorTarget

**A neon-soaked puzzle-physics game where you bend light with mirrors to hit targets. Simple to learn, devilishly tricky to master.**

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Place Mirrors** – Drag mirror tiles from the right palette onto the grid. Tap/click to rotate in 45° increments.
2. **Aim & Fire** – Use the angle slider (0–360°) and power slider (0–100%) at the bottom, then hit **Fire**.
3. **Watch the Beam** – The cyan laser reflects off mirrors (law of reflection) and splits at prisms.
4. **Hit Targets** – Strike all colored targets (⭐ 🔴 🔵) to clear the level.
5. **Optimize** – Fewer mirrors + faster time = higher score. Earn ⭐⭐⭐ stars!

**Controls**
- **Mouse/Touch:** Drag to place, click to rotate, right-click/long-press to delete
- **Keyboard:** `R` rotate selected mirror • `Delete` remove • Arrows fine-tune sliders
- **Undo/Redo:** Up to 10 actions (toolbar buttons)
