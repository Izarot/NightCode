# COMET SNAKE 🌠

Pilot a glowing comet through a neon asteroid field. Eat debris to grow a tapering particle tail, stack combo multipliers, and survive the accelerating void.

## How to Play

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrow Keys / WASD | Steer your comet |
| Space / P | Pause |
| Enter | Start / Restart |
| Swipe anywhere (touch) | Change direction |

**Goal:** Consume Ice Asteroids (+10 pts, +1 tail) and Golden Comet Fragments (+50 pts, +3 tail, speed immunity). Build combos (x1→x5) by eating within 4 seconds of your last meal. Avoid walls and your own tail tip. Speed ramps up with every bite — eat fast or get vaporized!

**Pro tips:** The tail is a continuous tapered particle trail, *not* blocky segments. Golden fragments appear every 5th asteroid. Space rocks spawn at scores 100/250/500 and are lethal.
