# Pong with Gravity Wells

## How to Play
- **Move paddles:**  
  - Left paddle – **W** (up) / **S** (down)  
  - Right paddle – **↑** (up) / **↓** (down)  
  - Touch: drag up/down on the left/right screen edges.
- **Inertia:** Paddles speed up/slow down smoothly; they have a max speed.
- **Gravity wells:**  
  - When a paddle nears a well (≤ 50 px) it feels a pull toward the well’s center.  
  - Hitting a well stuns the paddle for 1 s (reduced responsiveness).  
  - The ball bounces off paddles (horizontal velocity reversal) and is attracted/repelled by wells, slowing 30% while inside and showing a “suck” effect.
- **Score:** Send the ball past the opponent’s paddle to earn a point. Wells can block the ball—time your moves!
- **Pause/Restart:** Click the bottom‑center button or press **P**.

## Visuals & HUD

- Minimalist dark background with subtle starfield.  
- Top‑center score display, optional timer, and responsive UI.
