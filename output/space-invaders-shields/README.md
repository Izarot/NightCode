# 🚀 Starward Invaders

**Starward Invaders** is a retro-futuristic 2D top-down shooter where you defend your base against relentless waves of alien invaders. Blast them out of the sky, manage your shields, and survive as long as you can!

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### Objective
Destroy every alien in each descending wave before your base health reaches zero. Clear all waves to win!

### Controls

| Action | Desktop | Mobile |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Move** | Arrow keys (← ↑ →) | Virtual joystick |
| **Fire** | Spacebar | Tap center of screen |
| **Repair Shield** | R key | Tap repair button |
| **Pause** | ⏸ icon (top-center) | ⏸ icon (top-center) |

### Tips
- 🔹 Your **shield** regenerates when you're not taking damage — let it recharge between assaults.
- 🔹 Position your **defensive shield blocks** to intercept enemy fire and protect your base.
- 🔹 Unlock the **secondary bomb** at level 5 for explosive area damage.
- 🔹 Destroy an entire wave without taking damage for a **1.5× score bonus**!

### Scoring
- **10 points** per enemy destroyed (scales up each wave)
- **Bonus multiplier** for flawless wave clears
