# 💧 Water Drop Maze

Splash into a wobbly physics adventure! Guide your droplet through 50 fluid-filled levels, absorb tiny drops, dodge hazards, and flow your way to the exit.

## How to Play

- Use **WASD** or the **arrow keys** to roll, slide, and splash.
- Collect **blue droplets** to grow—but bigger drops are slower.
- Avoid **red evaporators**, oil slicks, and other shrinking hazards.
- Ride currents, bounce off walls, and hit pressure plates to open new paths.
- Reach the exit before time runs out to earn up to **3 stars**.

Mobile players can use the on-screen joystick and tap controls.
