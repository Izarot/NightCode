## ✨ What Is This?

Welcome to **Flock Formation** — a mesmerizing HTML5 puzzle game where you guide a flock of luminous birds into target silhouettes. Watch as your tiny flockers swirl and swirl, drawn to your cursor like moths to a moonbeam, until — *snap!* — they form the perfect shape.

## 🎮 How to Play

### 🐦 Your Mission
Each level shows a ghostly **target shape**. Move your birds into formation. Hold them there long enough to score!

### 🕹️ Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **Mouse Move** | Birds within range are drawn to your cursor ✨ |
| **Scroll Wheel** | Adjust attraction strength (1× – 5×) |
| **Right Click** | BOOM! Radial repulsion burst 💥 |
| **Space** | Restart the current level |
| **R** | Scatter birds to random positions |

### 🌟 Levels

1. **The Line** — A humble horizontal row of 5 *(par: 10s)*
2. **The Triangle** — Three vertices in harmony *(par: 15s)*
3. **The Circle** — Eight points in orbit *(par: 20s)*
4. **The Star** — A celestial pentagram *(par: 25s)*
5. **The Arrow** — Point the way forward *(par: 30s)*

### 🏆 Scoring

Earn up to **3 stars** per level by hitting the par time! Faster formations = brighter stars. Your total score climbs with each perfect arrangement.

## 🧠 Pro Tips

- 🎯 **Don't chase perfection instantly.** Let the flock settle before nudging the final pieces into place.
- 🌊 **Use the repulsion burst** to break up clumping when birds get too cozy.
- ⚖️ **Lower attraction power** for delicate adjustments; crank it up for sweeping moves.
- 👁️ **Watch the pattern outline** — it pulses brighter as you near success!
