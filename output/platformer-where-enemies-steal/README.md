# Echo Thief

## How to Play
- Move with **WASD** or **Arrow Keys** (8‑directional).  
- Jump with **Space** or **Up**.  
- Each step leaves a sound marker (pulsing circle).  
- Enemies (Echo Thieves) steal those markers, creating silent zones.  
- Retrieve stolen echoes by defeating or avoiding enemies to reactivate sound‑based puzzles (pressure plates, echo bridges, audio switches).  
- Collect all echoes to finish the level.
