# Weapon Mimicry Shooter

**A reactive 2D arena where enemies become your weapons.**

## How to Play
- **Move**: Arrow keys or WASD
- **Aim & Fire**: Hold Shift + Left Click (or Right Click)
- **Switch Weapons**: Press F1–F5 to swap between Pistol, Rifle, and Shotgun
- **Objective**: Shoot enemies! When an enemy dies, it instantly transforms into a clone of your currently equipped weapon and starts chasing you. Adapt your strategy as the battlefield mirrors your arsenal.
- **Survive** as long as possible, rack up kills, and master the ever-changing armory.
