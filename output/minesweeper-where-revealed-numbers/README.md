

# Living Field

A roguelike Minesweeper variant where revealed numbers are autonomous agents ("Critters") with distinct behaviors. The board is a living ecosystem. You don't clear the board—you survive the shifting logic long enough to extract the Core.

## How to Play

- **Reveal tiles** to uncover safe paths and spawn Critters. Each number (1–8) is a unique species with its own movement and special trait.
- **Queue actions** (Reveal, Flag, Deep Scan, Ping) and **commit turns** with Space. The simulation resolves, Critters move, interact, and evolve.
- **Manage resources:** Scan Charge (for actions), Stability (hit mines and lose), and Extraction Progress (stabilize tiles).
- **Use the Radial Menu** (right-click) for advanced actions like Lure, Suppress, or Analyze.
- **Win condition:** Reveal all non-mine tiles, stabilize all Critters, then trigger extraction.
