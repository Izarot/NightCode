# Symphonic Conductor: Baton of the Maestro

**Wave your baton, cue the orchestra, and conduct your way to a standing ovation!**

## How to Play
- Move your mouse/finger/trackpad to swing the virtual baton.  
- Aim at the colored zones (Strings = blue, Brass = red, Woodwinds = green, Percussion = yellow) when the playhead hits a cue block.  
- Faster swings = louder (forte); gentle moves = soft (piano).  
- Use sharp, short gestures for staccato notes; smooth loops for legato.  
- Press **Space** to start/pause, and **S/B/W/P** keys to mute/solo sections.  
- Keep the Harmony Meter full for maximum applause!
