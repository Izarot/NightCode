# Sandfall 🏜️

Don't stop moving, or the desert will eat you.

A neon-cyberpunk physics platformer where endless sand pours from above and you have to build, dig, and run your way out of being buried alive.

## How to Play

**Move:** `[ARROWS]` - Run left/right and jump. Keep moving!

**Build:** `[SPACE]` / Left Click - Drop a sand block to make platforms over spikes and pits.

**Dig:** `[SHIFT]` / Right Click - Blast sand away around you to clear a path.

**Survive:** Standing still fills your burial meter. Get buried = game over. Collect falling Dust to upgrade your build speed and dig radius.

**Controls Hint:** `[SPACE] Build | [SHIFT] Dig | [ARROWS] Move`

Built with HTML5 Canvas, Vanilla JS (ES6+), and a 60 FPS fixed-timestep loop.
