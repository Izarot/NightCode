# VOIDBREAKER: INVASION PROTOCOL

## Overview
A retro-inspired space shooter where you pilot the Voidbreaker starship against an alien armada. Defend Earth from the Hollow Collective in a 20-stage campaign featuring challenging waves, multi-phase boss battles, and smooth 60 FPS gameplay on HTML5 Canvas.

## How to Play
- **Objective**: Destroy all aliens before they reach the bottom defense line
- **Waves**: 20 stages total — stages 1-4, 6-9, 11-14, 16-19 contain grid-based alien waves; stages 5, 10, 15, and 20 are boss battles
- **Controls**:
  - **Move**: Left/Right arrow keys, D-pad, or swipe on mobile
  - **Shoot**: Spacebar, left mouse click, or tap screen
  - **Pause**: Press Escape or P
- **Power-Ups**: Collect shield hexagons for temporary invincibility (2-hit durability)

## Gameplay Loop
1. Navigate through 20 progressive stages
2. Defeat waves of invaders using your Voidbreaker's single vertical projectile
3. Reach the final boss (The Hollow Mother, Stage 20) to win
4. Lose if all lives deplete or enemies cross the defense line

## Boss Battles
- **The Sentinel** (Stage 5) – Bullet spread + drone summons
- **The Weaver** (Stage 10) – Laser sweeps + homing minions
- **The Hive** (Stage 15) – Grids of weak invaders + charge attack
- **The Hollow Mother** (Stage 20) – 3-phase fight combining all previous attacks plus bullet hell patterns

## Visuals & Sound
- Retro vector neon aesthetic (black background, glowing entities)
- Synthwave soundtrack with stage-specific boss themes
- Particle explosions, screen shake on hits, and pulsing HUD

## Technical Specs
- **Engine**: Pure HTML5 Canvas 2D (no external libraries)
- **Resolution**: 1280×720, responsive scaling
- **Performance**: Object pooling for projectiles/particles, target 60 FPS
- **Platforms**: Keyboard, mouse, and touch support
