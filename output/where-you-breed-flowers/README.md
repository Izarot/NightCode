## How to Play

**1. Plant Seeds**
Click an empty plot to open the Seed Catalog and plant your first flowers. Watch them grow in real-time!

**2. Breed for Glory**
Drag pollen from a mature flower to another to cross their genes. Each offspring inherits random alleles from both parents—plus a tiny chance of mutation.

**3. Match the Patron**
Fairies visit your garden with cryptic requests like *"A blue flower with 6 pointed petals and a magenta center."* Breed flowers that match their description to earn rewards and unlock new biomes.

**4. Crack the Code**
Press `[Tab]` to inspect genomes and predict offspring using Punnett squares. Dominant traits hide recessive alleles—so observe carefully!

**5. Build Your Legacy**
Complete the Codex, discover legendary blooms, and eventually unlock the mythical **Genesis Bloom**—a flower that requires 50+ perfect ancestors to achieve.
