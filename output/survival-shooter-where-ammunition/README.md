### The Loop
1. **Hunt** — Defeat robotic enemies (Swarmers, Shooters, Heavy Units).
2. **Harvest** — Each kill drops **Mineral Cells**. Heavy/Elite kills yield **3 cells**.
3. **Convert** — Cells instantly refill **10% of your magazine**. Empty magazines slowly regen **1% per second**.
4. **Survive** — Waves escalate: enemies spawn faster and move faster. Don't camp — hunt!

### Weapons
- **Pistol** — Infinite ammo, low damage. Thin crosshair.
- **SMG** — Fast fire rate, low damage. Fast dot crosshair.
- **Shotgun** — Slow fire rate, high close-range damage. Spread crosshair.

### Tactics
- Crouch/Prone reduces max speed by **40%** and enlarges your hitbox by **20%** — great against ranged enemies.
- Use the **flashlight** (dynamic radial gradient around you) to spot threats in the dark.
- Watch the **circular ammo gauge** in the bottom-right — it pulses red when empty.
