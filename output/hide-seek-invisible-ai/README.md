# 👻 Phantom Hide

A neon-noir stealth arcade game. You are the Hider. The Seeker is invisible. Survive.

## How to Play
- **Move:** WASD or Arrow Keys  
- **Sprint:** Hold Shift (stamina limited!)  
- **Echo Pulse:** Spacebar — sends a wave that briefly reveals the Seeker, but also leaks your position  
- **Hide:** Enter lockers or crates, then tap the prompted key to stay quiet  
- **Win:** Stay alive for 3:00 without the Seeker touching you  

## Tips
- Watch the Detection Meter: blue is safe, red is danger.  
- Pulses cut both ways — use them wisely.  
- Silence is survival. Don't sprint unless you have to.
