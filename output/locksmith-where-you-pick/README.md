# 🔐 LOCKSMITH: The Art of the Silent Pick

*Welcome to the workshop. The lock won't open itself.*

---

## 🎮 What Is This?

A meditative 2D lockpicking simulation built entirely in HTML5 Canvas. No keys. No shortcuts. Just you, your pick, and the satisfying *click* of a pin finding its shear line.

Feel out each pin. Hold your tension. Open the lock.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

**Goal:** Set every pin at the shear line, then rotate the cylinder. Simple. Except it's not.

### Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| `WASD` / `Arrow Keys` | Move the pick (left/right, up/down) |
| `Mouse Wheel` | Adjust tension |
| `Space` (hold) | Apply tension |
| `Shift` (hold) | Precision mode — slow it down for fine work |
| `Q` / `E` | Hop between adjacent pins |
| `R` | Restart current lock |
| `Esc` | Pause |

### The Golden Rules

1. 🪶 **Light touch first** — find each pin's "click point" with minimal tension
2. ⚖️ **Find the sweet spot** — too light and pins won't set, too heavy and they reset
3. 🔁 **Set them back-to-front** — keep your progress as you work through the lock
4. 🎯 **One pin at a time** — rush nothing
