# Rotation Defense

Spin, slow, and shatter! **Rotation Defense** is a neon 2D tower defense where every enemy is a rotating geometric shape — and its rotation is its weakness.

Hit a flat edge for **150% damage**, dodge a vertex hit for only **50%**, and time your towers to exploit every vulnerability window.

## How to Play

**Move:** WASD / Arrow Keys to pilot the Architect Drone. Mouse to aim, E or Click to interact.

**Build:** Press 1-5 or click a tower card, move to a tile, and click to place.
- **Dart Spire (50g)** - Fast single-target shots
- **Frost Prism (75g)** - Slows movement and rotation
- **Arc Coil (100g)** - Chain lightning
- **Cannon Mortar (125g)** - Splash damage that ignores vertices
- **Chrono Turret (200g)** - Reverses rotation and widens edge windows

**Fight:** Towers auto-target (First / Last / Strongest / Closest). Upgrade to Level 3 for +40% damage per level. Collect energy orbs with your drone for bonus gold.

**Survive:** Start with 200 Gold and 20 Lives. Survive Wave 20 to win, then keep going in endless mode. Don't let shapes reach the Core!

**Controls:** Space = Pause, F = Speed Toggle, U = Upgrade, Delete = Sell, Esc = Cancel
