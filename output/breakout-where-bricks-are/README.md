# 🦠 CELL BREAKER

> *A biological breakout experience — fight the multiplying infection, one cell at a time.*

## 🎮 How to Play

You're piloting a surgical tool through a microscopic battlefield. **Paddle at the bottom, ball in play, cells multiplying above.** Stop the infection before it reaches the danger zone!

### Controls
- **🖱️ Mouse** — Move the paddle left and right
- **⬅️ ➡️ Arrow Keys** — Alternative paddle control
- **🖱️ Click** — Launch the ball (when docked)
- **P / Esc** — Pause / unpause
- **R** — Restart after game over

### Rules of Engagement
1. 🔬 **Bounce the ball** off your paddle to hit the cells above
2. 💥 **Pop cells** — each hit deals 35 damage, full destruction scores **+10**
3. 🧬 **Watch them multiply** — every 3 seconds, cells have a chance to **divide** into two
4. ⚠️ **Don't let them reach the bottom** — if any cell crosses the danger zone, it's **CONTAMENT FAILURE**
5. 💔 **Lose the ball** off the bottom edge = lose a life (you start with **3**)
6. 🏆 Clear all cells across **5 levels** to win

### Pro Tips
- 🎯 Hit the ball with the **edges** of your paddle for sharp angled shots
- ⚡ The ball **speeds up** each level — stay sharp!
- 🔢 Cap is **150 cells** on screen — divisions halt beyond that
- 🎨 Cells change **color** as they age: blue → mature blue → warning orange (about to divide!)
