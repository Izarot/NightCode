# 🚀 Asteroid Grav: Gravity Mayhem!  

**Dodge, shoot, and bend space itself in this mind-bending twist on the classic Asteroids arcade game!**  

---

## 🌌 Core Concept  
Pilot your ship through a field of floating asteroids while wielding **Gravitational Wells**—black-hole-like bubbles created by your bullets. Pull enemies into your orbit, trap them in your own gravity, or fling yourself through the cosmos with style!  

---

## 🎮 How to Play  

### 🕹️ Controls  
- **Arrow Keys / WASD**: Thrust, rotate, and dodge.  
- **Spacebar / Tap**: Fire bullets (they leave behind a gravitational well!).  
- **Hold Shift**: Enter **Gravity-Assist Mode** to boost your wells’ strength.  

### 🧠 Gameplay Tips  
- **Master the Wells**: Use them to slingshot your ship or drag asteroids into your crosshairs.  
- **Stack ’Em Up**: Overlapping wells create super-powered gravity zones!  
- **Survive & Score**: Destroy asteroids for points, collect power-ups, and avoid collisions!
