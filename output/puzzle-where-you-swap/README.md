# 🎮 **Cosmic Clicker: Nebula Edition**

> *One small click for a player, one giant leap for your high score.*

---

## 🚀 **How to Play**

1.  **Launch** the game (`python main.py` or open `index.html`).
2.  **Click** the glowing **Nebula Core** in the center of the screen.
3.  **Collect** *Stardust* (points) with every click.
4.  **Spend** Stardust on the **Upgrade Panel** (right side) to buy:
    *   🌑 **Auto-Orbiters** (idle income)
    *   💥 **Supernova Multipliers** (click power)
    *   🕳️ **Black Hole Prestige** (reset for cosmic bonuses)
5.  **Survive** the **Asteroid Events** (press `SPACE` to shield)!
6.  **Reach** **1 Quadrillion Stardust** to unlock the *Secret Ending*.

## ⌨️ **Controls**

| Key | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
