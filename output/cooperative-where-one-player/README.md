# Ash & Vapor 🔥💧

**Two elements. One very awkward friendship.**

## How to Play
You and a friend are elemental powerhouses who should probably learn to cooperate.

- **Water** cools, flows, extinguishes, and makes everything soggy.
- **Fire** heats, burns, melts, and makes everything crispy.
- Combine your powers to solve puzzles, create steam lifts, thaw ice, and survive.

**Controls:**
- Player 1 — Water: `WASD` to move, `Space` to dash/interact
- Player 2 — Fire: Arrow keys to move, `Enter` to dash/interact
- Co-op Synergy: Both players must sync up for big elemental moments

## Why You’ll Love It
It’s basically *Ghostbusters* meets *Mario*, but with more thermal dynamics.
