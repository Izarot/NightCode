# 🏃 Ground Disappears

An endless runner where the ground beneath you literally vanishes. Jump, dodge, and collect your way to the top score before the void catches up!

## 🎮 How to Play

- **Move**: Arrow keys ← → (or swipe on mobile)
- **Jump**: Spacebar (or tap) — double-tap mid-air for a **Boost Jump**!
- **Goal**: Survive the disappearing ground, dodge cacti & flying enemies, and collect coins for combos.
- **Watch the gaps**: Every 1500px the ground splits open — time your jumps or fall to your doom!
- **Difficulty ramps**: Speed increases, gaps grow, and enemies come faster as you score.

## ✨ Features

- **Shield & Double-Jump power-ups** to spice up the run
- **Parallax backgrounds** with mountains, hills & clouds
- **Combo multiplier** (up to ×3) for consecutive dodges
- **High scores** saved locally — can you beat your best?
