## 🎮 How to Play

**Welcome aboard, castaway!** You're stranded on a tiny wooden raft in the middle of a vast, hungry ocean. Your mission? **Don't die.** Sounds simple, right? *Spoiler: it isn't.*

### 🕹️ Controls
- **WASD / D-Pad** → Move your survivor
- **Space** → Jump off (or back onto) the raft
- **Mouse / Right Trigger** → Use equipped tool
- **Tab / Q** → Open crafting & inventory menu
- **Shift** → Sprint (burns stamina!)

### 🎯 Your Goals
1. **Stay Alive** — Watch your four vital gauges: 💙 Thirst, 💛 Hunger, 💚 Stamina, ❤️ Health
2. **Hook Everything** — Grab floating debris with your hook before it drifts away forever
3. **Catch Dinner** — Fish with a rod or spear your dinner (cooked > raw, trust us)
4. **Expand Your Empire** — Snap planks, barrels, and floors onto your raft to build a floating fortress
5. **Explore** — Sail toward mysterious islands to find rare clay, ore, and answers

### ☠️ Things That Want To Kill You
- 🦈 **Sharks** — They smell fear. And blood. And movement in the water.
- ⛈️ **Storms** — Bigger waves, lightning strikes, and your raft drifting to who-knows-where.
- 🌊 **The Ocean Itself** — Swim too long and you'll drown. Drink seawater and you'll poison yourself. The ocean doesn't care.

### 💡 Pro Tips From Previous Castaways (R.I.P.)
- 🔥 Always cook your fish unless you *enjoy* explosive diarrhea.
- ⛵ Build a sail early — rowing a giant raft by hand is a one-way ticket to Stamina Town.
- 🌧️ Storms are scary, but they're also free showers. Put a bucket out.
- 🦈 If a fin appears, **get back on the raft**. We mean it.
