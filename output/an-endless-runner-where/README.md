# DOMINO DASH

**Don't stop. Don't look back. The floor is literally falling.**

DOMINO DASH is a 2D endless runner where the ground isn't ground at all — it's a giant chain of upright dominoes that tip, crash, and collapse right behind you. Sprint across the toppling path, chain the falls, and see how far you can get before you take a tumble.

## How to Play

- **Jump:** `Space` / `W` / `↑` or Tap / Click anywhere
- **Hold** for a higher jump, release early for a short hop
- **Swipe down** (touch) for a fast fall
- **Pause:** `P` / `Esc`
- **Restart:** `R` on Game Over

You auto-run forward — your job is to stay on top. Land on dominoes to knock them over, leap over missing gaps, and don't get caught by a standing domino or a fallen path.

Grab power-ups to survive the chaos:
- **Magnet Boots** — pull you toward the nearest domino
- **Feather** — floaty, low-gravity jumps
- **Shield** — survive one fatal fall
- **Double Jump** — one extra jump in mid-air

Score = distance + dominoes toppled. Chain topples fast for combos, skim falling tips for near-miss bonuses, and chase your high score as the speed keeps climbing.

## Tips

- You can't stand still — dominoes tip under your weight in under a second. Keep moving!
- Broken dominoes only give you 0.3s of footing. Jump again, quick!
- Tall dominoes need a full-height jump. Time it right.
