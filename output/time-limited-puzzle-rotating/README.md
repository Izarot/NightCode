# 🌈 *Chrono Prism*  
**A mind-bending puzzle-platformer where time runs out, colors clash, and gravity bends to your will!**  

---

## 🕹️ How to Play  
1. **Move & Dash**:  
   - Use **← →** to move, **SPACE** to jump, and **SHIFT** to dash (50px, 2s cooldown).  
   - Master momentum: Your movement *persists* as the world rotates beneath you!  

2. **Embrace the Rotation**:  
   - The stage spins **clockwise** every 10 seconds (speeding up over time!).  
   - Time your jumps/dashes to land on platforms as they whirl past.  

3. **Color Chaos**:  
   - **Red Zones**: Speed boost + health drain. Risk it all!  
   - **Blue Zones**: Slow motion + invincibility. Hide here!  
   - **Yellow Zones**: Reverse rotation. Flip the script!  
   - **Green Zones**: Slow time. Breathe.  

4. **Race the Clock**:  
   - 60 seconds per level. Collect **Chrono Shards** (⏱️) to buy extra time!  
   - Solve puzzles by aligning lasers, activating switches, and redirecting mirrors.  

---

## ⚠️ Warning: Time’s Up!  
- **Win**: Reach the exit portal before the timer hits zero.  
- **Lose**: Timer expires, health hits zero, or you plummet into the void.
