# Angle‑Aimed 2D Shooter  

**Top‑down shooter where your aim locks to the mouse – move, rotate, and blast!**  

## How to Play  
- **Aim:** Move the mouse; your ship always points at the cursor.  
- **Thrust:** `W`/`↑` – forward, `S`/`↓` – backward (relative to aim).  
- **Rotate:** `A`/`←` – left, `D`/`→` – right (fine‑tune your facing).  
- **Fire:** Left click or `Space` – shoot bullets (watch the cooldown!).  
- **Survive:** Dodge enemies, collect score, and clear increasingly tough waves.  
- **Wrap‑around:** Fly off one edge and appear on the opposite side (or bounce off walls – pick your style).
