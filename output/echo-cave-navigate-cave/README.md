# Echo Cave  

A pulse‑pounding, 2D acoustic platformer where you *listen* your way through pitch‑black caves.  

## How to Play  
- **Move** with arrow keys or WASD. Your momentum carries you forward—don’t expect instant stops!  
- **Emit an echo** automatically every 0.4 s while moving (or hold **Space** to force a burst).  
- **Listen** to the returning “pings” and “thuds” to map walls, crates, and hazards.  
- **Adjust Focus** using the vertical slider on the left: higher focus = clearer echoes, lower noise.  
- **Navigate** the procedurally generated cave, avoiding spikes and collecting glowing orbs.  

> *Tip:* Faster movement = louder echoes, giving you richer spatial cues.
