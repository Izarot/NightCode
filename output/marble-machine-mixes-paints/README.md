# Marble Mixer

## How to Play

1. **Study the Target** — A swirling color appears at the top. Your job is to recreate it.
2. **Build Your Machine** — Drag ramps, funnels, splitters, and mixers onto the pegboard grid. Rotate parts with `R` or scroll.
3. **Assign Marble Colors** — Click a color in the selector, then click RUN. Marbles drop one by one.
4. **Watch the Mix** — Marbles bounce, collide, and blend colors as they tumble through your machine.
5. **Check the Vat** — At the bottom, the final swirl is compared to your target. Closer = higher score!
6. **Earn Stars** — 1 star for 70% match, 2 for 85%, 3 for 95%. Stars unlock new parts.

**Pro Tip:** Use Mixing Chambers for chaotic blending, Paint Droppers for precise tinting, and Color Filters to sort your flow!
