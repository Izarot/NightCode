# Resonance Clash

A neon‑lit, sound‑driven duel where every pulse, beam, and echo decides the victor.

## How to Play
- **Movement**: Arrow keys or WASD (8‑directional). Hold **Shift** to sprint.  
- **Jump/Dash**: Press **Space** for a short hop (cooldown 1.5 s).  
- **Pulse‑Step**: Press **E** for a quick forward dash leaving an echo trail.  
- **Attacks**:  
  - **Left‑Click** – fire a circular Pulse (80 px radius, 15 HP).  
  - **Shift + Left‑Click** – charge a Beam (cone, up to 300 px, damage scales).  
  - **Right‑Click** – raise an Echo Shield (120 px radius, 30 % damage reduction, reflects projectiles).  
  - **Q** – activate Resonance Burst when health < 30 % (200 px radius, 30 HP AoE, stun).  
  - **F** – launch an Echo‑Leap projectile that creates a temporary platform.  
- **Audio**: Each attack uses a distinct frequency; higher pitch = hotter color.

## Objective
Reduce your opponent’s health to 0. In Timed Mode, the higher‑health player after 90 s wins. In Resonance Capture, secure 3 sound‑zones to claim victory.
