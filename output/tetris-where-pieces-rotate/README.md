# AutoRotate Tetris

Welcome to the future of falling blocks! In **AutoRotate Tetris**, the board is alive—pieces don't just fall, they spin on their own. Can you outsmart the clock?

## 🎮 How to Play
*   **Move:** ← → (Arrow keys)
*   **Soft Drop:** ↓ (Arrow key)
*   **Hard Drop:** ↑ / Spacebar
*   **Pause:** P

### The Twist
Every 4 seconds, all active pieces on the board execute a 90° clockwise rotation! You have less than 200ms to reposition your piece before it locks. Move fast, think smart, and clear lines before the neon chaos takes over.

## 🕹️ Game Mechanics
*   **Cyberpunk Neon Aesthetic:** Dark void background with glowing tetrominoes.
*   **Ghost Piece:** Dim gray outline shows where your blocks will land.
*   **Scoring & Levels:** Clear lines to score points. Every 10 lines increases your level, speeding up the auto-rotation interval. Miss a rotation, and it's game over!
