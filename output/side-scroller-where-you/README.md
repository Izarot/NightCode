# 🌟 Stellar Runner

**An endless side-scroller where stars grant you the power to defy danger!**

---

## 🎮 How to Play

| Action | Keys |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Move Left / Right** | `←` `→` or `A` `D` |
| **Jump** | `Space` or `↑` / `W` |
| **Restart (Game Over)** | Click **Restart** Button |

### The Loop
1.  **Run** automatically through an infinite starfield.
2.  **Collect Stars** (⭐) to gain **5 Seconds of Invincibility** (🛡️).
3.  **Survive Hazards:** Pits, Lasers, and Spikes.
    *   *Normal:* Hazards = **Game Over**.
    *   *Invincible:* Phase right through them!
4.  **Chase the High Score.** Every star = 10pts. Speed bonus = 1pt/sec.

---

## ✨ Features
*   **Smooth Physics:** Velocity-based movement with jump cooldowns.
*   **Dynamic Difficulty:** Star spawn rate increases after 30s.
*   **Visual Flair:** Glowing stars, cyan invincibility shaders, particle bursts.
*   **Audio Reactive:** Distinct SFX for jumps, collection, shields, and defeat.
*   **Persistent High Scores:** Top 5 saved to LocalStorage.
