# 🌠 STELLAR DRIFT
**2D Physics-Based Orbital Action**

## How to Play
- **WASD / Arrows** — Thrust and steer your comet
- **Collect** dust & asteroids to increase mass (and gravity!)
- **Watch** the trajectory line — white = safe, red = 🔥
- **Slingshot** around planets for gravity assists
- **Avoid** solar flares and oversized debris

## Features
- Real Newtonian gravity (F = G...)
- Dynamic comet tail & parallax starfield
- Mass affects steering & orbital pull
- Ice fuel system (sublimate near the star!)
- Screen shake, vignette, neon glow effects
