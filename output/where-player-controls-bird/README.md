# Egg Drop Raiders

## How to Play
- **Fly** your bird using **WASD** (or arrow keys) and **adjust altitude** with **Space** (up) and **Ctrl** (down).  
- **Drop eggs** by pressing **Space** while in the air—choose egg types via the wheel to match the target.  
- **Avoid** flak cannons, SAM missiles, and environmental hazards while timing your drops for chain‑reaction bonuses.  
- **Score** points with consecutive hits, perfect timing, and destroying clusters of targets.  
- **Upgrade** your bird and eggs in the shop between missions to stay ahead of the challenges.

## Controls
- **Movement:** WASD / Arrows  
- **Altitude:** Space (boost) / Ctrl (dive)  
- **Drop Egg:** Space (while airborne)  
- **Target Lock:** Left‑click or tap a target for homing eggs
