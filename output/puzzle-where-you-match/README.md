# Chromatic Velocity

A neon‑blasted, rhythm‑tuned 2D physics puzzle where you steer a glowing particle through a torrent of color‑coded obstacles. Match your particle’s hue to the obstacle’s hue at the instant of impact to score—miss, and the screen gets a reality‑check!

## How to Play
- **Controls**  
  - **Desktop:** Use **WASD** or **arrow keys** for 8‑way movement. Press **Space** (or tap) to cycle your particle’s color.  
  - **Mobile:** Drag the virtual joystick to move; tap the right side to change color.  

- **Objective**  
  - Your particle has a **color state** (`#FF5555`, `#55FF55`, `#5555FF`, `#FFFF55`).  
  - Collide with an obstacle **only when the colors match**. A perfect match adds points and spawns a flashy particle burst.  

- **Scoring**  
  - **Combo multiplier** grows with consecutive hits.  
  - **Mismatch** triggers a brief screen shake and a penalty to your score (and a life loss).  

- **Tips**  
  - Keep an eye on the **drag coefficient**—it naturally slows you down, so small inputs give precise control.  
  - Use the **color cooldown** (200 ms) wisely; rapid spamming won’t help and just wastes time.
