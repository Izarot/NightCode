# DUALITY
A precision puzzle-platformer where **one input controls two avatars** across split-screen realities.

## How to Play
- **Move**: `A/D` or `←→` (moves both avatars horizontally)
- **Jump**: `W`, `Space`, or `↑` (triggers jump for both, respecting individual physics)
- **Dash/Action**: `Shift` or `Ctrl` (Prime: Blink | Echo: Burst)
- **Interact**: `E` (context-sensitive: activate buttons, disable lasers, etc.)
- **Goal**: Reach the Dual Exit Zone (one pad per screen) simultaneously for 0.5s.

Watch the **Sync Meter** (gold bar on divider) – high sync grants speed/resonance gates, low sync triggers desync bridges and screen effects.
