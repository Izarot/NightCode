# 🪨 Sisyphus Roll

*A meditative-triumphant uphill boulder platformer where momentum is everything.*

---

## How to Play

**Roll your boulder to the summit!** Control a massive stone as it struggles and climbs through treacherous mountain terrain. Master the physics of momentum—build it up, brake strategically, and never stop believing.

### Controls

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| `←` `→` or `A` `D` | Steer the boulder left/right |
| `Space` | Jump (when grounded) |
| `Shift` | Brake / hold position |
| `R` | Restart from checkpoint |

### Gameplay Tips

- **Build momentum** on downhills to conquer steep ascents
- **Brake wisely** on dangerous descents
- **Collect sun shards** (3 per level) for completion
- Watch the momentum bar above your stone—**green means go!**
