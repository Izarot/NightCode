# COIL: KINETIC ASCENT

Boing your way up an industrial neon tower as Coil, a sentient spring with places to be!

## How to Play

Hold **Space / S / Down Arrow** to compress, release to launch! The longer you charge, the higher you fly. Hold **A / D** or **Left / Right Arrows** while launching to steer sideways.

Reach the **Anchor Point** at the top to beat the level. Grab hidden **Gears** 🔧 for cosmetic skins, dodge spikes and crushers, and ride moving platforms — just don't get squashed!

**Controls:**
- **A / D** — Move Left / Right
- **Space** — Hold to Compress, Release to Launch
- **R** — Restart Level
- **Esc** — Pause
