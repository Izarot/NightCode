# Sky Typer

Alien word-ships are descending! Blast them with your keyboard before they breach the Death Line.

## How to Play

- **Type the falling words** to destroy them. The cursor will snap to the matching word and highlight letters as you type.
- **Score points** for each word destroyed: 10 points × word length.
- **Build combos** by destroying words without missing. Missing a key resets your combo to 1x.
- **Avoid strikes!** Wrong keys flash a red X, deduct 5 seconds, and break your combo. Three strikes = game over.
- **Survive Wave Breaches** every 30 seconds — words fall faster, spawn more often, and get longer.

Good luck, pilot. The synthwave sky is counting on your WPM!
