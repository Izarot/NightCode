# 🎮 Pixel Quest

## How to Play
- **Move** your hero with the arrow keys (or WASD).  
- **Jump** to avoid obstacles and reach higher platforms.  
- **Collect** coins to boost your score.  
- **Defeat** enemies by landing a well‑timed attack.  
- Reach the flag at the end of each level to advance.  

## Controls
| Action | Key |
|--------|-----|
| Move Left | ← or A |
| Move Right | → or D |
| Jump | ↑ or W |
| Attack | Space |

## Tips
- Keep an eye on the health bar; avoid prolonged contact with hazards.  
- Coins can be spent on power‑ups at the shop (if unlocked).  
- Experiment with different jump heights for precise platforming.
