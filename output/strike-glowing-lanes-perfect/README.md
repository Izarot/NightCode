# Lumina Beats 🎵

A neon-soaked rhythm game where timing is everything. Strike the glowing lanes to the beat, keep your combo alive, and don’t let your energy bar flatline.

## How to Play

1. Start a track and watch the notes slide toward the precision zone.
2. Press the matching key the instant a note lines up with its lane.
3. Nail the timing for **Perfect** or **Good** hits to fill your energy and multiply your combo.
4. Miss too many notes and your energy drains—hit zero and it’s game over.
5. Survive the song to unlock new tracks, lane skins, and visual effects.

## Controls

- **Q / W / E / R** — Strike lanes 1 through 4
- **Arrow Keys** — Alternative keyboard input
- **Touch / Click** — Tap the glowing lanes on any device

- HTML5 Canvas

- Web Audio API
