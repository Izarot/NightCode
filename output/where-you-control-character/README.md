# Maze Runner: Directional Challenge

## Game Overview
Dive into an endless 2D maze where you must navigate your circular avatar through shifting obstacles. Move smoothly in one direction until you choose to turn—each decision shapes your journey. Master timing, plan routes, and survive the ever-changing labyrinth!

## How to Play
- **Control**: Use arrow keys or WASD to set your direction (up, down, left, right).
- **Move**: Your character glides at a steady pace in the chosen direction.
- **Survive**: Avoid walls and randomly placed obstacles. Colliding ends the game.
- **Score**: Earn points for distance traveled plus bonuses for quick direction changes.
- **Upgrade**: Collect power-ups like speed boosts and invincibility shields for extra challenge.

## Game Features
- Procedurally generated infinite maze with dynamic difficulty
- Trail visual effect following your path
- Particle bursts when changing direction
- Responsive controls with input buffering
- Multiple game states: start screen, pause, and game over

## Controls
| Action | Keys |
|--------|------|
| Move / Change Direction | ← → ↑ ↓ (Arrow keys) or W A S D |

## Game States
- **Start Screen** – Learn the controls and begin your adventure
- **Pause Menu** – Toggle pause and view stats
- **Game Over** – See your final score and restart
