# 🟢 Goo Bridge Builder

Squish, stretch, and snap your way across wobbly gaps! Build bouncy bridges, outsmart hazards, and guide your goo to the glowing goal.

## How to Play

- **Place:** Click or tap empty space to add goo balls.
- **Connect:** Drag between balls to build stretchy joints.
- **Move:** Select **Move**, then drag your structure into position.
- **Delete:** Right-click a ball or connection to remove it.
- **Navigate:** Scroll to zoom; use two fingers to pan on touchscreens.
- **Win:** Reach the goal without exceeding your ball limit or collapsing your bridge.

## Pro Tips

- Start with at least **3 balls**.
- Build **triangles** for extra strength.
- Use **arches** to spread weight.
- Watch the stability meter—green is safe, red is *very* splattery.
