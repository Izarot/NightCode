# ☀️ Solar Evaporation

*A sunbeam descends. Puddles tremble.*

---

## How to Play

**Move your mouse** to aim the blazing sunbeam across the ground.

**Evaporate puddles** before they overwhelm the field! The more still you hold your aim, the **hotter** your beam becomes.

### Scoring
| Puddle Size | Points |
|-------------|--------|
| Small | 100 |
| Medium | 250 |
| Large | 500 |
| Golden | 3x bonus! |

**Combo chains** multiply your score — evaporate 3+ puddles in 2 seconds for **2x**, 5+ for **3x**!

### Don't Let the Ground Flood!
A meter shows puddle coverage. If it hits **60%**... ☔ Game Over.

---

## Special Puddles
- 🟡 **Golden** — Sparkles and scores triple
- 🔵 **Frozen** — Takes extra heat to evaporate
- 🟤 **Mud** — Spreads when hit, stubborn!
