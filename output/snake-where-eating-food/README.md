# WallSnake 🐍🧱

*Eat to build walls. Build walls to protect what matters.*

---

## How to Play

**Move** your snake with **WASD** or **Arrow keys**. Eat food to grow and build protective walls around it — walls block enemies from reaching your food and shield your head from collisions!

### The Basics
- **Eat apples** (green) → +1 score, +1 body segment
- **Eat golden apples** (gold) → +3 score, +2 segments, spawns extra wall
- **Eat bombs** (red) → Damaging wall that blocks ALL snakes for 3 seconds!

### The Strategy
- Walls protect your food from enemies and buffer head-to-head collisions
- Keep walls clustered near your food, or strip them to move faster? **That's the game.**
- Each wave gets harder — more enemies, faster movement, shorter food lifetimes
- Manage your HP (6 hits) and survive as long as possible

## Game Modes

🟢 **Apple** — Common food, +1 score, +1 body segment

🟡 **Golden Apple** — Rare food, +3 score, +2 segments, spawns an extra wall

🔴 **Bomb Food** — Special food. Creates a damaging wall that blocks all snakes for 3 seconds and deals 1 damage on contact. Resets on contact.
