# SPLITSHOT

One bullet in. A whole problem out.

**SPLITSHOT** is a fast-paced 2D arena shooter where every shot you fire splits mid-flight into a fan of sub-projectiles. Master your positioning, time your splits, and chain the chaos to survive escalating waves.

## How to Play

- **Move:** WASD / Arrow Keys
- **Aim:** Mouse
- **Fire Carrier:** Hold Left Mouse
- **Dash:** Shift / Right Mouse — brief invulnerability, use it!
- **Nova Split:** Space / E when your Split Meter is full — instantly splits every Carrier on screen
- **Pause:** ESC / P
- **Mute:** M

Your Carriers split after 280px, on impact, or on hitting a wall — into 4 sub-projectiles by default. Grab upgrades like SPLIT+1, WIDE SPLIT, and FRACTURE CORE to turn the arena into a fireworks show. Watch out for Chasers, Splitters, Stingers, Tanks, and the Boss every 5 waves.

Survive. Score big. Don't get hit.
