# SPLITFIRE

**One bullet in. A neon storm out.**

SPLITFIRE is a 2D arena shooter where every shot you fire splits into more shots. Survive 10 escalating waves, manage the chaos, and take down The Divider.

## How to Play

**Move:** WASD / Arrow Keys  
**Aim:** Mouse  
**Fire:** Hold Left Mouse Button  
**Dash:** Space / Right Mouse Button — grants brief invulnerability  
**Pause:** P / Esc  
**Mute:** M

Your primary bullets split on impact, on a timer, or when they kill — spawning faster, smaller projectiles at an angle. Chain those splits with upgrades like Bifurcation+, Tertiary Split, Homing Shards, and Chain Reaction.

Collect green Split Cores from enemies to unlock upgrades between waves. Choose 1 of 3, then dive back in.

**Win:** Defeat the Wave 10 Boss  
**Lose:** Your HP hits 0

Watch your screen. Watch your framerate. Every split is power — and a little bit of danger.

Good luck, pilot.
