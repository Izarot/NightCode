# Limited FOV Shooter 🔦

A top-down arena survival shooter where you fight through darkness with only a narrow flashlight cone to see enemies. Survive escalating waves, manage your stamina, and keep your head on a swivel.

## How to Play

1. **Start** — Click "Click to Start" on the main menu.
2. **Move** — WASD or Arrow Keys. Press Shift to sprint (drains stamina).
3. **Aim & Shoot** — Move your mouse to aim (the cyan triangle follows). Hold Left Mouse Button to fire.
4. **Reload** — Press R when ammo runs out.
5. **Dash** — Press Space or Right-Click for a burst dash (0.15s, brief invulnerability).
6. **Survive** — Waves get harder. Collect health/ammo drops that appear briefly in your light.
7. **Mute** — Press M to toggle audio.
8. **Pause** — Press Esc or P.
