# Stardust Clicker: Harvest the Cosmos! 🌌✨  

## How to Play  
Dive into a mesmerizing clicker adventure where you control a **harvest node** to collect floating stardust particles!  

- **Move Your Avatar**:  
  - Drag with the mouse, use arrow keys, or touch-drag to navigate your 12px circular harvester.  
  - Your node accelerates smoothly (max speed: 300px/s) and slides along canvas edges.  

- **Collect Stardust**:  
  - Click/tap directly on glowing particles (6px radius) to add them to your counter.  
  - Each particle drifts randomly; some vanish after 5 seconds if uncollected!  

- **Upgrade & Evolve**:  
  - Spend stardust to boost spawn rates, collection radius, or particle value.  
  - Unlock milestones every 100 stardust for spectacular visual bursts!  

## Features  
- **Dynamic Particle System**: Random hues, drift, and wind-affected movement.  
- **Responsive UI**: Stardust counter, upgrade buttons, and milestone indicators.  
- **Visual Spectacle**: Pulsating particles, glowing trails, and cosmic background effects.
