# ASTRO TRAIL

## How to Play
- **Steer** your rugged asteroid‑salvaging ship with **WASD** or the arrow keys.  
- **Thrust** (↑/W) pushes you forward, leaving a hot debris trail you can later collect.  
- **Reverse** (↓/S) lets you brake or back‑up, but watch your max reverse speed!  
- **Rotate** (←/A, →/D) to point your ship where you need to go—rotation feels heavy and responsive.  
- **Collect** the glowing debris by flying into its 18‑pixel radius; each scoop adds score, cargo, and fuels upgrades.  
- **Survive** asteroid waves: dodge or smash them, then watch them split into smaller rocks.  
- **Upgrade** your ship with cargo: boost thrust, expand collection radius, add shields, or fine‑tune weapons.  
- **Score** as high as you can in endless mode, or hit the salvage target in timed challenges.

> *Tip:* Keep your combo multiplier alive—collect debris within 2 seconds for up to an 8× score boost!
