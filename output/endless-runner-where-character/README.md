# Luminous Dash

## How to Play
Run, jump, and collect glowing energy nodes in this neon-soaked endless runner! Your character leaves a radiant trail as you dash through the cyberpunk cityscape. Absorb power-up nodes to unlock abilities like Speed Boost, Magnet Pull, and Phase Dash. Navigate gaps, avoid spikes, and chain combos to rack up massive scores!

**Controls:**
- **Arrow Keys/WASD**: Move left and right
- **Space**: Jump
- **Mobile**: Virtual joystick and tap-to-jump

Collect nodes before they fade, maintain your energy trail for combo bonuses, and watch your score soar as you race toward the horizon!
