# ⚙️ GEAR GATE ⚙️

*A steampunk puzzle adventure where brass, cogs, and clever clicks collide!*

---

## 🎮 How to Play

Welcome, tinkerer! The ancient gate stands before you, sealed shut by a mess of misaligned gears. Your mission? **Rotate the golden master gear** to set the entire mechanism in motion and unlock the way forward.

### 🕹️ Controls

| Action | What To Do |
|--------|------------|
| **Select a gear** | Left-click (or tap) on it |
| **Rotate slowly** | Click + drag around the gear's center |
| **Rotate quickly** | Single-click to snap 15° |
| **Deselect** | Right-click or click empty space |

### 🎯 Objective

Every gear has a **target angle** — get *all* gears within 3° of their goal, and the gate rumbles open! Watch the pulsing rings: the brighter they glow, the closer you are.

### 🔗 The Golden Rules

- **Meshing gears spin in OPPOSITE directions** — turn one, its neighbor fights back!
- **Big gears turn slow, small gears turn fast** — it's all about the teeth ratio!
- **Locked gears won't budge** — only the gold master gear takes direct orders.
- **No timer, no pressure** — puzzle at your own pace.

### 🏆 Win the Level

When every gear clicks into alignment:
1. 🔩 Lock pins retract
2. 🚪 The gate swings upward
3. ✨ Steam and sparks fly!
4. 🎉 Hit **NEXT LEVEL** and keep climbing!
