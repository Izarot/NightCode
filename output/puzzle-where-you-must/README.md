# PATHFINDER: INK TRAIL

**Draw. Traverse. Survive.**

---

## How to Play

Guide a glowing orb from its origin point to the goal beacon by drawing a path of ink. The orb follows physics-based rules as it rolls along your drawn rail, accelerating downhill and slowing on inclines. But beware—the path is filled with obstacles!

### Core Mechanics

- **Draw Your Path:** Click and drag to create a cyan trail from the origin orb to the goal beacon. Your ink meter depletes as you draw.
- **Physics Orb:** The orb accelerates with gravity, decelerates with friction, and must stay on your path. If it derails or hits an obstacle, it explodes and resets.
- **Obstacles:** Dodge static blocks, moving platforms, spinning blades, gravity wells, conveyors, and breakable barriers. Each requires timing and strategy!
- **Ink Management:** Use your limited ink wisely. Efficient paths earn star ratings (1-3 stars) based on ink used and time taken.
