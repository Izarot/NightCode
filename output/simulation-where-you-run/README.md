# Pizza Rush – Topping‑Time Tycoon

## How to Play
1. **Grab the dough** – Move your avatar (WASD) to the prep station and click to add dough, sauce, cheese, then drag toppings onto the pizza.  
2. **Cook it** – Slide the pizza into the oven; the timer grows with each topping’s cooking modifier.  
3. **Serve fast** – When the timer ends, deliver the pizza before the customer’s patience runs out for maximum coins.  
4. **Upgrade** – Earn coins and spend them on faster ovens, extra prep space, or snazzy skins.  
5. **End of Day** – After the shift, see your stats, unlock new recipes, and start the next day.

## Controls
- **WASD / Arrow Keys** – Move avatar  
- **Mouse** – Click stations, drag toppings, interact  
- **E** – Confirm interaction when prompted  
- **Space** – Pause / open menu  

## About
A frantic 2‑D pizza‑shop sim where every topping changes the cooking time. Build your tycoon, master the rush, and become the ultimate pizza master!
