# Path-Painter Tower Defense 🎨🏰

Enemies don’t follow the map—they **paint it as they march**. Build your defenses before their chaotic trails become tomorrow’s invasion route!

## How to Play

1. Start with **1,000 coins** and choose a tower from the shop.
2. Drag or tap a **32px grid tile** to place it.
3. Watch enemies draw a glowing trail toward your base.
4. Predict where the path will lead, then place archers, mages, cannons, supports, and walls.
5. Upgrade strong towers or sell them for a **50% refund**.
6. Stop every wave before your **100 HP** reaches zero!

🖱️ Use mouse or keyboard on desktop, and touch controls on mobile. Survive all waves to claim victory!
