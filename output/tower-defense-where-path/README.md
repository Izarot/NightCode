# 🚀 TRAILBLAZE

> *Draw the line. Defend the line. Rewrite the line.*

## ⚡ What Is This?

**TRAILBLAZE** is a tactical tower defense game where the enemies build the road. Watch neon Scouts march across the void, *paving* a glowing highway as they go. Read the map. Place your towers. Then grab the **Path Eraser** and vaporize their road to reroute them straight into your kill-zones.

## 🎮 How To Play

1. **Watch** the Scouts march. They leave a permanent glowing trail called the **Highway**.
2. **Build** towers on the hex-grid cells next to the Highway to intercept them.
3. **Erase** parts of the Highway with your Path Eraser to force Scouts to turn at the next node.
4. **Survive 20 waves.** If 30+ Scouts reach the Core, it's game over.

### Controls

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| `Mouse Move` | Aim / hover |
| `Left Click` | Place tower / fire Eraser |
| `Right Click` | Deselect |
| `1` `2` `3` `4` | Select tower type |
| `E` | Toggle Path Eraser |
| `Space` | Send next wave early (bonus Energy!) |
| `Scroll Wheel` | Zoom |
| `Middle-Click Drag` | Pan the map |

### Tower Types

- 🔷 **Pulse** — Fast cyan orbs, rapid fire
- 🔶 **Mortar** — Arcing neon plasma, AoE splash
- 🔺 **Sniper** — Instant pink laser beams
- 🌀 **Slow** — Frost pulse to freeze them in their tracks

### HUD Cheat Sheet

- **ENERGY** — Currency for towers
- **CORE** — Your health bar (turns red as damage hits)
- **WAVE X / 20** — How far you've survived
- **PATH ERASER CHARGES** — Reroute ammo (regens over time)

## 🏆 Victory

Survive all 20 waves and witness your final masterpiece: a fully illuminated map of every unique Highway you forced into existence. Your enemies drew the first line. You drew the last.
