# Summoner's Arena

A fantasy battler where you don't swing a sword — you **summon creatures** to do the fighting. Survive 10 escalating waves and defeat the Void Dragon boss.

## How to Play

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `WASD` / `Arrows` | Move your Summoner |
| `Shift` / `Space` | Dash (burst + i-frames) |
| `1` / `2` / `3` | Summon Ember Imp / Stone Golem / Storm Wisp |
| `ESC` / `P` | Pause |
| `M` | Mute audio |

### The Loop
1. **Move** to position yourself around the arena
2. **Gather Mana** — it regenerates over time; enemies drop more on death
3. **Summon** a creature into one of 3 slots (press `1`, `2`, or `3`)
4. **Watch them fight** — creatures seek, attack, and defend autonomously
5. **Collect Soul Shards** from kills to upgrade between waves
6. **Survive** each wave, then upgrade and push deeper

### Three Summons
- **Ember Imp** (Fire) — ranged fireball, leaves burning DoT
- **Stone Golem** (Earth) — tanky melee slam with knockback resistance
- **Storm Wisp** (Lightning) — fast chain lightning, teleports every 4s

### Win Condition
Defeat all 10 waves including the **Void Dragon** boss. Lose when your HP hits zero.
