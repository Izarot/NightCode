# Gravity Shift

## A Puzzle Platformer Where Up Becomes Down

**Genre:** Puzzle Platformer | **Engine:** HTML5 Canvas | **Platform:** Web

Navigate interconnected worlds by reversing gravity on special surfaces. Flip floors, turn ladders into stairs, and escape impossible traps—all while solving clever pathfinding puzzles.

## How to Play

- **Move** with arrow keys or WASD to walk and crouch
- **Jump** (spacebar) to cross gaps and reach higher platforms
- **Activate** gravity-reversible objects by pressing **E** while standing on them
- **Avoid** enemies that now fall *upward* when gravity is inverted
- **Explore** four progressive levels, each requiring strategic gravity manipulation

## Core Mechanics

- **Gravity Reversal** – Stand on weighted blocks, pendulums, or inverted floors to flip gravity direction
- **Layered World** – Ground stays stable while inverted surfaces become ceilings
- **Timed Cooldown** – 3-second pause after activation to prevent spam
- **Visual Cues** – Blue pulsing glow on active objects, color-coded gravity indicator

## Quick Start

1. Launch the game in your browser
2. Use **WASD** to move and **SPACE** to jump
3. Find the **E** key to activate gravity-reversible objects
4. Discover hidden paths and beat all four levels!
