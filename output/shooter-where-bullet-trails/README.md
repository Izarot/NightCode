# Afterimage

*The gun is a pen. The trail is the trap.*

## How to Play

You don’t shoot enemies into oblivion. You shoot to **paint**. Enemies die when they touch your persistent Ink trails.

- **Move:** WASD / Arrow Keys
- **Aim:** Mouse / Right Stick
- **Fire:** Left Click / A / Cross
- **Dash:** Right Click / Shift / B / Circle

Paint trails. Lure Smudges into them. Survive the swarm.

## Tips

- Dense trails make dense kill zones, but watch your Ink.
- Dash to sever your own trail and escape your own paint.
- Use the right weapon for the right trap.
