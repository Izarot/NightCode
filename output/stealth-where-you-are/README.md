# 👻 Phantom in the House

Sneak through a pixelated Victorian manor as a mischievous ghost. Avoid the living, climb walls, phase shift through doors, and plant your crystal trap in the attic before you get caught.

## How to Play
- **Move:** Arrow Keys or WASD  
- **Jump:** Down Arrow / S  
- **Crouch:** Spacebar (slower, higher jump, more visible)  
- **Wall Climb:** Hold Left Mouse Button against a wall  
- **Phase Shift:** E (become invisible for 3 seconds)  
- **Open Door:** F  
- **Check Objectives:** R  

**Goal:** Reach the attic and place the ghost trap on the highest shelf without being detected.
