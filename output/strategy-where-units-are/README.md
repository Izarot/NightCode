# Bactaria 🦠

*A 2-D HTML5 Canvas Real-Time Strategy where you command a horde of evolving bacteria.*

## Overview

Welcome to **Bactaria**, a fast-paced RTS where your microscopic army battles for dominance on a boundless world. Think *StarCraft* meets *Antz* — except your units are bacteria that split, mutate, and evolve under your command.

## How to Play

1. **Gather Nutrients** – Move your units over nutrient patches to earn energy (E) and mutagen.
2. **Spawn & Split** – Use nutrients to spawn Plasmids, Phages, or Spores. Select a unit and issue **"Split"** to double its numbers (with cooldown).
3. **Mutate** – Kill enemies for MP, collect mutagen particles, and upgrade your strain with powerful mutations like enhanced metabolism, cell walls, toxicity, photosynthesis, or spore bursts.
4. **Outmaneuver** – Navigate obstacles, dodge attacks, and outthink your opponent through superior evolution.
5. **Win** – Destroy the opponent's colony hub, reach 500 MP, or control 75% of the map for 60 seconds.

## Core Mechanics

- **Units**: Plasmids (fast, weak), Phages (melee attackers), Spores (tanky, colony seeders).
- **Splitting**: Divide a unit into two with half health and slightly reduced speed.
- **Mutations**: Spend MP to gain abilities like faster healing, higher defense, or self-repair.
- **Resources**: **Nutrients** (spawn units) and **Mutagen** (unlock mutations).

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Mouse/Touch Drag** | Move units along path |
| **Right-Click** | Order unit movement |
| **Scroll/Zoom** | Adjust camera view |
| **R** | Toggle resource panel |
| **M** | Show/mini-map |
| **1-5** | Quick-select unit types |

## Tech Stack

- **Engine**: Pure HTML5 Canvas + JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Framework**: Vite for bundling
- **Assets**: Pixel-art sprites, particle systems, animated UI
- **Performance**: Object pooling, LOD, capped at 200 units @ 60 FPS

## Visual Style

- Cyan (player) vs Magenta (enemy) color schemes
- Pixel-art sprites with smooth scaling
- Particle effects for splits, mutations, and attacks

- **Secondary**: Accumulate 500 MP or hold territory for 60s
