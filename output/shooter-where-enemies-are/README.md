#  🎮 Ink Cleanse

*A neon-soaked arena cleanup shooter where solvent meets splatter.*

---

## 🧪 The Gist

You're the last janitor in a facility drowning in sentient ink. Armed with a solvent blaster and a dash of clean energy, dissolve waves of dripping, drifting, blobbing horrors before the floor consumes you. Speed. Efficiency. Style. That's the job.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

| Action | Control |
|--------|---------|
| **Move** | `WASD` / Arrow Keys |
| **Aim** | Mouse Position |
| **Primary Fire (Solvent)** | Left Click / Hold |
| **Clean Burst (AoE Clear)** | Right Click (3s cooldown, 20% energy) |
| **Dash / Roll** | `Space` (0.4s cooldown, i-frames) |
| **Pause** | `Esc` |

### Core Loop
1. **Survive** — Enemies spawn in escalating waves every 45s.
2. **Dissolve** — Hit enemies with solvent to trigger satisfying fade-outs + particle bursts.
3. **Manage Ink** — Missed shots & dead enemies leave puddles. They slow you down. Clean them.
4. **Score Big** — `Points = Base × Survival × Cleanup Efficiency`. Clear fast. Clear clean.

### Enemy Field Guide
| Type | Behavior | Threat |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Drip** | Stationary, short-lived | Low |
| **Splash** | Drifts toward you, splits on hit | Medium |
| **Blob** | Fast, leaves death puddle | High |
| **Boss Wave** | 1 Blob + 6 Splashes, enraged at 10s | 💀 |

## ⚡ Pro Tips
- **Ink Traction:** 3 steps in one spot = sticky floor. Keep moving or clean it.
- **Dash Smart:** Invincibility frames + speed burst. Escape corners. Reposition free.
- **Energy Economy:** Clean Burst costs 20%. Don't waste it on single puddles.
- **Efficiency = Score:** Every undissolved puddle at wave end hurts your multiplier.
