# BeatBox: Rhythm Drums 🥁

A high-energy, minimalist 2D rhythm game where you drum on neon boxes as they scroll down four lanes. Match the beat, keep the combo alive, and don't miss a hit!

## How to Play

| Lane | Key | Color |
|------|-----|-------|
| Far Left | `D` | Neon Pink |
| Middle Left | `F` | Cyan Blue |
| Middle Right | `J` | Lime Green |
| Far Right | `K` | Vibrant Orange |

- Tap the matching key when a box crosses the hit line.
- Hold keys for **Hold Boxes** (drum rolls).
- Press multiple keys at once for **Simultaneous Hits**.
- Timing matters: **Perfect**, **Great**, **Good**, or **Miss**.

Watch your combo multiplier climb and feel the screen pulse on every perfect strike!
