

# 🎨 Voxel Sculptor

Welcome to **Voxel Sculptor** — your browser-based 3D voxel modeling sandbox! Sculpt volumetric objects on an isometric grid, paint faces with vibrant materials, and export your masterpieces. No downloads required — just open and create!

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

### **Basic Controls**
- **Left-click + Drag (empty space)**: Pan camera  
- **Scroll wheel**: Zoom (0.5×–3×)  
- **Q/E**: Rotate camera 90°  
- **Arrow keys**: Nudge camera  

### **Sculpting Tools**
1. **Select** (V) – Click-drag to choose voxels  
2. **Add Voxel** (1) – Click exposed faces to build  
3. **Remove Voxel** (2) – Right-click or Shift+Left-click to erase  
4. **Paint Faces** (4) – Assign materials to individual voxel sides  
5. **Transform** – Move selections with the gizmo (drag colored arrows)  

### **Materials & Layers**
- **1–8**: Quick-select materials (Clay, Metal, Glass, etc.)  
- **Layers panel**: Organize your model with named layers  
- **Per-face painting**: Click voxel sides to customize colors  

### **Advanced Moves**
- **Ctrl+D / Ctrl+V**: Duplicate/paste selections  
- **Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y**: Undo/redo (50-step history)  
- **S**: Export PNG snapshot  
- **Delete**: Remove selected voxels  

---

## 🌟 Features
- **32×32×32 voxel workspace** with infinite grid floor  
- **8 dynamic materials** (emissive, transparent, textured)  
- **Real-time lighting** with ambient occlusion  
- **Particle effects** on voxel placement/removal  
- **Turntable export** for 3D previews  

---

## 🚀 Get Started
1. Click an exposed grid face to place your first voxel!  
2. Try painting the top face with **Neon** material (key 5)  
3. Use **Ctrl+D** to duplicate your creation  
4. Export your art as a PNG with **S**
