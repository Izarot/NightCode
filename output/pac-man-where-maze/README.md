#  🎮 Pac-Man: Peripheral Vision Maze

> **Navigate the darkness. Trust your memory. Survive the ghosts.**

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

**Move:** `Arrow Keys` or `WASD`  
**Pause:** `P` or `Escape`

### The Twist
You can only see within a **dynamic spotlight** (150px radius) that follows your movement direction. The maze, dots, power pellets, and ghosts are **invisible outside your vision**. Memorize the layout. Plan your route. Don't get cornered.

### Core Mechanics
- **Eat all dots** to win. Grab **power pellets** (yellow) to turn the tables.
- **Power Pellet Effect (10s):**  
  - Ghosts turn blue & flee  
  - Spotlight expands to **200px**  
  - Score multiplier: **2x**  
  - Chain ghost eats for bonus: 200 → 400 → 800 pts
- **Ghosts have personalities:**  
  🟥 Blinky – Hunts you directly  
  🟩 Pinky – Ambushes ahead  
  🟦 Inky – Unpredictable, Blinky-influenced  
  🟧 Clyde – Cowardly when close, bold when far
- **Lose a life** on ghost contact (unless powered up). **3 lives. No continues.**

### Scoring
