# Slime Serpent  

A neon‑slick twist on the classic snake arcade game. Slither, leave a glowing slime trail, and watch the arena get slippery as your own slime ages. Dodge walls, avoid fresh slime, and master the slippery physics to survive as long as possible.

## How to Play  
- **Controls:** Arrow keys / **WASD** to steer.  
- **Start:** Press **Space** on the title screen.  
- **Gameplay:**  
  1. Eat the magenta food orb to grow longer.  
  2. Your tail leaves a **fresh slime** that’s solid for 3 s, then becomes **stale slime** (slippery) for 4 s.  
  3. Slipping adds a half‑cell “slide” and locks direction for one tick.  
  4. Keep the **Viscosity Meter** full to unleash a **Slime Boost** (speed up for 3 s).  
- **Game Over:** Hit a wall or fresh slime.
