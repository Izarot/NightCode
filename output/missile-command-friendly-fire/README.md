# Missile Command – Friendly Fire Penalty

## How to Play
- **Move** your launcher left and right with the **← →** arrows.  
- **Launch** missiles with **Space** (subject to a short cooldown).  
- **Avoid friendly fire** – any missile that hits your own cities (or the launcher) triggers a penalty, costing points and using up your allowance.  
- **Survive waves** of incoming enemy missiles, protect your cities, and reach the target score before your friendly‑fire budget runs out or time expires.  

## Controls
| Key          | Action                |
|--------------|-----------------------|
| ← / →        | Move launcher         |
| Space        | Launch missile        |
| R            | Reset friendly‑fire budget (in Options) |
