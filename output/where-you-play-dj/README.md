## How to Play

Navigate as your DJ avatar through a series of beat-synced stages. The core challenge is **timing**: match incoming rhythmic notes to a pulsing gate that must be charged to 100% to unlock progress. Each level introduces new note types (single taps, holds, slides, rapid sequences) and increasingly complex door mechanics.

### Gameplay Loop

1. **Start** – Music plays, your DJ spawns, and notes begin falling toward you.
2. **Match** – Press arrow keys (or tap) when a note enters your hit zone.
3. **Charge** – Perfect hits fill the **Gate Charge** meter; Good hits add less, Misses drain it.
4. **Unlock** – Once Gate Charge reaches 100% and Crowd Energy remains positive, the locked door opens and you advance.
5. **Survive** – Missing too many notes drains Crowd Energy and ends the run.

### Controls

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Arrow Left/Right or A/D | Move left/right (horizontal scroll) |
| Tap / Click | Activate note zone |
| Touch drag | Slide-style note interaction |

### Quick Tips

- **Perfect (±15ms)** gives +3% Gate Charge — aim for these!
- **Holds** require pressing and releasing within the hold zone.
- **Slides** follow a curved path from start to end point.
- **Rare Rapid** patterns (4+ notes in 1 second) earn bonus combo multipliers.
