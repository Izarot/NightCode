# 🎮 Bullet Split Mayhem

## How to Play
- **Movement**: Use WASD or arrow keys to control your triangular ship. Accelerate smoothly with momentum physics and use the dash (spacebar) for quick escapes.
- **Combat**: Click to fire your semi-automatic weapon. Bullets split into 3 projectiles when hitting enemies, creating chain reactions. Position yourself strategically to maximize splits!
- **Survival**: Dodge enemy waves using wall sliding and screen wrapping. Watch your health bar and ammo counter.
- **Progression**: Unlock weapon upgrades and ship customizations as you survive longer and achieve higher scores.
