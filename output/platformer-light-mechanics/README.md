

# 🌟 Luminous Platformer

**Step into the light. Dodge the shadows. Become the beacon.**

Welcome to *Luminous Platformer*, a fast-paced 2D side-scrolling adventure where light is your weapon, your shield, and your key to progress. Navigate treacherous dark zones, solve luminous puzzles, and collect charged orbs to reach the exit!

## ⚡ How to Play

- **🏃 Move:** `Arrow Keys` to walk and run (hold `Shift` for a burst of speed).
- **🦘 Jump:** `Space` to leap. Hold it longer for a higher jump, tap to shorten it.
- **💡 Light Aura:** Press `L` to toggle your light aura on or off.
- **🔦 Light Beam:** Hold `Shift` (or Right-Click) to fire a focused beam that melts ice, breaks walls, and powers switches.
- **🖱️ Aim:** Move your mouse or touch the screen to direct your aura and beam.
- **🎯 Objective:** Collect all **Light Orbs** and **Charge Crystals**, then find the exit!
- **⚠️ Warning:** Stay out of the dark! Shadow hazards drain your health. Keep your aura active to stay safe.

## 🎮 Quick Tips

- Glowing platforms increase your aura radius.
- Mirrors reflect your beam—use them to reach hidden switches!
- Press `P` to pause whenever you need a breather.
