# Allied Ghosts

## How to Play
- **Move** with Arrow Keys or **WASD** (smooth acceleration, slight turn delay).  
- **Eat** every Pac‑Dot to score points.  
- **Grab** Special Items:  
  - *Ghost Charm* → nearby ghosts become friendly allies for 8 s.  
  - *Speed Boost* → faster Pac‑Man for 5 s.  
  - *Shield* → temporary invincibility for 4 s.  
- **Allies** chase the Goal Tile, clear dots in their path, and guard you when close.  
- **Reach** the Goal Tile after all dots are cleared before the 180‑second timer hits zero.  
- **Avoid** aggressive ghosts unless you have a shield active.  
- **Score** big by eating allied ghosts, finishing fast, and collecting items.
