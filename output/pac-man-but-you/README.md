# Maze Entity  
*You are the maze. Trap the ghosts, don’t get eaten.*

## How to Play
- **Move** with **WASD** or Arrow Keys – instant, friction‑stopped movement.  
- **Hold SHIFT** to phase through your own walls for 1.2 s (cooldown 4 s).  
- **Press C** to delete the nearest wall segment (costs 10 Mana, range 120 px).  
- As you move, a neon cyan trail becomes solid wall (costs Mana).  
- Enclose a ghost completely for ≥1.5 s to capture it – watch for the red overlay!  
- Grab Mana Orbs from captured ghosts to refill your pool.  
- Avoid touching any active ghost; collision ends the game.  
- Clear a level by trapping all ghosts – then face more, faster spirits and new abilities.

## Tips
- Toggle the 20 px grid (hold **G**) for neat, blocky corridors.  
- Watch your Mana bar – low Mana glows dim, full bar blazes cyan.  
- Power Nodes (golden spots) give bonus Mana and speed up rescued ghosts.  
- Press **F3** for debug FPS, **F2** for high‑contrast mode.
