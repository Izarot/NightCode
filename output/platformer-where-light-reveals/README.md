# Luminaut: Light & Spikes

A 2D platformer where **light reveals safe platforms** and **darkness hides deadly spikes**. Navigate through shadowy levels by managing your light source—step into the dark and you might just land on a spike!

## How to Play
- **Arrow Keys / WASD**: Move and jump
- **Mouse Move**: Direct your light source
- **Click**: Toggle light radius (limited battery!)
- **Goal**: Reach the exit portal on the far side of each level
- **Watch out**: Spikes only appear where darkness reigns—keep moving!

## Features
- Dynamic lighting engine with real-time shadow casting
- Procedurally generated levels with increasing difficulty
- Light battery management—darkness is your enemy
- Retro pixel-art aesthetic with glow effects
