## How to Play

**Objective:** Race your tokens from START to FINISH along the winding path!

1. **Start the Game** — 2-4 players compete in a race to the finish line
2. **Roll the Dice** — Hit the ROLL button or press `SPACE` to roll
3. **Move Your Token** — Glide tile-by-tile across the board
4. **Watch for Special Tiles:**
   - ⚡ **ROLL AGAIN** (blue) — Instant bonus roll!
   - ⏸️ **SKIP** (red) — Lose your next turn!
   - 🏁 **FINISH** — Victory!

5. **Win!** — First player to reach the finish tile claims the crown
