# Badminton Court: Clears & Drops

Sharpen your clears and drops in this fast-paced 2D badminton training simulator!

## How to Play

1. **Pick a Drill** – Choose from High Clear, Low Drop, Mixed Shot, or Speed Challenge.
2. **Serve** – Hit the shuttle from the back line.
3. **Execute** – Hold Shift to clear, tap Space to drop. Time it right!
4. **React** – Watch the AI return with increasing difficulty.
5. **Score** – Earn points for accuracy, timing, and power.
6. **Repeat** – Master each drill and climb the leaderboard!

Use **WASD** or arrow keys to move. Sprint with **Shift**, jump with **Ctrl**.
