# 🕰️ Chronos Descent

*A 2D HTML5 Canvas Platformer*

---

## 📜 The Premise

You are an old soul descending a crumbling clocktower. Time flows **forward, only forward** — and so do you. Each real-world second ages your character, robbing you of speed, jump height, and reflexes. Descend through 50 chambers, collect **Memory Shards** to turn back the clock, and reach the bottom of time itself before the sand runs out.

> *Time isn't your enemy. It's your stage. And the curtain is always falling.*

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

### Controls
| Action | Key |
|--------|-----|
| Move Left/Right | `←` `→` or `A` `D` |
| Jump | `Space` |
| *(Optional)* Restart | `R` |

### The Rules
- **⏳ Age in real time.** Your character grows older every 5 seconds. Start spry, end stooped.
- **⬇️ Descend, don't rush.** Each chamber is 10 screens tall. 50 chambers total. The floor number is shown top-right.
- **🦴 Frailty is real.** Jump height, walking speed, air control, and even **coyote time** all decay as you age. Land too hard at 90+? You *stagger*.
- **💎 Grab Memory Shards.** Each one reverses your age by 10 years and grants a 3-second speed boost. They are the difference between reaching the bottom and joining the dust.
- **⚙️ Watch the clockwork.** Pendulum blades, grinding gears, and crumbling platforms test your timing — and your trembling hands.
- **🏁 Reach Chamber 50.** Touch the primordial floor and the circle closes.

### Death & Victory
- **☠️ Game Over:** *"Time claimed another."* (Black & white overlay, final depth shown.)
- **🌌 Victory:** *"Time is a flat circle."* (You dissolve into particles, obviously.)

## 🛠️ Tech Notes

- **Single-file** HTML5 app — drop it into a browser, play.
- Canvas 2D rendering at **60fps** with delta-time physics.
- Sprite swaps across 4 age brackets: Young Adult → Middle-Aged → Elderly → Ancient.
- FSM-driven player states: `Idle`, `Running`, `Jumping`, `Falling`, `Stagger`, `Dead`.
- Visual decay: vignette + chromatic aberration creep in as the hourglass fills.
