# 🏜️ SANDFALL

*A 2D Tower Defense Game Built on Sand and Strategy*

---

## 🎮 What is SANDFALL?

Forget gun turrets and wizards — in **SANDFALL**, your only weapon is **sand itself**. Gather Sand Points (SP), sculpt dynamic sand walls in real-time, and use physics, geometry, and a few desperate abilities to hold back endless waves of **Erosion** enemies. Stay sharp, build smart, and protect your **Sand Citadel**.

---

## 🗯️ How to Play

### 🕱️ Building Walls (Your Main Move)
- **Click & Drag** to draw a **Standard Sand Wall**. Watch it grow segment by segment!
- Hold **`Shift`** + Drag → Build a **Reinforced Wall** (thicker, tougher, pricier).
- Hold **`Ctrl`** + Drag → Drop a **Quick Barrier** (instant and cheap, but flimsy).
- Hold **`Alt`** + Drag → **Excavate** walls to refund some SP. Sand is reusable!

### 💥 Active Ability
- **Right-Click** or **Two-Finger Tap** → Unleash **Sandblast**, a radial pulse that knocks back nearby enemies.

### 📷 Camera
- **`WASD`** or **Arrow Keys** → Pan the camera.
- **Edge Scroll** → Move by mousing near the screen border.
- **Mouse Wheel** / **Pinch** → Zoom in and out.

### 🏰 Survive the Waves
1. **Wave Starts** — Erosion creeps in from the map edges.
2. **Build & React** — Drag walls to block, funnel, or crush enemies.
3. **Wave Ends** — Earn **Sand Points** based on performance.
4. **Fortify or Excavate** — Upgrade walls for tougher fights ahead, or reclaim SP.
5. **Repeat** — Each wave gets harder, faster, and weirder.

### 🐹 Meet the Enemies
- **Standard Erosion** — Pushes and breaks your walls.
- **Sluice** — Small and slippery. Will flow around gaps.
- **Crusher** — Big and brutal. Smashes through walls in a few hits.
- **Phase** — Phases through walls... unless they're **Fortified**.

---

## 🧠 Pro Tips
- Funnel **Sluices** into tight kill zones with curved walls.
- Save **SP** for **Reinforced Walls** in chokepoints.
- A well-placed **Sandblast** can save a collapsing breach.
- Don't forget to **Excavate** — sand is a resource, not a commitment!
