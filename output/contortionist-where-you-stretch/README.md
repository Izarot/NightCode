# FlexiForm: Safe Stretch Simulator 🧘‍♂️  

**Stretch your limits—safely!** Guide a contortionist avatar through yoga-inspired puzzles by applying precise forces to joints. Balance flexibility, safety, and fatigue to master each pose without injury.  

---

## How to Play  
1. **Select a Stretch** – Choose from poses like Forward Fold or Side Bend in the library.  
2. **Execute** – Drag limbs to apply torque and reach target angles.  
3. **Stay Safe** – Keep the safety meter green! Exceeding limits triggers "danger" mode.  
4. **Recover** – Pause to reduce fatigue before your next stretch.  
5. **Complete Levels** – Hit all target poses within the time limit to earn stars and unlock new moves!  

---

- **Mouse/Touch**: Drag limbs to stretch.
