# 🎳 Bowl Pong

**Where classic pong meets physics, drama, and curved chaos.**

Bowl Pong reimagines the arcade classic with concave bowl-shaped paddles. Hit the center for a clean shot straight across the court—or graze the edges to send the ball screaming at impossible angles. Every bounce becomes a strategic choice.

---

## How to Play

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `W` / `↑` | Move your paddle up |
| `S` / `↓` | Move your paddle down |
| `SPACE` | Start / Pause |
| `R` | Restart (after game over) |

**Goal:** Be the first to score **7 points**. First to seven takes the match!

---

## The Bowl Mechanic

The curved paddle surface changes everything:

- 🎯 **Center hit** → Ball returns nearly straight
- ⚡ **Edge hit** → Ball ricochets at a sharp angle
- 💥 **Every hit** → Ball speeds up slightly

Master the curve, master the game.
