# 🚀 Pixel Quest

Grab your sword, chart the wilds, and outsmart the monsters lurking around every corner!

## How to Play

- Use **WASD** or the **arrow keys** to move.
- Press **Space** to jump over spikes and trouble.
- Click or press **J** to swing your sword.
- Collect shiny coins and find the magic key.
- Reach the glowing portal to escape and claim your reward!
- Keep an eye on your hearts—monsters and traps can knock you down.

## Game Tips

- Every boss has a pattern. Watch, wait, then strike!
- Health potions restore hearts, so grab them when you see them.
- A brave hero can always try one more run.
