# 🐍 Snake-Cylinder

Snake got a cylindrical makeover! Wrap around the edges, munch glowing food, and grow as fast as you can—without biting yourself or crashing into the walls.

## How to Play

- Steer with the **Arrow keys** or **WASD**.
- On mobile, **swipe** to change direction.
- Eat red food to grow and earn **+1 point**.
- Cross the left or right edge to reappear on the opposite side.
- Avoid your own body and the solid top and bottom walls.
- Every **5 foods**, the snake speeds up.
- Grab **rainbow food** for **+10 points** and a turbo boost.
- Find the **blue shield** for three seconds of invincibility.
- Press **P** or **Esc** to pause. Your best score is saved locally.

## Run the Game

Open `index.html` in a modern browser—no installation required.
