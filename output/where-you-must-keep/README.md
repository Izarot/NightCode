#  🎯 BOUNCE ARENA

*A minimalist survival game where the walls close in and only your reflexes keep you alive.*

---

## 🎮 HOW TO PLAY

**Objective:** Survive as long as possible inside a shrinking circular arena.

**Controls:**
- **Mouse/Touch** — Move cursor to pull your ball toward it
- **Release** — Let momentum carry you (physics baby!)
- **ESC** — Pause | **R** — Restart

**Physics:**
- Zero gravity, pure momentum
- Elastic bounces off the arena wall (98% energy retained)
- Gentle friction (0.995/frame) so you don't slide forever
- Speed capped at 12px/frame — no tunneling through walls

**The Twist:**
- Arena shrinks **0.5% every 10 seconds** (accelerates over time)
- Minimum size: **30%** of original
- Warning pulse **3 seconds** before each shrink
- Brief **invincibility** during shrink events

**Scoring:**
- Base score = survival time
- Bonus = proximity to the edge × 100
- High scores saved locally with timestamps
