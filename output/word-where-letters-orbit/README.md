# 🔥 EMBER ORBIT - Letters of the Hearth

A wild word-spelling game where letters orbit a burning hearth and you pilot an Ember Wisp to grab them in sequence before the fire dies!

## How to Play

**Goal:** Spell target words by touching their letters in order — one letter at a time — before your Fire Fuel runs out.

**Controls:**
- **Keyboard:** `A`/`D` or `←`/`→` to orbit (tangential thrust). `W`/`S` or `↑`/`↓` to push inward/outward (radial thrust).
- **Mouse / Touch:** Drag to steer; release keeps momentum.
- **Pause:** `Esc` or `P`.

**Scoring:** Correct letters multiply your Combo (every 4 correct = +1 multiplier, max x5). Wrong letters scorch the fire and reset combo. Complete words for big bonuses and stoke the flames!

**Difficulty escalates** as you level up — faster orbits, more decoys, trickier letters (Wildcards `*`, Bombs `!`).
