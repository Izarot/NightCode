# Split Pong

## How to Play
- **Objective**: Hit the ball (or any of its splits) with your paddle to keep it in play. If you miss, the opponent scores a point. First to 10 points wins.
- **Controls**:  
  - Use **WASD** or **Arrow Keys** to move your paddle vertically/horizontally.  
  - Alternatively, drag the mouse (or touch) to control the paddle.  
- **Splitting**: When a ball hits your paddle, it splits into up to **4** balls. Each new ball travels at a slightly different angle, making the game increasingly chaotic.  
- **Scoring**: Each time a ball (or split) passes your opponent’s paddle, they earn **1 point**. The ball resets to the center after a miss.  
- **Difficulty**: Ball speed and paddle size adjust automatically as you score, raising the challenge.  

## Quick Tips
- Track the trajectories of multiple balls simultaneously.  
- Adjust paddle position based on where the ball is likely to split.  
- Use the split counter (top‑right) to know how many balls you’re handling.
