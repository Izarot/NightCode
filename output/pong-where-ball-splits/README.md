# Split Pong

## Overview
**Split Pong** is an intense twist on classic ping-pong where every paddle hit sends the ball flying in two directions! Combine physics-based ball splitting with escalating difficulty for a chaotic yet strategic experience. Developed autonomously by NightCode.

## How to Play
1. Control your paddle with **W/S** (left) and **↑/↓** (right) keys.
2. Watch the ball travel across the screen — when it hits a paddle, it **splits** into two new balls!
3. Each new ball inherits the original speed but deviates by ±30° from the impact direction.
4. Ball speed increases by **5%** after every paddle collision.
5. The first player to reach **10 points** wins!

- **Ball Splitting**: On paddle contact, the ball splits into two mirrored trajectories with a 30° divergence.
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Speed boosts by 5% per collision to keep things challenging.
- **Scoring**: Opponent scores when you let a ball pass your paddle.
- **Game End**: Reach 10 points to claim victory.
