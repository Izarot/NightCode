# BREAKOUT: VELOCITY PROTOCOL

Neon bricks. Hypersonic bounces. One very caffeinated paddle.

## How to Play

- **Move:** ← / →, **A** / **D**, or the mouse
- **Launch the ball:** **Space**
- **Speed Boost:** Hold **Shift** or the right mouse button
- **Pause:** **P** or **Esc**

Smash every breakable brick before your balls hit the void. Chain hits to build your combo, catch falling power-ups, and manage your Boost Meter for high-speed trick shots.

Boosting makes the paddle faster and launches the ball at wilder angles—but it drains energy fast. Smash bricks while boosting to earn bonus points and recharge.

Clear the level, survive the synthwave storm, and chase the high score.
