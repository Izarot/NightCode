# Atelier Bloom 🌸

A cozy 2D dress-up game where you style an animated character for themed fashion briefs — no timers, no ads, just pure creativity!

## How to Play

1. **Read your brief** — Each brief (e.g., *Starlight Gala*, *Sunset Market*) tells you style tags, target colors, and required categories.
2. **Pick a category** — Hair, tops, bottoms, dresses, accessories, shoes. Click or tap to browse the wardrobe.
3. **Layer your outfit** — Drag items onto your character to stack them. Order matters! Skirts sway, scarves trail, sleeves swing.
4. **Preview & submit** — Watch your look animate, then hit "Submit" for a style score.
5. **Save & replay** — Your completed looks are saved automatically. Try different combos until you nail the brief!

## Controls

| Input | Method |
|-------|--------|
| Browse wardrobe | Mouse click / Touch tap / Keyboard arrows |
| Layer items | Drag onto character |
