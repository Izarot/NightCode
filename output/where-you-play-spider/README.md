# 🕷️ Spider Ascend

A tiny spider. A giant balloon. One very high sky.

## How to Play

- **Pump the balloon** to gain lift and climb higher.
- **Swing left and right** to dodge storms, debris, and bad decisions.
- **Collect fuel cells, silk fibers, and altitude crystals** to keep flying and score points.
- **Watch your fuel, balloon health, and the wind** — space is not very forgiving.
- **Reach space altitude** without letting the balloon burst.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys / WASD**: swing and move
- **Up / W**: ascend
- **Down / S**: descend
- **Space**: boost
- **Touch controls**: available on mobile
