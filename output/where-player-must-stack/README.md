#  🏗️ Stack‑Shift

*A wobbly, physics‑driven tower‑building arcade game. How high can you go before it all comes crashing down?*

---

## 🎮 How to Play

| Platform | Move Block | Place Block |
|----------|------------|-------------|
| **Mouse / Trackpad** | Move cursor left/right | Click **or** press `Space` / `Enter` |
| **Keyboard** | `A` / `D` **or** `←` / `→` | `Space` / `Enter` |
| **Touch** | Drag finger horizontally | Tap anywhere in the placement zone |
| **Gamepad** | Left stick ←/→ | `A` button |

1. A **preview block** follows your input horizontally.  
2. Align its center over the tower and **place** it.  
3. The physics engine settles the block—if the tower’s **center of mass** leaves the stability zone (the circle around the base), the tower topples and the run ends.  
4. Each successful block adds to your **height** and **stability multiplier**.  
5. Keep stacking! The higher you go, the tighter the stability envelope gets.

---

## 🏆 Scoring

- **Height** = number of blocks in the tower.  
- **Stability Bonus** = how close the center of mass stayed to the center (green = max, red = critical).  
- **Milestones** at 10, 25, 50, 100 blocks grant a **×1.5 score multiplier** and a tiny celebration.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5 Canvas** (2D context) + optional WebGL fallback  
- **Vanilla JS / TypeScript** – no external physics library (custom AABB + SAT)  
- **requestAnimationFrame** game loop, 60 fps target  
- **localStorage** for high‑scores & settings  
- Responsive down to 640 × 360, works on desktop & mobile
