# MEMORY MUNITIONS

You are a Keeper in a collapsing mindscape. Your weapon doesn't fire bullets—it fires crystallized memories. Spend them wisely, or risk losing them forever.

## How to Play
*   **Move:** `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or Arrow Keys
*   **Aim & Fire:** Mouse to aim, Left Click to shoot (hold for auto-fire).
*   **Dash:** `Space` or Right Click (burst of speed + brief invulnerability).
*   **Switch Ammo:** `1`-`4` keys or Scroll Wheel.
*   **Recall (E):** Instantly pull all on-screen Memory Shards to you. Use it carefully!

### The Core Loop
Survive wave after wave of "Forgetting" enemies in a neon void. Collect dropped Memory Shards to refill your magazine. If your HP drops to zero, one random Memory is permanently forgotten—keep your ammo management tight to survive!
