## 🚀 How to Play

**Move** your ship with `WASD` or `Arrow Keys`

**Collect** golden stars for points — but move fast! Build combos by collecting stars within 1.5 seconds of each other for up to **5x multiplier**!

**Avoid** the menacing red Void Orbs — they drain your lives!

**Pause** anytime with `ESC` or `P`
