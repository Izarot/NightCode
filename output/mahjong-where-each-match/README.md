# 🌩️ Tempest Tiles

A roguelike Mahjong solitaire where clearing the board is easy — surviving the weather is not!

## How to Play

- **Match free tiles:** A tile is free if its top is clear and at least one side is open. Click two matching tiles to clear them.
- **Watch the Barometer:** Every match advances the forecast. When the current weather hits, it changes everything — wind makes your cursor drift, rain makes tiles blurry, lightning freezes you, quakes shuffle the board, and fog hides tiles!
- **Play the forecast:** Match Season/Flower tiles to delay the storm, Dragon tiles to discard the next weather card, and chain quick matches for big combos.
- **Survive:** Clear the whole mountain or outlast the weather cycles. Run out of moves and Wind Chimes? Game over!

Good luck, storm chaser!
