

# Echo Plateau

### *Can you master the invisible?*

Welcome to **Echo Plateau**, a precision 2D puzzle-platformer set in a pitch-black void. The terrain is fundamentally invisible. To survive, you must rely on your memory, spatial intuition, and a limited **Resonance Pulse** ability to map out the world around you.

---

## How to Play

The goal is simple: navigate the dark, avoid the hazards, and reach the golden Exit Portal. But in the darkness, nothing is where you think it is.

### Controls
*   **Move:** Arrow Keys / WASD
*   **Jump:** Space / Ctrl (Variable height—release early to jump short, hold for max height).
*   **Resonance Pulse:** Hold **SPACE** to emit a circular wave. It reveals nearby platforms in neon cyan for a brief moment, but costs precious **Focus**.
*   **Dash:** Press **SHIFT** to dash forward instantly. Great for crossing wide gaps, but also consumes Focus.

### Core Gameplay Loop
1.  **Probe the Darkness:** Use your **Resonance Pulse** to map out the invisible layout of platforms.
2.  **Memorize:** Platforms quickly fade back into latency. Keep their locations in your visual memory.
3.  **Execute:** Leap blindly, trusting your muscle memory and the faint proximity glow.
4.  **Survive:** Avoid the deadly magenta spikes and the bottomless void. Respawn at checkpoints (Beacons) when you fail.

### Key Mechanics
*   **Proximity Glow:** Platforms within 40 pixels of your player will faintly glow, helping you find your footing without using a Pulse.
*   **Focus Resource:** Manage your purple Focus bar. If it runs out, Pulse and Dash become unavailable. Focus regenerates slowly over time.
*   **Audio Cues:** Listen closely! The ambient hum gets louder as you approach a platform edge, with directional panning helping you locate hidden surfaces.
