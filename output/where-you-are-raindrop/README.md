# 🌧️ AETHEL — Raindrop Ripple Genesis

*A meditative, physics-driven pond where every tap paints the water.*

## How to Play

There are no enemies. No scores. No failure. Just you and the water.

- **Tap the pond** to drop a raindrop. Watch ripples bloom, collide, and fade.
- **Tap rapidly** for harder rain — faster taps create bigger splashes with more foam.
- **Tap slowly and rhythmically** to compose intricate, intersecting wave patterns.
- **Drag the slider** near the bottom to summon gentle ambient rainfall.
- **Listen** — every drop plays a unique synthesized "plink" with a watery tail.

That's it. Find your flow. ✨
