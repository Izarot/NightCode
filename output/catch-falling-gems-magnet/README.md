# Magnetic Gemfall

Catch the gems. Dodge the trouble. Don't let the heavy ones drag you down.

You control a powerful horseshoe magnet at the bottom of the screen. Your field doesn't just catch falling gems — it bends their fall toward you. Use that power wisely: the same pull that grabs high-value gems will also yank in scrap, bombs, and cursed junk.

Survive, build combos, and chase the high score before your hull runs out.

## How to Play

- **Move:** Mouse, Touch Drag, or ← / → / A / D
- **Pulse:** Space, Click, or Tap — doubles your magnet field for a short burst (2.5s cooldown)
- **Pause:** Esc or P

### Gems & Trouble

- **Value Gem (green diamond):** +100 points, +1 combo. Strongly attracted to your magnet.
- **Weight Gem (purple hexagon):** +350 points, +2 combo. Heavy, fast, and hard to pull in. Catching one dips your magnet and slows you down briefly.
- **Scrap Iron:** -1 Hull, breaks combo, stuns your magnet.
- **Bomb Gem:** -2 Hull and explodes, clearing nearby gems.
- **Cursed Gem:** +500 points, but inverts your controls for 4 seconds.

Miss a Value or Weight Gem and you lose 1 Hull. Lose all 3 Hull hearts and it's game over.

Chain catches to raise your multiplier up to x8. Every 20 seconds, gems fall faster and trouble gets more common.

Good luck, magnet master!
