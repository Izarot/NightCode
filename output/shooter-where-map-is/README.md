# Cube Rotor

A neon-soaked arcade arena shooter where the floor is literally rotating beneath you.

## How to Play
- **Move:** WASD or Arrow Keys  
- **Aim & Shoot:** Mouse  
- **Survive:** Don't let the rotating cube throw you off the face. Enemies spawn from the vertices, so keep moving!  
- **Power-ups:** Triangle = Health, Square = Rapid Fire, Hexagon = Shield  

Every 8 seconds, the cube rotates 90°. Watch for the red warning arc at the top edge—then hold on tight.
