## 🌊 What's This?

Dive into a deep-sea adventure where **math meets fishing**! Operate your trusty boat, drop a line into the briny depths, and catch **numbered fish** and **operator fish** to solve equations for epic points.

Think fast. Cast faster. Solve equations before the timer bites!

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Watch the equation** at the top of the screen (e.g., `? + ? = 15`)
2. **Click & hold** to charge your cast power
3. **Release** to launch your hook into the water
4. **Click again** to reel in the line
5. **Catch fish** that match the numbers and operators you need
6. **Solve the equation** for points + time bonus + combo multiplier!
7. **Repeat** — difficulty ramps up as your score climbs 📈
