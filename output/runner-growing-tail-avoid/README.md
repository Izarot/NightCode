
# 🐍 Runner with Growing Tail

**A self-destructive endless runner where your greatest strength becomes your biggest threat.**

![Neon cyberpunk endless runner]

---

## 🎮 How to Play

| Control | Action |
|---------|--------|
| `SPACE` or `↑` | Jump |
| `↓` or `SHIFT` | Slide/Duck |
| `P` | Pause |
| `R` | Restart (after game over) |

### The Twist

Collect **glowing orbs** 🔮 to grow your magnificent tail! But beware — your tail is *you*. Touch it, and it's game over. The longer it grows, the harder it becomes to avoid your own beautiful self.

### Tips & Tricks

- **Sliding** is your friend — tuck that tail under low obstacles
- **Timing** is everything — pause to plan your tail's path
- **Risk vs. Reward** — every orb makes you stronger but more dangerous
- **Near-miss bonus** — squeak past obstacles for extra points!
