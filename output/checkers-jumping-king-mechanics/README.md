# Checkers (Draughts)

## How to Play
1. **Select a piece** – Click or tap a piece of your color. 
2. **Move or capture** – Choose a highlighted square to move your piece (regular pieces move forward diagonally; kings move any diagonal direction). 
3. **Mandatory jumps** – If a capture is possible, you must jump over the opponent’s piece and remove it. Continue jumping while possible. 
4. **Promote to king** – When a piece reaches the opposite back rank, it becomes a king and gains extra movement powers. 
5. **Win** – The game ends when a player has no legal moves or all opponent pieces are captured. 

## Controls
- **Mouse/Touch:** Select and move pieces. 
- **ESC:** Cancel selection. 
- **R:** Restart the game. 
- **U:** Undo the last move (up to two moves). 
- **+/-:** Adjust AI difficulty (if playing vs. AI).
