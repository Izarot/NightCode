# 📐 Geometric Clicker

Welcome to **Geometric Clicker**, where math meets madness! Turn your clicks into energy, your energy into shapes, and your shapes into a neon-soaked geometric empire.

## 🚀 How to Play

### 🖱️ The Basics
*   **Click to Earn:** Left-click or tap anywhere on the canvas to generate **Energy**.
*   **Move Your Avatar:** Your cursor is a small circle. Move it around to navigate the canvas.
*   **Turbo Mode:** Hold the **Shift** key to boost your movement speed from 200px/s to a blistering 600px/s!

### 🔺 Grow Your Empire
Spend your energy in the **Upgrade Panel** to purchase and level up geometric shapes:
*   **Shapes:** Triangle ➔ Square ➔ Circle ➔ Hexagon ➔ Star.
*   **Passive Income:** Each shape generates energy per second (EPS) automatically.
*   **Visual Evolution:** As you level up, your shapes don't just get stronger—they grow larger, more opaque, and emit more particles!

### 🌟 Master the Game
*   **Strategic Positioning:** Your avatar cannot pass through the interior of shapes. Position yourself carefully as your shapes grow to dominate the canvas.
*   **Prestige:** Once you've hit the ceiling, reset your progress for a permanent energy multiplier and unlock powerful **Meta-Shapes**.
