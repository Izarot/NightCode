# Sketch Defense

Draw your way to victory! Sketch shapes on the battlefield to build towers, then defend the path from waves of quirky enemies.

## How to Play

- **Draw a closed shape** with your mouse or touch to create a tower.
- **Circle** = Laser Turret  
- **Triangle** = Archer Tower  
- **Square** = Cannon  
- **Star** = Mage Tower  
- **Free-form** = Generic Turret

- Earn **gold** by defeating enemies.
- Spend gold to **upgrade** or **redraw** towers between waves.
- Use **P** to pause.
- Use **Esc** or middle-click to cancel a drawing.
- Survive as many waves as you can!
