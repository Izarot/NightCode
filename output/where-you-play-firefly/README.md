# 🎵 Lumina Conducts: Firefly Orchestra 🎵

*A glowing adventure in moonlight and melody.*

---

## ✨ How to Play

Guide your **firefly** across a moonlit stage and conduct a silent orchestra back to life with rhythmic pulses of light!

### 🎮 Controls
- **Move** — Glide your mouse or finger to guide the firefly (smooth & responsive)
- **Pulse** — Click or tap to release a wave of light that cues nearby musicians
- **Hold** — Charge a stronger, wider pulse for up to 1.5 seconds
- **Conduct** — Time your pulses with the beat line for maximum points

### 🎯 Objective
Bring the orchestra to life by pulsing on the beat near musicians. Each section — **Strings**, **Woodwinds**, **Brass**, and **Percussion** — waits to play its part. Cue them at the right moment, and watch the stage fill with sound and light.

### ⭐ Scoring
| Timing | Window | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Perfect | ±50ms | 100 |
| Good | ±100ms | 50 |
| Okay | ±200ms | 25 |
| Miss | >200ms | Combo broken |

Build **combos** for multipliers (up to 8x) and cue multiple sections at once for a **2x section bonus**! Keep your **light meter** charged — it depletes with each pulse but regenerates over time.

---

## 🌙 Tips for Conductors
- 🔆 Stay near sections when their readiness fills up — they'll dim when they need attention
- 🎶 Cue 3+ sections simultaneously for spectacular harmonies and bonus points
- 💡 Hold your pulse to cover more musicians with a single wave of light
- 🌟 As your musicality improves, the tempo rises from 90 BPM up to 140 BPM
