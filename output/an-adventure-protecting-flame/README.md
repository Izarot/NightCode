# 🔥 Flamekeeper - Protect the Flame

A cozy painterly 2D adventure where you play as a tiny spirit carrying a living flame through five handcrafted levels to reach the Eternal Hearth. Wind is your enemy — shield up, grab fuel, and keep the fire alive!

## How to Play

**Controls:**
- **Move:** `W`/`↑` or `A`/`←`
- **Jump / Double Jump:** `Space` / `↓` + `Space` (Level 2+)
- **Dash:** `Shift`
- **Shield:** Hold `E` or `S`/`↓`
- **Pause:** `P` / `Esc`
- **Mute:** `M`

**Core Loop:** Move & explore → wind gusts threaten your flame → shield / use cover / dash → collect fuel & embers → reach the exit portal → next level with stronger winds.

**Goal:** Reach the Eternal Hearth in Level 5 with the flame alive!  
**Lose:** Flame health hits 0 and no embers remain.

## Quick Mechanics

| System | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Flame Health** | Drains passively in dark zones; drains fast during wind gusts. Collect Oil Flasks (+25) and Ember Pickups (+10) to relight. |
| **Wind** | Always telegraphed 1.2s before a gust. Tier 1/2/3 push harder. Shield blocks all drain; stand behind Windbreaks for full protection. |
| **Shield Stamina** | 3.0s hold, drains 1.0/s, regenerates 0.6/s. Overuse locks out 1.5s. |
| **Braziers** | Safe checkpoints that restore flame to 100%. |

## Levels at a Glance
