# Life Point: Character Arcade Game  

## How to Play  
1. **Pick a Character** – Press **TAB** to swap between your active Life Character (LC) and the others.  
2. **Meet the Needs** – Keep **Hunger**, **Thirst**, **Energy**, **Stress**, and **Social** meters in the green.  
3. **Move & Act** – Use **WASD** (or joystick) to move, **Shift** to run, **Space** to jump, and **Q** to grab items (food, water, sleep zones, etc.).  
4. **Complete Objectives** – Follow the mission markers while juggling the LCs’ ever‑changing needs.  
5. **Swap & Strategize** – Keep an eye on the mini‑map, swap characters when one’s needs are critical, and use **Ctrl** for formation movement.  

> **Tip:** A satisfied LC moves faster, jumps higher, and can pull off slick combos. An ignored LC will wander off‑screen and become unusable!
