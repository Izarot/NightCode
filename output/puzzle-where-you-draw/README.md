# ShapeShift – Physics Puzzle

Draw it. Drop it. Let physics do the rest.

## How to Play
- Move your agent with **WASD / Arrow keys** or swipe on touch.
- Press **E** or tap the pencil to enter **Draw mode**.
- Sketch lines, rectangles, polygons, or circles to build ramps, bridges, and contraptions.
- Shapes drawn while grounded become **static**; shapes drawn mid-air become **dynamic** and obey gravity.
- Guide the ball into the goal zone before time or moves run out.
- **Z** undo · **Ctrl+Z** clear · **R** restart · **H** hint · **P** pause.

## Goal
Use your drawn shapes and physics to get the ball to the target. Faster finishes and fewer moves mean a higher score.

## Tips
- Build ramps, not walls.
- Use dynamic shapes for levers and drops.
- Respect the vertex and area limits—no giant doodles.

## Tech
Pure HTML5 Canvas + JavaScript. No external physics engine. Built for smooth 60 fps play.
