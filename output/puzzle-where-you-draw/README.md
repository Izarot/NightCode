# Chroma Cascade

Dodge the dull. Draw the destiny.

**Chroma Cascade** is a minimalist neon physics-puzzle game where you draw glowing line-segments in real time to deflect a never-ending shower of colored blocks into their matching bins. Simple to learn, brutally satisfying to master.

---

## How to Play

- **Draw lines:** Click and drag (or touch and drag) inside the play area to create physical barriers. Blocks will bounce, slide, and ricochet off your lines.
- **Match the colors:** Route each falling block into the bin that matches its color at the bottom of the screen.
- **Erase mistakes:** Right-click drag (or two-finger touch drag) to wipe away any lines you regret.
- **Shift to snap:** Hold `Shift` while drawing to lock angles to clean 45° increments.
- **Survive the timer:** You have 90 seconds. Keep your **Integrity** meter above zero by sorting at least 75% of all spawned blocks into the correct bins.
- **Speed up:** Every 15 seconds, the cascade accelerates. Stay sharp.

### Controls

| Action | Mouse | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draw line | Left click + drag | One-finger drag |
| Erase line | Right click + drag | Two-finger drag |
| Angle snap | Hold `Shift` | — |

---

## Tips

- Short lines are your friends. A well-placed 50px wedge beats a sprawling 400px bridge every time.
- Watch the motion blur trails — they tell you where a block is *going*, not just where it *is*.
- Combos stack fast when you sort within 1 second of the last block. Chain them for bragging rights.
- Bigger blocks are heavier and bouncier. Cushion their landings or they'll bulldoze your setup.
