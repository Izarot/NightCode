# ROYAL KITE LAUNCH: Shield Trampoline Knight

Bounce the Royal Kite to the Castle Beacon at 100m! Use your shield as a trampoline, steer the kite through the sky, and collect Royal Ribbons before the wind dies and the string snaps.

## How to Play

**Move the Knight:** A/D or Arrow Keys, or Mouse/Touch
**Aim Your Shield:** W/Up to tilt up, S/Down to tilt down
**Charge a Bounce:** Hold Space to crouch and charge, release to launch — time it for a PERFECT! bonus
**Pause:** P or Esc | **Restart:** R | **Mute:** M

Catch the falling kite on your shield to bounce it higher. Collect at least 5 Royal Ribbons, dodge crows, watch your string tension, and dock gently in the Beacon Zone at the top of the tower. Don't let the kite hit the ground 3 times!
