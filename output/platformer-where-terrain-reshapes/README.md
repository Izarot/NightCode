# TERRAFORM

Run fast. The ground runs with you — and sometimes against you.

TERRAFORM is a velocity-responsive precision platformer where your speed literally reshapes the world. Go slow and the terrain is calm. Go fast and it crests, ripples, and threatens to collapse beneath your feet.

## How to Play

- **Reach the rightmost checkpoint** in each of the 10 zones.
- **Manage your speed:** The faster you run, the more the Perlin-noise terrain deforms in real time.
- **Stay in the sweet spot:** Hold 6–10 px/frame for 8+ seconds to trigger **Resonance Mode** for a x2 score multiplier and stable terrain.
- **Don't fall:** Avoid the void, spikes, and terrain collapse when your speed is too high.
- **Use the world:** Hit boost pads for speed, brake zones to slow down, and activate checkpoints to reset your speed to 0.

## Controls

- **Move:** Arrow Keys / WASD / Gamepad
- **Jump:** Space / W / Up (hold for higher jumps)
- **Pause:** Esc
- **Reset:** R
- **Mobile:** Touch joystick (left) + boost button (right)

## Speed Zones

- **0–4:** Calm, static terrain
- **4–8:** Gentle undulation
- **8–12:** Active deformation — watch your step!
- **12+:** High-risk fragmentation zone
