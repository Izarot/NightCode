# REVERIE: THE RESTLESS SLEEPER

> You are a spark of consciousness. You are inside a sleeping giant. It is dreaming about you. **Run.**

A rhythm-driven endless runner set in the anatomy of a colossal dreamer. The world breathes, the heart beats, and the deeper you go, the more restless the sleeper becomes.

| **Pause** | `Esc` `P` | Start | Two-finger tap |

### The Breath Is Your Metronome
The giant inhales for **3 seconds**, exhales for **3 seconds**. Learn the cycle:

- **Inhale** → floaty gravity, faster scroll, higher jumps. Chain double-jumps here to clear wide gaps.
- **Exhale** → heavy, slow, low. Slide through rib arches at the bottom of the breath.

### Calm Is a Resource
- **Dream Shards** (amber) boost your multiplier — but raise **Restlessness**.
- **Lull Motes** (cyan) soothe the sleeper and lower Restlessness — but sit off the safe line.
- Hit **100 Restlessness** and the sleeper **SHIFTS**. The world inverts. The nightmare begins. Survive it.

You have **3 Lucidity pips**. Lose them all and the dream dissolves.

## Tips

1. Watch the lung gauge at the bottom — it tells you exactly where you are in the breath.
2. Near-misses raise your multiplier *and* your restlessness. Greed is a strategy, not a mistake.
3. Dash has i-frames. Dash *through* danger, not away from it.
4. Every hazard is readable in pure silhouette. If you can't identify it in black, you're not looking.
