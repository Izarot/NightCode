# 🎮 Chromatic Shift

*A mind-bending 2D platformer where **color changes everything** — gravity, friction, bounce, and even which way is up!*

---

## 🌈 The Core Twist
The ground beneath your feet isn't just scenery — it's **physics**.  
Land on **blue ice** and you'll slide forever.  
Hit **red rubber** and you'll bounce to the ceiling.  
Step on **purple void** and *gravity flips upside down*.  

Your color = your rules. Change color, change the game.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `←` / `→` or `A` / `D` | Move left / right |
| `↑` / `Space` / `W` | Jump |
| `Esc` | Pause |

**Collect Chromatic Orbs** to instantly swap your color — and your physics.  
**Match the portal's color** to exit each level.  
**Avoid spikes, blades, and color-sensitive enemies** — or *become* their color to defeat them!

---

## ✨ Features
- **5 color states** — Ice, Bouncy, Normal, Sticky, Gravity-Invert  
- **Color-coded puzzles** — think in physics, not just platforms  
- **Chiptune soundtrack** & retro pixel art  
- **Speedrun-friendly** — fixed 60fps physics, no RNG
