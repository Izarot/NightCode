# 🔄 Project Boomerang 🔄

*A boomerang. Two hits. Zero mercy.*

## 🎮 How to Play

**Movement:** `WASD` or `Arrow Keys` — zip around the arena with zero momentum drift. Snappy and clean.

**Aim:** Move your mouse to aim. Your little dude spins 360° to follow your cursor like a loyal puppy.

**Throw:** `Left Click` or `Z` — send your boomerang flying. It'll slice through enemies on the way out **AND** on the way back. Double trouble. 🪃

**Dash:** `Spacebar`, `Shift`, or `X` — instantly teleport 100px in your movement direction. Brief invulnerability, stylish afterimage, 2-second cooldown. Unlocks at 25,000 points.

**Pause:** `Esc` or `P` — because sometimes you need a snack.

**Restart:** `R` or `Enter` — when the inevitable happens.

## 🏆 Objective

Survive endless waves of geometric nightmares, stack that high score, and slap the big green hexagon boss every 5 minutes. Collect power-ups to evolve your boomerang into:

- ⚡ **Piercing** — Phase through fools
- ✌️ **Split** — Two is better than one
- 🎯 **Homing** — It seeks targets
- 💥 **Explosive** — Boom goes the dynamite
- 🏃 **Rapid** — Fast and furious

## 💀 Pro Tips

- Boomerangs home back to your **current** position, so keep moving to catch them in optimal spots.
- Red triangles bad. Yellow octagons? Hit 'em from behind, champ.
- The apex of the boomerang's arc is when it's slowest — *chef's kiss* for tight corridors.
