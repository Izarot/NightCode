# ORBITFALL

**Rotate the chamber. Survive the fall.**

A mobile-first survival game inside a spinning industrial ring. You’re magnetically attached to the wall. The rocks? Not so much.

## How to Play

- **Swipe or drag horizontally** to rotate the chamber: right is clockwise, left is counterclockwise.
- **Keyboard:** use **A / D** or **← / →** to rotate.
- Watch the amber hatch warnings and steer clear of incoming rocks.
- **Rocks always fall screen-down**, no matter how far you spin.
- Survive to earn points. Risk close calls for near-miss bonuses and combos up to **×5**.
- Press **P / Escape** to pause, or **Enter / Space** to start or restart.

One hit ends your run. One more try is practically inevitable.

## Run Locally

Keep `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `game.js` together, then open `index.html` in a modern browser. No downloads, libraries, or external assets required.

## Make It Yours

Toggle sound and reduced motion in the menus. Best scores and settings stay saved locally when browser storage is available.
