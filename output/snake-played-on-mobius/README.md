# Snake on a Möbius Strip 🐍🌀

A neon snake adventure with no walls—just endless, mind-bending wraps!

## How to Play

- Use **Arrow Keys** or **WASD** to steer.
- Eat the glowing food to grow and score points.
- Escape one edge of the grid and reappear on the opposite side.
- Avoid crashing into your own tail.
- The game gets faster as your level increases.

### Controls

- **P** — Pause or resume
- **R** — Restart
- **M** — Mute or unmute
