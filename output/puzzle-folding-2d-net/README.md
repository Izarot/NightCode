# Cube Net Maze Puzzle Game

## How to Play
Navigate the maze across a folding cube net! Select paths in 2D, then fold the net into a 3D cube to continue your journey. Reach the goal on each face to advance through 20 increasingly complex levels.

**Controls:**
- Arrow keys or WASD: Move your selector
- F key or tap edge cell: Fold the cube
- R key: Restart level
- Esc: Pause menu

On mobile, use the virtual joystick and fold button at the bottom of the screen.

## Game Features
- 20 challenging levels with procedurally generated mazes
- Fold puzzle mechanics requiring strategic thinking
- Time limits and move counters for competitive play
- Particle effects on level completion
- Color-blind friendly mode
