# 🐍 HUNGER GRID

*A survival Snake where the apple doesn't fall far from the tree—because it barely falls at all.*

## 🎮 How to Play

**Survive. Don't Starve. Don't Hit Walls.**

| Input | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Arrow Keys` / `WASD` | Change Direction |
| `Space` / `Tap` | Pause / Resume |
| `Swipe` (Mobile) | Steer the Serpent |

### The Rules of Scarcity
1.  **The Hunger Meter** (bottom center) constantly rises. **100 = Starvation.**
2.  **Apples are rare.** Only **one** exists on the grid at a time. Spawn rates are low (~2% per tick).
3.  **Eat to live.** Green Apples: `-25 Hunger`, `+20 HP`, `+1 Length`. Golden Apples (60s drought): Massive bonuses.
4.  **Walls are permanent.** 15% of the grid is blocked at start. Learn the maze.
5.  **Speed kills.** Every 5 apples, the tick rate increases. Capped at 25 TPS.

### Pro Tips
*   **Buffer your turns.** The game queues 2 inputs. No more accidental 180° suicides.
*   **Watch the vignette.** It centers on your head—use the darkness to judge distance.
*   **Heartbeat = Danger.** Red pulsing heart means HP < 50%. Find food *now*.
