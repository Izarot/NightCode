

# Cube‑Tic‑Tac‑Toe

A 3‑D twist on tic‑tac‑toe. Rotate a cube with six 3×3 faces and get three in a row across any visible surface.

## How to Play
- Two players (X and O) alternate placing marks on empty cells.
- Rotate the cube to view all faces.
- Form a straight line of three identical marks (rows, columns, diagonals, or cross‑face lines) to win.
- If the board fills without a winner, it's a draw.

## Controls
- **Drag** to rotate the cube.
- **Click/Tap** a cell to place your mark.
- **Enter/Space** to confirm selection, **Esc** to return to menu.
