# GravityShift

**Genre:** 2D Platformer with Gravity Manipulation  
**Play:** Open in browser — desktop & mobile.

Gravity is broken. You’re the fix.

## How to Play
1. Run, jump, and collect Gravity Orbs.
2. Press `G` to flip gravity between Normal and Inverted.
3. Hold `Shift` to enter Zero‑Gravity while in Normal mode.
4. Use gravity zones, dodge spikes, and reach the Gravity Core.

## Controls
- **Move:** Left/Right or A/D
- **Jump:** Space / Up Arrow
- **Toggle Gravity:** `G`
- **Zero‑Gravity:** Hold `Shift`

## Why GravityShift?
Because falling up is way more fun than falling down.
