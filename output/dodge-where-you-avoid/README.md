# 🍂 AUTUMN STORM 🍂

*Brace yourself. The leaves have come to claim you.*

---

## 🎮 What Is This?

A chaotic 2D survival dodge game built with HTML5 Canvas. You're a tiny figure in an orange raincoat, the sky is falling, and the autumn wind has *opinions*. How long can you stay warm?

---

## 🕹️ How To Play

### Movement
- **Arrow Keys** or **WASD** — Run for your life (or slide, technically)
- **P** — Pause when the storm gets too real
- **Space** / **Enter** — Restart after you've been leafed to death

### Mobile
- Tap and hold to move in your chosen direction. Simple. Brutal.

### The Goal
Survive. Watch that timer. Watch those hearts. Don't get hit by leaves.

> *Spoiler: You will get hit by leaves.*

---

## 🌬️ The Rules of Nature

1. **🍁 Leaves fall.** That's gravity. Blame Newton.
2. **💨 Wind blows.** It's angry. It's chaotic. It's *oscillating.*
3. **❤️ You have 3 Warmth Points.** Lose them all and become one with the autumn.
4. **⏱️ The longer you live, the worse it gets.** Spawn rate climbs. Wind intensifies. Leaves get faster. Bigger. *Meaner.*

---

## ✨ Cool Stuff To Notice

- **Momentum-based movement** — You slide. The wind pushes. It feels *heavy*.
- **Procedural maple leaves** — Every leaf is a unique polygon. No square offenders here.
- **Parallax rain** — For that dramatic "end of autumn" aesthetic.
- **Screen flash on hit** — Brief. Painful. Disorienting.
- **60 FPS storm chaos** — 50+ leaves on screen? No problem.
