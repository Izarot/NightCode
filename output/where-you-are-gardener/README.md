# Bloom & Grow: The Gardener's Quest

## How to Play
- **Move**: Use **arrow keys** or **WASD** to navigate the 10×10 garden grid.  
- **Water**: Press **Space** (or click) on a plant to water it – each watering costs **1 water unit**.  
- **Water Meter**: Starts at **10 units**, refills slowly when idle and instantly from **rain barrels** or **rain clouds**. Keep it above 0!  
- **Plant Growth**:  
  - **Seedling** → needs 1 water → **Budding** → needs 2 water → **Blooming** → needs 3 water → **Overgrown** (wilts after 10 s).  
  - Watered plants advance a stage instantly; unwatered plants grow a stage every 15 s.  
- **Challenges**: Dodge **pests**, survive **seasonal events**, and unlock new areas by blooming special plants (e.g., Pumpkin Patch).  
- **Pause**: Press **P** to open the menu for settings, saving, and stats.

*Enjoy cultivating your digital Eden!*
