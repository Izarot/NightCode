# Railbreaker

*The last railgun you’ll ever love to charge up.*

## How to Play

You’re a lone exo-suit operator in a neon-noir industrial nightmare. Your only friend is the railgun. Your only problem is everything.

- Hold to charge your shot. Release to fire.
- Low charge: fast scratch. Medium charge: pierces one enemy. Full charge: rail through the whole damn level.
- Dodge, dash, and reposition. Recoil is real. Consequences are realer.
- Don’t touch the spikes.

## Controls

- Move: `A` / `D` or arrows
- Jump: `W` / Up / Space
- Dash: `Shift`
- Aim: Mouse / right stick
- Charge Fire: Hold left click / hold right screen

## Death

You die. It’s fine. Again.
