# 🎨 Paint‑Trail Rally

> **Top‑down racing meets tactical route‑painting.** Drive, splash color, and guide your AI allies to victory!

---

## 🚗 How to Play

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **W / ↑** | Accelerate |
| **S / ↓** | Brake / Reverse (max 30 % speed) |
| **A / ←** | Turn left |
| **D / →** | Turn right |
| **Space** | Handbrake – drift harder! |

1. **Spawn** with a full **Paint Meter** (100 %).  
2. **Drive** around the arena – your car leaves a colored trail.  
3. **Paint** consumes meter (0.5 %/s while moving, 0.1 %/m of trail). Idle or coast to refill (1 %/s).  
4. **Switch paint** (1‑4) for Red, Blue, Green, Yellow – each gives different trail width & safety rating.  
5. **Allies** (Scout, Guard, Carrier) automatically follow painted paths, gaining **+15 % speed** and **30 % damage reduction** while on your trail.  
6. **Complete the map objective** – escort allies, paint a required length, or survive the timer.  

---

## 🎮 Quick Tips

- **Drift** (Space) to hug tight corners and lay sharper trails.  
- **Manage your meter** – coast on straightaways to recharge.  
- **Layer trails** – multiple allies on the same path stack speed boosts (up to 2×).  
- **Watch for hazards** – spikes, enemy fire, ice patches. Painted routes keep allies safe.
