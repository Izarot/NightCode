# Ninja Star Dodge

**A fast‑paced side‑scrolling dodge adventure!**  
Control a spinning ninja star and weave through incoming boomerangs. Timing is everything—evade, combo, and rack up the highest score before you get hit!

## How to Play

- **Move** – Use the arrow keys or **W/A/S/D** to steer the star left/right (and up/down if you fancy a top‑down twist).  
- **Pause** – Press **Space** to pause/resume the game.  
- **Fullscreen** – Hit **Esc** to toggle fullscreen mode.  

**Goal:**  
- Dodge every boomerang that spawns from the edges.  
- Keep your combo chain alive for extra points.  
- Avoid collisions—any hit ends the game!  

The game ends when you’re hit, but you can try again to beat your high score.
