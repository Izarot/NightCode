# PHOTON REFLEX

**Arcade Survival / Physics Dodge** — A high-speed neon-noir experience where you control a photon bouncing through a collapsing mirror chamber, dodging Void Drones and surviving waves of chaos.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

- **Control**: Use arrow keys or WASD to steer. Hold **Up** for a 1.5× boost (cooldown: 3s).
- **Reflect**: Hit mirrors to bounce—each successful reflection earns points!
- **Survive**: Avoid Void Drones, conductive tiles, and the deadly outer walls.
- **Power-ups**: Collect Prism Shards (double reflection angle) or Phase Shifts (become invincible briefly).

> **Core Hook**: You can’t stop moving—only redirect your path with reflections and precise thruster adjustments.

---

## 📋 Features

- ⚡ **Physics-Driven Movement**: Constant velocity, perfect mirror reflections, energy loss after bounces
- 🔄 **Dynamic Environment**: Rotating mirrors, phase-shifting walls, and procedurally spawning enemies
- 💨 **Combat & Progression**: Destroy drones, collect power-ups, and unlock combo multipliers
- 🎨 **Neon Noir Visuals**: Additive-blend trails, bloom, chromatic aberration, and a cyberpunk aesthetic
- 🗣️ **Hud**: Live score, health bar, ability status, and combo counter

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Open the game in any modern web browser (or download the standalone package).
2. Press **ESC** to pause.
3. Use **W/A/S/D** or **Arrow Keys** to maneuver.
4. Watch your cyan photon dart through the chamber!
