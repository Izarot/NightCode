# SKETCHBOUND

Draw it. Bring it to life. Play as it.

## How to Play
1. **Sketch** your creature in the workshop — scribbles, lines, and abstract shapes all work.
2. **Pick** a badge ability: Feather (glide), Spark (dash), or Spring (double jump).
3. **Preview** your character, then leap through 5 handcrafted sketchbook levels.
4. **Collect** ink droplets and gold stars, hit checkpoints, and reach the exit.
5. **Edit** anytime — your progress stays safe.

## Quick Specs
- Desktop: Arrow/WASD + Space, Touch: on-screen buttons
- 60 Hz physics, local save, export/import characters
- Reduced-motion, high-contrast, and portrait-mode friendly
