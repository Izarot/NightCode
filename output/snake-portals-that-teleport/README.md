# Portal Snake

A fast‑paced arcade twist on classic Snake where teleporting portals add puzzle‑like routing and risk/reward decisions.

## How to Play
- **Move** the snake with Arrow Keys / WASD or swipe on touch devices.  
- **Eat** the glowing food to grow longer and increase your score.  
- **Avoid** hitting the walls or your own tail – that ends the game.  
- **Enter** a portal to instantly teleport to its paired exit, preserving your direction.  
- Each portal use slightly reduces your score multiplier, so use them wisely.  
- Survive as long as you can, chase the high score, and restart for a new run.
