# Aether Kite

## How to Play
Launch your kite into the wind and try to stay airborne longer than your regrets. Steer with **WASD / Arrow Keys**, adjust line tension with **Zoom**, pull off **tricks**, ride **thermals**, and chase high scores across breezy zones.

## Controls
- **Arrows / WASD**: Fly
- **Space / Tap**: Trick
- **Touch**: Left stick + shoulder buttons
