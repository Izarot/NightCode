# Raccoon Cartographer
A whimsical 2D side‑scrolling adventure where you play as Rex, a curious raccoon cartographer, mapping a living city that reshapes itself behind you.

## How to Play
- **Move & Jump:** Use WASD or Arrow keys. Run, jump (and double-jump) through vibrant districts.
- **Scan & Map:** Press **E** near city tiles to scan them. Scans cost energy (regenerates over time) and populate your persistent map.
- **Rearrange the City:** Hold **R** for 1 second to swap two adjacent **unlocked** tiles. Rearrange ground, buildings, water, and landmarks to create new paths and reveal fragments.
- **Collect Fragments:** Gather all 5 Map Fragments per level to unlock new districts. Grab Compass Stones for a temporary magnet effect.
- **Win:** Collect all fragments and solve the final “Map Completion” puzzle by rearranging the city to match your map.
- **Watch Out:** Dodge moving vehicles, wind gusts, and glitch zones that distort the layout.

## Controls
- Move: WASD / Arrow keys
- Jump: Space
- Scan: E
- Rearrange: Hold R (1s)
- Inventory: Q
- Pause: Esc
