# **Health‑Ammo Mayhem**  

*An adrenaline‑pumping 2D top‑down shooter where your health **is** your ammunition.*

---  

## How to Play  

1. **Move** – Use **WASD** (or arrow keys) for smooth, momentum‑friendly 8‑directional movement.  
2. **Aim** – The mouse points your weapon; fire with the left mouse button.  
3. **Shoot** – Every shot costs health equal to the damage dealt (5 HP per shot).  
   - You can’t fire if your health is below the shot cost.  
   - When health hits **0**, you die.  
4. **Collect** – Grab **vitality packs** (+25 health) to restore both health and ammo.  
5. **Survive** – Balance aggression with caution: high health = more ammo = more fire, low health = fewer shots = high tension.  

> **Tip:** Keep an eye on the glowing core—its pulse tells you how much “ammo” you have left.
