# Artillery Ace: Trajectory

## How to Play

**Goal:** Hit all targets using your cannon before running out of shells!

1. **Aim:** Use the mouse wheel or arrow keys to rotate the cannon barrel. Adjust the angle between 10° and 170°.
2. **Set Power:** Click and drag down from the cannon, or press Spacebar to cycle power from 10% to 100%.
3. **Fire:** Release the mouse button or press Enter to launch your projectile.
4. **Watch the Arc:** Your shot will follow a realistic parabolic path affected by gravity and wind.
5. **Hit Targets:** Destroy all ground and aerial targets to complete each level.
6. **Manage Ammo:** You have limited shells per level—make every shot count!

**Pro Tip:** Use the trajectory preview (if available) to plan your shots, and watch the wind indicator for adjustments!
