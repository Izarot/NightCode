# Refraction Tag

## How to Play
- **Move** with **WASD** (or arrows). Hold **Shift** to sprint, **Space** for a quick dash (cooldown). Slide with **Ctrl** while sprinting.  
- **Primary Fire (LMB)** – Point the **Bending Beam** at walls. Rotate segments to open fire lanes, trap enemies, or redirect projectiles.  
- **Secondary Fire (RMB)** – Shoot rapid pulse rounds. Ricochet off walls for extra damage.  
- **Ability (Q)** – Throw an overload grenade that EMP‑locks walls and stuns enemies.  
- **Objective** – Clear each procedural sector of drones, break through obstacles, and reach the exit.  
- **Upgrades** – Visit sector terminals to choose upgrades like kinetic dash, split beam, grav‑boots, or overclocked ricochets.
