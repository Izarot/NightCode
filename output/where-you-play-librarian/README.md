# 🌟 STELLAR ARCHIVE ✨

> *A cosmic cataloging adventure through an infinite library of light.*

## What Is This?

You are a **Celestial Librarian**—a quiet archivist adrift in the velvet void, tasked with documenting every star you encounter into the **Grand Cosmic Catalog**. No combat. No pressure. Just you, your vessel, and the endless night full of untold stories.

## 🚀 How to Play

Glide gracefully through space using momentum-based physics. Drift, don't dash. When a star drifts into range, lock on and catalog it.

### Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `W` `A` `S` `D` / Arrow Keys | Thrust in any direction |
| `SHIFT` | Boost (2× speed, more particles) |
| `SPACE` | Scan & catalog a star in range |
| `TAB` | Toggle your Stellar Catalog |
| `ESC` | Pause the cosmos |

### The Mission

- 📚 **Catalog stars** of all 7 spectral classes (O, B, A, F, G, K, M)
- ✨ **Find rare entries** — Golden Stars, Binary Pairs, and Pulsars
- 🏆 **Complete the archive** — track progress across every class
- 💾 **Your journey saves** automatically to your browser

### Tips for New Archivists

- Keep moving! New stars bloom into existence at the edges of your view
- Rare stars are worth **far more points**—don't miss a Pulsar
- Open the Catalog with `TAB` to see your spectral breakdown
- There's no rush—the universe isn't going anywhere
