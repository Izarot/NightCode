A neon-noir wizard survival duel — dodge the dark arts, survive the phases, and crush the evil wizard!

## How to Play

**Controls:**
- `WASD` / `Arrows` — Move (8-directional)
- `Space` / `Shift` — Dash/Blink (140px, i-frames, cooldown)
- `E` — Counter-Spell (when meter is full)
- `P` / `Esc` — Pause
- `M` — Mute audio
- `Enter` — Confirm in menus

**Goal:** Survive **5 phases** and defeat the Evil Wizard. Each phase escalates spell patterns — fireballs, frost shards, lightning, homing orbs, then a brutal spell storm. Dodge everything, grab mana crystals, and unleash your counter-spell when ready.

**Scoring:** +10/sec survived · +50/phase cleared · +100/near-miss
