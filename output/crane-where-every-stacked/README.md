# 🪛 Crane Game: Center of Mass Stacking

## How to Play
Stack the tower, not the chaos.

Move the crane, grab quirky cargo, and drop it onto the pile. Every object has a sneaky center of mass, so things can wobble, tip, and ruin your day in hilarious ways.

- Move the crane with the mouse.
- Hold `SHIFT` for fine adjustments.
- Grab, drop, repeat.
- Keep the stack stable before gravity files a complaint.

## Controls
- **Mouse:** Move crane
- **SHIFT:** Fine-tune placement
- **Release:** Drop cargo
