# 👽 MOTHERSHIP: Inverted Space Invaders

You are the alien Mothership. Earth is below. Humanity is shooting up. Time to invade.

## How to Play

**Move:** `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` to drift, `W` / `S` or `↑` / `↓` for vertical thrust  
**Fire:** Hold `Space` or Left Click for your Plasma Lance — watch the heat!  
**Dash:** `Shift` for a warp burst  
**Cluster Bomb:** `F` / Right Click  
**Tractor Beam:** Hold `Q` to suck up enemy bullets for Energy  
**Drone:** `C` to deploy a temporary wingman  
**Deploy Pod:** Hold `E` while hovering still over a destroyed city  
**Pause:** `P` | **Mute:** `M`

### Objective

1. Destroy the **Shield Generators** to make the cities vulnerable
2. Destroy all **4 Human Cities**
3. Deploy **3 Invasion Pods** — hover over each deploy zone and keep them alive for 10 seconds

Don't let your Hull hit 0, and don't let all your Pods get shot down!

Destroy turrets, SAM launchers, and fighter jets for score and Energy. Graze bullets to gain even more. Survive all 3 sectors to conquer Earth.
