# Neural Sketch

**A fast‑paced, multiplayer drawing & guessing showdown where your strokes meet AI insight!**

---

## How to Play

1. **Join a Room** – Enter a lobby, pick a name, and get ready.
2. **Draw!** – When it’s your turn you’ll get a secret prompt (e.g., *“A sleepy cat on a keyboard”*). Use the brush tools, layers, and undo/redo to capture the idea.
3. **Guess** – Other players type their guesses into the chat‑style input. The first correct guess (or a synonym) scores points for the drawer and the guesser.
4. **AI Spectator** – A local AI runs in the background, analyzing the canvas every 500 ms. If it’s confident enough, it can even guess the prompt for a bonus!
5. **Score & Win** – Earn points for correct guesses, time bonuses, and AI‑assisted clues. Reach the target score or out‑last everyone in the max‑round limit to claim victory.

**Controls**
- **Desktop:** Mouse or stylus for drawing; `Tab` to switch layers; `Shift` for shape helpers.
- **Mobile:** Touch to draw; pinch to zoom; tap tools.

---

## Quick Tips
- **Pressure matters:** Faster strokes = thinner lines, slower strokes = bold strokes.
- **Use layers:** Background for scenery, foreground for details.
- **Watch the AI:** Its confidence meter can hint at the answer—use it wisely!
