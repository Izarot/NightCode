# 🕯️ LUMEN STRIDER

> *A 2.5D atmospheric platformer where light is the only solid ground.*

---

## 🌑 What Is This?

You are a fragile wisp carrying a flickering lantern through absolute darkness. **Platforms only exist where light touches them.** Walk off the light, and you fall into the hungry void.

Every step is a commitment. Every shadow is a question.

## 🎮 How to Play

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| **Walk** | `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` |
| **Jump** | `Space` / `W` / `↑` |
| **Focus Beam** (narrow & long-range) | Hold `Shift` or `Right Click` |

### Core Rules
- ⛔ **No light = no ground.** Platforms are silhouettes that vanish the instant your lantern leaves them.
- 🔋 **Your lantern drains.** Find glowing **Light Orbs** scattered in levels to recharge.
- 🎯 **Mistimed jumps are fatal.** You have 6 frames of *coyote time* after leaving a ledge and 6 frames of *jump buffering* before landing — use them.
- 🐍 **Shadow Eels** hunt the edge of your light. Stay illuminated.
- 👻 **Light Wraiths** live *inside* the light and snuff it out. Avoid them.

### Tips from the Void
- The lantern **flickers** between 78px and 82px. A platform you just stood on may betray you.
- Your lamp **tilts** as you move and falls — this shifts the light pool, so watch your footing.
- Use **Focus mode** to throw a narrow beam across chasms and spot distant ledges (a ghostly silhouette will flash in the beam). But beware: your feet go dark while focused.
- Listen for the **sub-bass drone** — it means an Eel is near, even when you can't see it.

## 🗺️ The Journey

5 Chapters. 25 Levels. One Eternal Dark.

1. **The Awakening** — Learn the rules. Wide gaps, slow drains.
2. **Flickering Ruins** — Timed lanterns pulse on and off. Time your crossings.
3. **The Sunken Gallery** — Bounce light off reflective water to create temporary platforms.
4. **The Clockwork Spire** — Ride sweeping beams from giant gears.
5. **The Eternal Dark** — Minimal light. Pure momentum. Pure mastery.

Collect the **Echo Fragments** — three hidden in every level.
