# ChronoShift

**A mind-bending 2D platformer where time bends to your will!**

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Run & Jump** through challenging pixel-art levels using `A/D` or `←/→` to move and `W` or `Space` to jump.
2. **Rewind Time** by holding `Q` to undo mistakes and relive the last 10 seconds of action.
3. **Fast Rewind** by holding `Q` + `Shift` to quickly zip back to a crucial moment.
4. **Collect gems**, avoid hazards, and reach the goal flag to complete each level.
5. **Master time** — use rewinds wisely to solve puzzles and perfect your runs!

## 🕹️ Controls

- **Move:** `A` / `D` or `←` / `→`
- **Jump:** `W` / `Space`
- **Rewind:** Hold `Q`
- **Fast Rewind:** Hold `Q` + `Shift`
- **Pause:** `Esc` or `P`

*Mobile players can use on-screen touch controls!*

## ⚡ Features

- **Time-Rewind Mechanic:** Undo up to 10 seconds of gameplay in real-time.
- **Precise Physics:** Smooth platforming with Coyote Time and Jump Buffer.
- **Retro Pixel Art:** 16-bit inspired visuals with parallax backgrounds.
- **Challenging Levels:** Test your skills across increasingly tricky stages.
