# Gear Master: Align & Activate!  

**How to Play:**  
Navigate your character using **WASD/arrow keys** through a grid-based world where every move counts! Align gears by clicking them to rotate (left-click for smooth motion, right-click to snap to 90°). Each gear (small, medium, large) spins at different speeds—get them *perfectly synced* within ±5° of their target angles to power the glowing core!  

Fill the **power meter** by aligning all gears simultaneously, and watch the door swing open with a satisfying hinge animation. Keep an eye on the HUD:  
- **Power Meter:** Circular bar filling as gears align.  
- **Gear Indicators:** Green/red icons show real-time alignment status.  
- **Door Status:** Unlock the exit when fully powered!  

**Pro Tip:** Misalignment resets your progress—precision is key!
