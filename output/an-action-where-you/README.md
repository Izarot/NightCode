# 🗡️ BLADE REFLEX

*A high-tension neon dodge-'em-up where the only way out is through the gaps.*

---

## 🎮 How to Play

**Objective:** Survive the endless cascade of falling knives. Don't get hit.

### Controls
| Platform | Move Left | Move Right | Pause |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Desktop** | `←` / `A` | `→` / `D` | `P` |
| **Mobile** | Tap Left Zone | Tap Right Zone | — |

### The Rules
1.  **Dodge.** Knives fall from the top. Some drop straight, some *wave*, and the heavy ones crush the unwary.
2.  **Glide.** Movement is momentum-based—tap to nudge, hold to drift. Master the friction.
3.  **Endure.** You have **3 Shields**. A hit costs one and grants 1.5s of invulnerability (you'll flicker).
4.  **Score.** Points for survival, bonuses for streaks. The spawn rate accelerates every 10 seconds.
5.  **Die.** Lose all shields → Game Over. Beat your High Score (saved locally).

## ✨ Features
*   **Dynamic Difficulty:** Spawn rates tighten automatically as you survive.
*   **Three Knife Archetypes:** Straight, Sine-Wave, and Heavy Tank variants.
*   **Juicy Feedback:** Screen shake, particle bursts, near-miss sparks, and damage vignettes.
*   **Synthwave Aesthetic:** Neon outlines, grid floors, and a pulsing HUD.
*   **Zero Dependencies:** Pure HTML5 Canvas / Vanilla JS. Runs offline.
