

# 🌍 World Tilt

**A gravity-bending physics platformer where you tilt the world, not the ball!**

## 🎮 How to Play

Control gravity by tilting the entire world to guide your rolling ball through challenging obstacle courses to reach the goal.

### Controls
- **A/D or ←/→**: Tilt world left/right (gravity pulls sideways)
- **W/↑**: Reset tilt (gravity pulls straight down)
- **S/↓**: Tilt world "up" (gravity pulls upward - advanced move!)
- **Mouse Drag**: Click and drag horizontally to set tilt angle
- **Space**: Jump (when grounded) - optional toggle

### Goal
- Collect all golden orbs in each level
- Avoid toxic green hazards
- Reach the cyan goal zone
- Complete levels as fast as possible for bonus points!
