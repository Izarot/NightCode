# Echo Maze

Navigate the labyrinth using only your ears!

## How to Play
- Use **WASD** or **arrow keys** to move your avatar
- Listen carefully to the unique sound patterns:
  - Low hums = straight walls
  - High-pitched beeps = corners
  - Clanks = collisions
- Find the exit (marked by a distinct chime) before time runs out
- Press **SPACEBAR** to replay the current area's sound map
- Collect audio keys to unlock new sound cues

## Features
- Procedurally generated mazes with unique audio signatures
- Smooth movement physics with 0.5s response time
- Pulsating HUD that reacts to your proximity to objectives
- Built-in tutorial with audio instructions
- Volume controls for accessibility
