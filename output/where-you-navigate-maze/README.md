# 🌀 Torus Maze Navigator

> *An infinite labyrinth that loops on itself. Collect the data. Outrun the Glitch. Break the loop.*

---

## 🎮 How to Play

You wake up in the center of a maze that **wraps around itself**. The right edge connects to the left. The top edge connects to the bottom. There is no edge. There is only the loop.

### Your Mission
1. 🔷 **Collect all 4 Data Fragments** — glowing cyan diamonds scattered across the torus.
2. ⚪ **Reach the Exit Gate** — it only activates once every fragment is yours.
3. 🔁 **Break the Loop** — escape and a new maze generates. How fast can you go?

### Controls
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `WASD` / `Arrow Keys` | Move (grid-locked, smooth lerp) |
| `Spacebar` | **Dash** — burst to 9 cells/sec for 1.5s (15s cooldown) |

### Threats
- 👾 **The Glitch** hunts you using A\* pathfinding. It gets faster every 10 seconds. Touching it knocks you back 2 cells and costs you 1 fragment.
- 🚫 **Walls** block movement, but you can slide along them on axis-aligned impacts.
- 🌍 **The topology itself** — your trail helps you learn the seams. Cross an edge, and you'll emerge on the opposite side.

### Pro Tips
- 🗺️ **Watch the minimap** — gradient edges glow to remind you: *this world has no borders.*
- 🏃 **Save your dash** for tight corridors or last-second escapes.
- 👂 **Listen** — the Glitch's drone gets louder as it closes in.
- 🔁 **The loop continues** until all fragments are collected. The Glitch doesn't end your run — it just makes it harder.
