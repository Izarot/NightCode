# Maze Rotator

A grid‑based puzzle game where you rotate pre‑placed room tiles to align doorways and create a navigable path.  

## How to Play

1. **Select a Room** – Click (or tap) any room tile to highlight it.  
2. **Rotate the Room** – Use **Q** (counter‑clockwise) or **E** (clockwise) – or the on‑screen buttons – to spin the room 90°.  
3. **Match Doors** – Doors appear on the four sides. Rotate until doors line up, opening a passage.  
4. **Navigate** – Move with **WASD** (or touch controls). Hold **Shift** to sprint.  
5. **Solve** – Connect the start to the exit within the allowed rotations and moves.
