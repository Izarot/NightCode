

# ⏳ Chrono Clash

**Time is your weapon. Speed is your score.**

Dive into **Chrono Clash**, a retro-futuristic 2D action-platformer where you bend time itself to survive! Traverse five worlds, dodge rotating blades, and race against the clock to top the global leaderboard.

## 🎮 How to Play

*   **⬅️ ➡️ Arrow Keys** – Move Left/Right
*   **⬆️ Up / Space** – Jump (press again in mid-air for a **Double Jump**!)
*   **🏃 Shift** – Sprint (Run Speed)
*   **🔄 R** – **Rewind Time** (Requires a green Chrono Orb!)
*   **⌨️ E** – Interact with prompts

## 🏆 The Goal

1.  Collect **Chrono Orbs** to build a rewind buffer.
2.  Avoid spikes, blades, and falling off the edge!
3.  Find hidden **Temporal Keys** to unlock bonus stages.
4.  Beat the timer and climb the **Global Leaderboard**.

## 🌍 Worlds

Forest → Clockwork → Neon City → Space Station → Temporal Abyss
