# Deflect Blade

A fast-paced 2D HTML5 Canvas shooter where you swing a sword to deflect incoming bullets and send them back at your enemies!

## How to Play

- **Move**: Use Arrow keys or on-screen joystick to dodge incoming projectiles.
- **Swing**: Press Shift (or tap the attack button) to swing your sword and catch bullets.
- **Deflect**: Bullets caught during the swing are redirected 180° with increased speed toward the enemy.
- **Survive**: Last as long as possible, collect power-ups, and achieve the highest score by chaining deflections.
- **Goal**: Eliminate waves of enemies, avoid direct hits, and climb the leaderboard!
