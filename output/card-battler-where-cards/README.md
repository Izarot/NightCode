# Weather Card Battler

## How to Play
- **Draw & Play:** At the start of each turn you draw 5 cards. Spend energy (3 per turn) to play cards from your hand.
- **Summon Weather:** Use weather cards to alter movement physics, boost attacks, or hinder your opponent. Only one weather effect can be active at a time.
- **Move & Battle:** Move left/right across the arena, switch lanes with **Space**. When you get close to the opponent, attacks trigger automatically. Use skill cards for special moves.
- **Win Conditions:** Reduce the opponent’s health to 0, exhaust their deck, or out‑last them when both decks run out.
