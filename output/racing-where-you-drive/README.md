# Velocity Rush

Burn rubber on dynamic, obstacle-filled tracks! Customize your ride, master the drift, and leave your opponents in the dust.

## How to Play

*   **Steer**: Arrow Keys or WASD
*   **Accelerate/Brake**: Up/Down Arrow Keys or W/S
*   **Drift**: Hold the turn key sharply at high speed to skid and maintain momentum around tight corners.
*   **Power-Ups**: Collect Boost Pads for speed, Oil Slicks to slow rivals, and Shields to absorb one collision.
*   **Objective**: Complete laps, hit all checkpoints, and cross the finish line first!
