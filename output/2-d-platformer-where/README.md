# Bridge Builder

A side‑scrolling 2‑D platformer where you create temporary bridges to cross gaps, solve puzzles, and avoid hazards.

## How to Play
- **Move:** ← → or A / D  
- **Jump:** ↑ or Space / W  
- **Build Bridge:** B (or tap the Build button) – costs Bridge Points (BP)  
- **Crouch:** Hold Shift to slip through tight spaces  
- **Dash:** Double‑tap left/right (costs 5 BP)  

Collect BP (starts at 100, regenerates 5 BP/s) to build longer bridges. Bridges last 5 seconds before fading. Reach checkpoints to restore BP and save progress. Avoid spikes and enemies, or trap them with your bridges.
