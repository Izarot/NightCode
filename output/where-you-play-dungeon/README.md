# DUNGEON MASTER: TRAP LAYER

## How to Play

**Goal:** Build deadly dungeons and stop the AI hero from escaping!

1. **Plan Phase** (30s): Place traps on the grid before the hero arrives. You have a limited budget — spend wisely!
   - Left-click to place traps
   - Right-click to remove traps (full refund)
   - Place walls to block paths (only 5 per level)

2. **Simulate Phase**: Watch the hero navigate your dungeon in real-time.
   - Hero uses A* pathfinding to find the exit
   - Traps trigger on contact — deal damage, slow, or instantly kill
   - If the hero dies → YOU WIN!
   - If the hero escapes → YOU LOSE!

3. **Level Up**: Win to advance. Each level gives you more budget but tougher challenges.

### Trap Types
| Key | Trap | Cost | Effect |
|-----|------|------|--------|
| 1 | Spike | 1 | 30 damage on touch |
| 2 | Pit | 2 | Instant kill |
| 3 | Fire | 2 | 15 damage per tick (3x) |
| 4 | Freeze | 3 | 50% slow for 3 seconds |

### Controls
- **Mouse**: Place/remove traps, click buttons
- **Keys 1-4**: Quick-select trap types
- **Space**: Start simulation early
- **Esc**: Cancel current selection

## Scoring
- **Win**: (Traps Used × 10) + (Hero HP Lost × 2) + Speed Bonus
- **Lose**: -5 points
- High scores saved locally!
