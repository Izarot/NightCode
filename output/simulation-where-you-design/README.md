# Roller Coaster Designer 🎢  
Design wild coasters, test physics, and thrill passengers!  

## How to Play  
1. **Draw Tracks**: Click-drag to sketch paths. Curves auto-smooth, and loops stay safe with a 50px minimum radius.  
2. **Add Features**: Use the toolbar to insert launches, brakes, or boosts. Snap to the 20px grid for precision.  
3. **Test Ride**: Press "Test" to ride your coaster! Watch G-forces, speed, and passenger happiness.  
4. **Tweak & Repeat**: Adjust elevation, add templates, or fix derailments. Share your design or race for the fastest time!
