
# 🧪 Alchemical Rhythm

*A 2D rhythm-puzzle game where you mix potions by tapping, dragging, and releasing ingredients in time with the beat.*

---

## 🎵 How to Play

1. **Watch the Beat** — A pulse bar at the top of the screen keeps rhythm with the music.
2. **Grab Ingredients** — Tap or click to select ingredients orbiting the cauldron (Fire Leaf, Moon Water, Stone Dust, and more!).
3. **Mix on Beat** — Drag your ingredient to the cauldron and release on the beat for maximum points.
   - 🟢 **Perfect** (±10ms): Big points + particle burst!
   - 🟡 **Good** (±30ms): Decent score
   - 🔴 **Miss**: No points, progress bar takes a hit
4. **Build Combos** — Chain successful mixes within 2 beats to multiply your score!
5. **Hold for Power** — Press and hold an ingredient for 0.3+ seconds before releasing to increase potency and boost your score.
