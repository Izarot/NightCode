# Typing Runner: Dash

**Type your way to victory! Dash through obstacles by typing words faster than your opponents.**

## How to Play

Get ready for an adrenaline-pumping side-scrolling adventure where your typing speed determines your fate!

### Movement Controls
- **Type words** to dash forward at incredible speeds (up to 480 pixels/second!)
- **Spacebar** to jump over obstacles
- **Backspace** to correct mistakes in your words

### Game Mechanics
- Watch the word queue at the bottom of your screen
- Type the highlighted words to build up your dash meter
- Time your dashes perfectly to avoid obstacles and collect coins
- Build combos by typing consecutive words without mistakes
- Watch out for the death zone 200 pixels behind you!

### Scoring System
- Earn points for every character typed
- Get speed bonuses for how far you travel during dashes
- Build multipliers by maintaining accurate typing streaks
- Collect floating coins for extra points
- Avoid letting words expire - it costs you points!

### Power-ups & Hazards
- **Coins**: Collect for bonus points
- **Spikes**: Avoid these deadly obstacles
- **Barrels**: Jump over or dash through carefully
- **Pits**: Watch for gaps in the terrain

Can you reach the highest score and become the Typing Champion?
