# Elemental Bastion

Defend the crystal core from waves of elemental foes in this fast-paced tower defense showdown!

## How to Play

- **Move** your Builder with **WASD** or arrow keys
- **Aim** by moving your mouse — your Builder always faces the cursor
- **Build Towers** by clicking a tower in the bottom menu, then left-clicking on grass to place it
- **Cycle Elements** using keys **1–5** or click tower icons directly
- **Start Waves** by clicking the wave button or pressing **Enter**
- **Pause** the action with **P**

### Elemental Weakness Cycle
Fire beats Air, Air beats Earth, Earth beats Water, Water beats Fire. Build a balanced mix to exploit enemy weaknesses!

### Survive All 20 Waves
Each wave brings tougher enemies, including massive bosses. Protect the Bastion at all costs!
