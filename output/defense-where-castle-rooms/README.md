

# SHATTERED VAULT
### *Architect of Ruin*

Welcome to the **Shattered Vault**, a high-stakes 2D top-down tower-defense hybrid where the walls themselves are your weapons. The castle is under a relentless siege, and as the spectral **Spirit** of the architect, you must dynamically reshape the dungeon floor in real-time to survive the incoming waves.

Are you ready to bend the layout of reality and turn your own castle into a labyrinth of death?

---

## 🎮 How to Play

*   **Navigate as the Spirit:** Glide through the castle rooms with precise, grid-locked physics. Your movement is anchored to an 8x8 sub-grid, ensuring pixel-perfect alignment as you position yourself for the ultimate tactical swap.
*   **The Power of Spatial Swap:** Select two adjacent rooms and initiate a **Spatial Swap**. Watch as they slide along a smooth Bézier curve and rotate 90 degrees, reshaping the battlefield instantly.
*   **Design the Perfect Killbox:** 
    *   Funnel enemy hordes into **Trap Rooms** (Magenta) filled with spikes and poison.
    *   Shield your vulnerable **Utility Rooms** (Amber) by surrounding them with **Barricade Rooms** (Slate) and **Combat Rooms** (Cyan).
*   **Outsmart the Enemy AI:** Enemies dynamically recalculate their paths using A* pathfinding the second you rearrange the map. Force them into dead ends, cut off their access to healing rooms, and protect the castle gates at all costs.

---

## ⚙️ Core Mechanics

*   **Real-Time Rearrangement:** Swap rooms mid-wave to redirect enemy swarms. The tactical pause of a swap is your moment to breathe—use it wisely!
*   **Dynamic Physics:** Experience elastic collision bounces when hitting walls, and a smooth spring-damper velocity system for fluid spectral movement.
*   **Wave-Based Siege:** Enemies spawn in zones. Keep them away from the gates; if a zone falls, the castle's integrity weakens. Hold the line!
