# 🀄 Mahjong Solitaire Canvas

A sleek, browser-based tile-matching game where you clear the board by pairing free tiles. No downloads, no installs—just pure mahjong magic in your browser.

## How to Play
1. Click a **free tile** (not covered by another tile).
2. Click a matching **free tile** to make them vanish.
3. Clear all 144 tiles to win!
4. Stuck? Hit **Shuffle** when no moves remain.

Drag tiles around for that satisfying springy feel, then snap them into place.
