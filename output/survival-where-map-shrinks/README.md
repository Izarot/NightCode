# Void Closure

> *Last one standing when the lights go out.*

A neon-drenched survival arena where the map is your enemy. Drop in, loot up, and outlast 11 others as a living black hole devours everything around you.

## How to Play

**Move** with `WASD` (or a gamepad stick). **Aim** with the mouse. **Fire** with left-click. **Scope-in** with right-click (Sniper/Lance).

1. **Spawn** on the edge of the island. The map is huge — for now.
2. **Scavenge.** Hit buildings and landmarks first. Loot glows, so keep your eyes peeled.
3. **Fight.** Every weapon is deadly. Positioning matters more than aim. The closer you are, the louder the guns.
4. **Stay inside the circle.** The Safe Zone pulses blue. Outside it, gravity hates you and the void chips your HP.
5. **Survive the phases.** The circle shrinks every couple of minutes — faster, tighter, deadlier.
6. **Win.** Be the last player alive when only the singularity remains. You become the **Void Champion**.

### Quick Tips
- 🎯 The **Repeater (SMG)** shreds at close range while you strafe.
- 💎 **Shield Orbs** cap at 100 — grab them whenever you can.
- 🚨 **Supply Drops** beam down every 60 seconds. High risk, Epic-tier reward.
- 🌀 If you're caught outside the circle, **run diagonal** — straight lines get sucked faster.
- 🔭 **Lance (Sniper)** is hitscan. Lead smart, not far.
