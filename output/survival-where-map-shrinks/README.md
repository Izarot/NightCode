# The Void Tether

Survive the endless consumption! Defend your shrinking perimeter, build turrets, and outlast the encroaching black hole. Don't let the void swallow you whole.

## How to Play
* **Harvest & Build:** Scrape up materials from the shrinking hex grid to construct perimeter turrets. Turrets anchor to hexes and hold the line against Hulks.
* **Defend the Tether:** Keep an eye on the circular cyan border. If it collapses entirely, you're dead. The safe zone shrinks exponentially over time.
* **Dash Out of Dodge:** When Hulks get too close, unleash a 2.5x speed burst (0.15s duration, 2s cooldown) to escape the gravitational pull.
* **Survive the Boss:** Every 90 seconds, the shrink rate doubles and a Boss Hulk enters the fray. Defeat it to pause the shrinkage for 15 seconds—a crucial breather!
* **Upgrade Up:** Spend scrap and bio-fibers to boost dash duration, max health, turret damage, and shrink-rate resistance.
