## 🎮 How to Play  
1. **Control Your Avatar**:  
   - **Tap/Click** or press **Space/Arrow Keys** to move.  
   - **Left/Right Arrows** or **A/D**: Switch lanes (3 lanes: left, center, right).  
   - **Up Arrow/W**: Jump over spikes or blades!  

2. **Rhythm is Key**:  
   - Obstacles appear in time with the beat. Hit them *exactly on the beat* to survive!  
   - **Perfect Timing** (±50ms) = +20 points + combo boost!  
   - **Good Timing** (±100ms) = +10 points.  
   - Miss the beat? Take damage! ❤️  

3. **Survive & Score**:  
   - Avoid walls, spikes, and rotating blades.  
   - Build combos to unlock score multipliers (up to 2x!).  
   - Last as long as possible or finish the song!
