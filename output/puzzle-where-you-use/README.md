# Magnet‑Key Puzzler

## How to Play
- **Move the magnet** – Click/Tap and drag (or use WASD/arrow keys) to place the glowing magnet cursor anywhere on the screen.  
- **Activate the field** – A quick click/tap fires a radial force (attraction or repulsion) that pulls or pushes ferromagnetic keys and metal objects.  
- **Guide the keys** – Use the magnet to steer each key onto its matching lock. Unlocking a lock opens the way to the next puzzle piece.  
- **Watch the budget** – Every magnet activation costs energy and counts as a move. Complete the level within the energy limit and move budget to earn stars.  
- **Avoid hazards** – Touching spikes or hazards resets the key and drains energy, so plan your moves carefully.  

## Controls
- **Desktop:** Left‑click to activate, right‑click (or Ctrl+click) to toggle polarity. Move the cursor with the mouse.  
- **Touch:** Tap to activate; hold to maintain the field. Two‑finger tap toggles polarity.  
- **Keyboard:** WASD/Arrow keys to move the magnet; Space to toggle polarity.  

## Goal
Unlock all required locks using the fewest moves and energy. Each level awards 1‑3 stars based on efficiency, time, and remaining energy.
