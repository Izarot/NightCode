## 🎮 How to Play

Welcome, Colony Leader! Your mission is simple: **grow your tiny ant startup into an underground empire**. Here's the buzz:

### 🏗️ Build Your Empire
- **Click** berry bushes, logs, and water puddles to assign workers
- **Right-click** underground terrain to **dig tunnels** (10 food per cell)
- Construct chambers like **Nurseries**, **Fungus Farms**, and **Barracks** to expand your colony's capabilities

### ⚔️ Defend Your Colony
- Beetles lurk above ground — rally your **soldiers** to fight them off
- Watch out for **anteater shadows** (that circle isn't friendly!)
- Other ant colonies will raid your food stores if you get too cozy

### 👑 Protect the Queen
- Your **queen** is everything. If she falls, the colony falls.
- Keep her fed, guarded, and happy in her royal chamber

### 🌙 Survive the Cycle
- Days last **5 real-time minutes** — plan accordingly
- Manage **food**, **water**, and **materials** to keep your workers busy
- Reach **Day 30 with 500+ ants** for the Survival Victory!
