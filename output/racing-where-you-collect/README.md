# Gear Drift – 2D HTML5 Racing Game  

## How to Play  
- **Desktop:**  
  - **W / ↑** – Accelerate  
  - **S / ↓** – Brake  
  - **A / ←** & **D / →** – Steer left/right  
- **Mobile / Touch:**  
  - Left third of screen – Steer  
  - Center third – Accelerate  
  - Right third – Brake  
- **Gamepad:**  
  - Axis 0 – Steer  
  - Button 0 – Throttle  
  - Button 1 – Brake  

Collect floating gears to boost your speed, avoid the track edges, and finish the lap as fast as possible. Your lap time and gear count appear on the HUD; top times are saved to localStorage.
