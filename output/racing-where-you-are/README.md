# 🐌🚀 Snail Rocket Racer  

**Zoom past potholes, collect power-ups, and blast to the finish line in this high-speed, low-speed (but still fast!) adventure!**

## 🎮 How to Play  

1. **Pick Your Mode**: Choose a 3-lap race on one of the whimsical tracks.  
2. **Steer & Boost**: Use arrow keys (or WASD) to guide your snail. Hit **Spacebar** (or swipe up) to fire your rocket boost—just don’t overdo it!  
3. **Collect & Conquer**: Snatch coins, shells, and snail snacks for points and power-ups. Dodge oil slicks and potholes to keep your speed (and lives) intact.  
4. **Race to the End**: Finish all laps with the highest score (time + collectibles) to win.
