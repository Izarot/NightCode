# 🪨 Boulder Rush

**A high-speed downhill racing game where you ARE the boulder.**

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### Keyboard
| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `A` / `←` | Steer Left |
| `D` / `→` | Steer Right |
| `S` / `↓` / `Space` | Brake / Drift |
| `Shift` | Activate Boost (when meter full) |
| `P` / `Esc` | Pause |
| `R` | Restart Race |

### Touch
- **Left side** — Steer left/right
- **Right side** — Brake & Boost buttons

---

## 🏁 The Race
- **Auto-accelerates downhill** — you steer, brake, drift, and boost
- **3 AI rivals** — they fight for the same line, use boost pads, and collide
- **Track hazards** — oil slicks, mud, rocks, ramps, cliffs
- **Boost pads** — hit them for a 1.5s speed surge
- **Checkpoints** — every 25% for progress & respawn
- **Finish line** — ~75–120 seconds of pure momentum

## 📊 HUD at a Glance
- **Top-Left** — Placement (e.g. `2 / 4`)
- **Top-Center** — Progress bar with checkpoints
- **Top-Right** — Race timer `MM:SS.mmm`
- **Bottom-Left** — Speedometer arc + numeric speed
- **Bottom-Right** — Boost meter (press `SHIFT` when full)
- **Right** — Minimap with you, rivals, and checkpoints

## ⚡ Pro Tips
- **Drift through chicanes** — brake to reduce grip, steer hard, release to rocket out
- **Hug the inside line** — shorter distance, but watch for hazards
- **Save boost for straights** — or to recover from a bad hit
- **Don't stop moving** — below 40 px/s for 3s = respawn penalty
