# 🦁 Zoo Stack

Stack 'em high, keep 'em steady, and don't let the zoo fall over!

## How to Play

You are the zookeeper on feeder duty. Animals are falling from the top — catch the chaos and build the tallest tower you can!

*   **Move:** Use `←` / `→` arrow keys (or touch controls) to slide the Feeder left and right
*   **Drop:** Press `Space` to release the animal. It keeps your horizontal speed, so time your drop!
*   **Stack:** Land animals on top of the stack. Get within 10px of perfect alignment for a magnetic snap!
*   **Score:** +10 per animal stacked. Perfect stacks earn +50, a combo multiplier, and a sweet 1-second freeze!
*   **Watch Out:** Heavy bears are wide, penguins are light and narrow. If the stack leans too far or touches the fence, it's game over!
*   **Power-Up:** Grab a falling Cage to magnetically realign your stack for 3 seconds.

Build tall. Stay balanced. Perfect or perish!
