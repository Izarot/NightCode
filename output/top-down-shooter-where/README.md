A neon-drenched survival shooter rendered entirely on an HTML5 canvas. Blast geometric horrors, watch them shatter into smaller, angrier fragments, and pray your reflexes hold.

---

## 🎮 HOW TO PLAY

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| **Move** | `W` `A` `S` `D` or Arrow Keys |
| **Aim** | Mouse Cursor |
| **Fire** | Hold Left Mouse Button (or just survive — auto-fire has your back) |

### The Rules of the Void

1. **You are a triangle.** Aim with your mouse. Your weapon fires automatically toward the cursor.
2. **Enemies split when shot.** Hexagons crumble into squares. Squares shatter into diamonds. Diamonds just die (finally).
3. **Smaller = Faster.** Every split multiplies speed. Stay mobile, or become a shish-kebab of your own bullets.
4. **Three hits and you're out.** Each enemy passing through you costs one shield pip. You have three.
5. **Clear the wave.** Annihilate every last polygon to advance. The next wave brings more. It always brings more.

### Scoring
Every fragment destroyed adds to your total. Chain kills for maximum chaos. Then post your score to no one, because this runs locally.

## 🌀 FEATURES

- **Pure Canvas 2D rendering** — no frameworks, no dependencies, no nonsense
- **Synthesized audio** — every blip, bang, and descending swoosh generated on the fly
- **Object pooling** — bullets and particles recycled for buttery 60fps
- **Screen shake** — subtle devastation feedback when big ones go pop
- **Glow effects everywhere** — because it's not neon if it doesn't bloom

## 🚀 RUNNING THE GAME

1. Grab `index.html`
2. Open it in a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge — you know the drill)
3. Click anywhere to start
4. Click again when you inevitably die

That's it. No build step. No `npm install`. No existential dread.
