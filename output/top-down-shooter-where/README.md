## How to Play  

- **Move** – Use **WASD** or **Arrow Keys** for 8‑directional thrust.  
- **Fire** – Bullets shoot automatically; each shot spawns a short‑lived afterimage.  
- **Dash** – Press **Space** for a quick burst (0.5 s, 3 s cooldown) to dodge or reposition.  
- **Overcharge** – Activate the glowing icon (or pick up an Overcharge Orb) for 10 s: bullets go faster, afterimages last longer, and damage spikes.  
- **Survive** – Keep the health bar full; each enemy hit costs 10 % health.  
- **Destroy** – Blast through Grunts, Drones, and the massive Overseer boss to win.  

> **Tip:** Let the afterimages linger – they deal 50 % damage and can hit enemies even after the original bullet disappears!
