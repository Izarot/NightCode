# Splinter Shooter  
*Top‑down pixel‑art shooter where enemies split into smaller copies on defeat.*

## How to Play
- **Move:** WASD / Arrow keys (or gamepad left stick / touch joystick)  
- **Shoot:** Hold **Space** (or fire button) for a continuous laser beam  
- **Interact / Pause:** **E** to interact, **Esc** to pause/open menu  
- **Goal:** Survive waves, defeat bosses, and maximize your score.  
  - When an enemy dies it splits into smaller, faster copies (up to depth 3).  
  - Grab power‑ups (ammo, speed boost, shield, split‑multiplier) to stay alive longer.  
  - Score bonuses for destroying split chains quickly and for collecting orbs.
