# Shield‑Burst

A fast‑paced 2‑D top‑down shooter where you dodge, shoot, and unleash a temporary shield to survive endless waves of enemies.

## How to Play
- **Move**: Arrow keys or WASD (touch joystick on mobile).  
- **Shoot**: Click or press Space to fire the primary pistol.  
- **Shield**: Press **Shift** (or right‑click) to activate the shield for 3 seconds; cooldown 5 seconds. While active you cannot be damaged and enemy bullets are absorbed or reflected.  
- **Upgrade**: Earn points by killing enemies and use them to unlock weapon mods, faster shield recharge, or longer shield duration.  
- **Survive**: Stay alive as long as possible; each wave increases enemy speed and numbers.  

## Controls (Desktop)
| Action | Key |
|--------|-----|
| Move | WASD / Arrow keys |
| Shoot | Space |
| Shield | Shift |
| Pause | P |

## Controls (Mobile)
- Tap‑and‑drag virtual joystick to move.  
- Tap the shield button (top‑right) to activate.
