# Slow Burn: A Top-Down Shooter

Take control of a neon ship on a chaotic arena battlefield—then chill the enemy horde by painting the stage with lingering **Slow Fields**.

## How to Play

- **Move:** WASD or Arrow Keys
- **Aim & Shoot:** Mouse; right-click enables auto-fire
- **Dash:** Shift or Ctrl—perfect for escaping a swarm
- **Use Slow Fields:** Your shots create pulsing zones that slow enemies by up to 50%. Overlap them for maximum crowd control!
- **Manage Your Resources:** Watch your ammo, dash cooldown, active fields, and health.
- **Grab Power-Ups:** Boost field size, duration, or other abilities.
- **Survive:** Clear 10 waves per level and defeat every boss to win the game.

**Tip:** Don’t just chase enemies—slow them, trap them, and let the battlefield do the heavy lifting.
