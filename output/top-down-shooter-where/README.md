# Shieldstorm Arena

**A fast-paced 2D arena shooter where you command a glowing ship through waves of enemies, deploy shields, and survive longer than your opponents.**

## How to Play

### Core Mechanics
- **Move** with WASD or arrow keys at up to 5 px/frame speed
- **Shoot** with Left Click or Space (projectiles travel at 15 px/frame)
- **Deploy Shield** with `F` — creates a protective bubble (radius 40px) that lasts 3 seconds
- **Power Up** with spread shots, speed boosts, or extended shield duration

### Combat System
| Element | Damage | Notes |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Melee enemy | 15 | Direct contact |
| Ranged enemy | 10–25 | Varies by type |
| Turret | 12 | Slow but persistent |
| Projectile | 10–25 | Depends on enemy type |

### Game Loop
1. **Survive waves** — each wave introduces new enemy types and difficulty
2. **Manage resources** — shield cooldown (8s), power-up timers
3. **Optimize** — use shield wisely, dodge attacks, maximize kills
