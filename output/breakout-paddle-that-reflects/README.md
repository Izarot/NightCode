# Angle Breakout  

A neon‑lit twist on the classic Breakout where the paddle’s angle is **dynamically calculated** from the ball’s exact contact point. Master the bounce, rack up points, and survive the ever‑changing brick gauntlet!  

## How to Play  
- **Move the paddle** by dragging your mouse (or finger) left‑right.  
- **Aim** the ball by positioning the paddle under the part of the ball you want to hit – the farther left or right you strike, the steeper the angle.  
- **Destroy bricks** by hitting them; each brick’s color tells you its point value.  
- **Collect power‑ups** that drop from broken bricks for extra fun (wide paddle, sharp angle, multi‑ball, etc.).  
- **Clear the level** by breaking all bricks. New levels bring denser grids and moving barriers.  

## Controls  
- **Mouse / Touch:** Click‑and‑drag to move the paddle.  
- **Keyboard:** Press **SPACE** to start/pause, **R** to restart, **M** to mute.
