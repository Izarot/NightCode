# 🏙️ CityBuilder 2D

Welcome, Mayor! Grab your hard hat and your city planning hat—it's time to turn a blank patch of land into a bustling metropolis! 🏗️✨

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Pick Your Zones** 🏘️🏢🏭
   - **Residential (R)** – Light blue tiles, where your citizens live.
   - **Commercial (C)** – Orange tiles, where they shop 'til they drop.
   - **Industrial (I)** – Gray tiles, where the magic (and pollution) is made.
   - **Parks (P)** – Green tiles, for happiness and fresh air.
   - **Special (S)** – Schools, hospitals, and more!

2. **Build & Upgrade** 🛠️
   - Click or tap a zone to drop buildings (1‑4 tiles big!).
   - Watch them grow with snazzy construction particles.
   - Upgrade up to 3 levels for bigger, better, and more profitable output.

3. **Manage Resources** ⚡💧🗑️💰
   - Keep an eye on **Energy**, **Water**, and **Waste**.
   - Collect **City Bucks** from taxes and spend them wisely on upgrades and policies.

4. **Grow Your Population** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
   - Balance jobs, housing, and happiness.
   - More parks = happier citizens. More industry = more cash, but watch the pollution meter!

5. **Pan, Zoom, and Explore** 🗺️
   - **Desktop:** Right‑click drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
   - **Mobile:** Long‑press to pan, pinch to zoom.

6. **Make Policies** 📜
   - Hit the **Policies** button for tax tweaks, green energy subsidies, and zoning reforms. Each decision shapes your city's future!

7. **Save Your Masterpiece** 💾
   - Use the **Save/Load** button to preserve your city and return later to keep building.
