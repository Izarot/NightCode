# Urban Canvas  
*Build your dream metropolis, one block at a time.*

## How to Play
- **Select a zone** from the left palette (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, or Utilities).  
- **Drag the ghost preview** onto the grid; green means you can build, red means nope.  
- **Click to place** roads, power lines, water pipes, and buildings.  
- **Watch your city grow**—keep an eye on money, population, power, and water in the top HUD.  
- **Press `R`** to rotate a building before dropping it.  
- **Use the wrecking ball** to demolish mistakes (you’ll get a partial refund).  
- **Zoom with the mouse wheel**, pan with middle‑mouse or `WASD`, and hit `Space` to pause/speed up.  
- **Meet the demand meter** to unlock higher‑density skyscrapers and fancy services.  
- **Avoid bankruptcy and blackouts**—happy citizens pay taxes; unhappy ones riot.
