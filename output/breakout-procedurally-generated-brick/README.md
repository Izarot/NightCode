# Procedural Breakout

Smash neon bricks. Defy gravity. Become the paddle wizard.

## How to Play

1. Slide the paddle across the playfield.
2. Launch the ball with a tap, click, or keypress.
3. Destroy every destructible brick before the ball escapes.
4. Chain combos, catch power-ups, and survive increasingly wild procedural levels.

## Controls

- **Move:** Arrow keys, `A`, or `D` — or move the mouse/touch
- **Launch:** `Space`, `Enter`, or tap the playfield
- **Pause:** `Esc` or `P`

Choose a seed for a reproducible challenge—or roll a fresh run and let chance build the wall.
