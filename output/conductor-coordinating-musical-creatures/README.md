# SYNCOPATION

## How to Play

You are the **Maestro**, standing in a silent, monochrome void. Wield your **Baton** to conduct **Ensembles**—musical creatures—precisely on the **Global Beat**. Each gesture injects color, geometry, and melody into the world.

### Core Loop
1. **Analyze** the Beat Map and upcoming creature cues.
2. **Queue** your gesture using the Baton.
3. **Execute** on the Downbeat with perfect timing.
4. Watch as Creatures perform and the world comes alive.
5. Build your **Combo** and **Harmony** to unlock synesthetic chaos.

### Gestures
- **Tap / Downbeat** — Strike the beat to trigger **Pulsars** (percussion) and **Resonators** (bass).
- **Hold + Drag** — Sustain to command **Drifters** (pads) and **Chorists** (vocals).
- **Flick / Release** — Cue **Arpents** (melodies) with a swift motion.
- **Dynamic Sweep** — Use your off-hand (or right stick) to crescendo or decrescendo the ensemble.

### Timing Windows
- **Perfect:** ±45ms — Maximum score and Harmony boost.
- **Good:** ±90ms — Decent payoff.
- **Miss:** ±150ms — Silence falls.

### Harmony System
Keep different creature types close together to generate **Harmony Particles**. Chain reactions across your ensemble to fill the **Harmony Meter** and unlock dazzling visual effects.
