# 🌌 Curved Void Pong

The zero-gravity arcade classic where your paddle has a curve and your momentum is your best friend.

## How to Play

- Pilot your floating concave paddle using **W/A/S/D** or the **Arrow Keys**.
- Thrust, coast, brake — zero gravity means momentum is everything.
- Hit the energy ball dead center for a straight shot, or off the curve for a sneaky angle.
- Score by bouncing the ball past your opponent’s paddle.
- Every rally gets faster. Blink and you’ll miss it.

## Controls

- **Spacebar**: Launch the ball
- **P**: Pause
- **ESC**: Menu
