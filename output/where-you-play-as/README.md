# 🐟 Fish Jump Adventure

## Play This Aquatic Platformer!

Dive into an underwater adventure where you control a golden fish navigating treacherous waters, leaping between floating islands, dodging hungry predators, and collecting treasures to reach the final destination.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Space / Enter / W** | Jump (when grounded) |
| **← / →** | Swim left/right |

1. Click **"Start Game"** on the main menu
2. Choose your difficulty (Easy → Normal → Hard)
3. Guide your fish through each level, avoid sharks and eels, and collect fish food/gems!
