# 🦌 Crimson Trail: The Hunt for the Hollow Stag

*A top-down stealth-tracking adventure through a haunting procedural forest.*

---

## How to Play

**Objective:** Track the legendary Hollow Stag to three ancient forest circles and observe it for 30 seconds without being detected.

### Controls
| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **WASD / Arrow Keys** | Move |
| **Shift** | Crouch (stealth mode) |
| **1** | Tracker Lens (reveals tracks & scent) |
| **2** | Sound Flare (distraction tool) |
| **3** | Stealth Cloak (10s invisibility) |
| **E** | Interact |

### Tips for Survival
- 🦶 **Stay quiet** — Running creates noise waves that attract the beast
- 👀 **Watch the meter** — When the detection bar hits 100% while in sight, it's game over
- 🌿 **Use tall grass** — Crouch-walk through foliage to mask your tracks
- 🎯 **Lead it astray** — Sound Flares can buy you precious seconds
- 🔍 **Follow the tracks** — Yellow glows mean old tracks, orange means fresh!
