# ⚔️ The Gilded Key

A atmospheric top-down puzzle-adventure game set in a cursed gothic castle. Explore haunting halls, solve mind-bending environmental puzzles, and battle traps to recover the legendary Gilded Sigils and unlock the path to the throne room.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### Controls
*   **Move:** `WASD` or Arrow Keys
*   **Sword Strike:** `J` or Left Click
*   **Shield:** `K` or Right Click (Hold to block/reflect)
*   **Interact:** `E` or Space
*   **Pause:** `Esc`

### Core Loop
1.  **Explore:** Navigate the dark corridors of the castle, avoiding spikes, lasers, and traps.
2.  **Puzzle Solve:** Manipulate the environment using pushable blocks, pressure plates, levers, and rotating mirrors to open gated exits.
3.  **Ability Use:** Use your sword to cut chains and trigger switches, and your shield to reflect light beams or block hazards.
4.  **Collect:** Hunt down three **Gilded Sigils** hidden throughout the castle.
5.  **Win:** Place the sigils on the throne room pedestals, open the grand doors, and claim your victory!
