# 🐌 SNAIL SPRINT

**The world's slowest racing sensation!** Guide your snail through treacherous terrain, master energy management, and out-slime the competition in this delightfully sluggish battle of wits.

## 🎮 How to Play

**Navigate your snail to the finish line first!** But beware—snails aren't built for speed.

### Core Mechanics
- **Hold / Click** to consume energy and accelerate (drains at 2%/frame)
- **Release** to coast and regenerate energy (1%/frame)
- **Spacebar** for a lightning-fast Shell Dash (costs 15% energy, 4s cooldown)
- **E** to eject a Slime Bomb, creating slippery puddles for opponents

### Master the Tracks
- **Dirt**: Normal racing conditions
- **Mud**: Slows you down—avoid or plan accordingly
- **Ice**: Low friction means slippery slides!
- **Mushroom Boost**: Temporary speed burst—grab it!
- **Flowers**: Heal your energy—don't miss them!

### Outsmart Your Competition
Leave slime trails that make other snails slip (-40% speed for 0.8s). Use Shell Dashes to overtake, Slime Bombs to block, and terrain to your advantage.

**Complete 3 laps to win!** Your position, lap progress, and time are always displayed.
