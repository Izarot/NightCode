# Split Breakout

## How to Play
Use your paddle to bounce the ball and destroy all the bricks! Hit special striped bricks to split the ball into multiple balls for more chaos and points. Collect power-ups like the Split Orb and Multi-Ball to maximize your score. Watch out—only 3 lives allowed, and if all balls fall below the paddle, it's game over!

## Features
- **Ball Splitting:** Hit special bricks to split into 2-4 balls (up to 8 max!)
- **Power-Ups:** Temporarily enable splitting on any brick or increase split count
- **Retro Pixel Art:** Classic 16-bit style with glowing effects and particle trails
- **10 Levels:** Increasing difficulty with denser brick layouts and special bricks
- **Boss Level:** Take on a massive brick structure in the final showdown

## Controls
- **Arrow Keys** or **Mouse** to move the paddle
- Smooth acceleration and friction-based movement
- Realistic ball physics with angle-based bouncing
