# Cosmic Catch  

## 🎮 Overview  
Dive into a neon‑lit cosmic ocean where you pilot a space‑fishing vessel, cast a glowing line, and reel in dazzling celestial creatures. Simple controls, endless challenges, and a splash of retro arcade vibes await!

##  ste How to Play  

1. **Start** – Click **Start** (or press Space) to launch your vessel.  
2. **Cast** – Drag (or swipe) to set the angle and power of your line.  
3. **Reel** – Hold the mouse button / tap to pull the line back.  
4. **Hook** – When a cosmic entity contacts the hook, a tug‑force meter appears.  
5. **Catch or Release** –  
   - **Catch**: Keep reeling until the meter fills → entity is secured.  
   - **Release**: Let the line go slack to watch the creature swim away (risking a miss).  
6. **Score & Upgrade** – Earn points, unlock upgrades (stronger line, faster reel, bigger hook).  
7. **Repeat** – Keep casting until the line snaps, you run out of time, or you crash into a hazard.  

## 🎛️ Controls  

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Drag / Swipe** | Set cast angle & power |
| **Mouse button / Touch‑hold** | Reel in |
| **WASD / Arrow keys** (optional) | Move vessel (drift left/right/up/down) |
| **Spacebar** | Quick‑cast (default power) |
| **Esc** | Pause / open menu |
