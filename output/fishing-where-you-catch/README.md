# Ocean Cleanup Quest

Dive into the waves and become the ultimate ocean hero! Cast your line, reel in trash, and restore the seas to their former glory.

## How to Play

1. **Move Your Vessel**  
   Use the arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen joystick to navigate your trusty fishing boat through the rolling blue.

2. **Cast Your Line**  
   Press `Space` (or tap and drag on mobile) to throw your line toward floating debris. Aim carefully—different trash types behave differently!

3. **Reel It In**  
   Hold the cast button and stay in the sweet spot of the reel meter to pull garbage aboard. Miss the sweet spot? Your progress slows down!

4. **Clean & Upgrade**  
   Every piece of junk you collect fills up the Ocean Health Meter and earns Clean Points. Use those points to upgrade your ship, extend your line, or unlock powerful new gear.

5. **Save the Ocean**  
   Fill the Ocean Health Meter to 100% before time runs out to complete each level and unlock the next polluted zone.

## Garbage Guide

| Type             | Reel Time | Points | Special Effect                     |

## Pro Tips

- Watch out for buoys—they’ll bump your ship and slow you down!
- Upgrade your line length early to reach those hard-to-get spots.
- Keep an eye on your timer in Time Attack Mode!
