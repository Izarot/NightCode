# Pixel Catch: Reef Runner

## 🎮 How to Play

**Cast Your Line:** Click and hold to set power, release to cast toward the bobber!

**Wait for a Bite:** Keep an eye on your bobber — when the "!" appears, click quickly to hook the fish!

**Reel It In:** Click rhythmically to reel, but watch the tension meter! Let it fill up and your line snaps!

**Catch & Collect:** Land your fish, sell them for gold, and upgrade your gear to explore new zones!

**Explore Zones:** Unlock new areas like Kelp Forest and Shipwreck Bay, each with unique fish and challenges!

## 🔧 Controls

- **WASD / Arrow Keys:** Steer your boat
- **Mouse:** Aim and cast your line
- **Space / Click:** Reel during the fishing minigame
- **ESC:** Pause menu

- 4 unlockable zones with distinct visuals
