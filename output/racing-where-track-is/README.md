# 🎲⚡ DOMINO RACER ⚡🎲

> *A 2D side-scrolling racing game where the track is made of dominoes — and they don't stay standing.*

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

Buckle up, speedster. Your racetrack is a 5,000px gauntlet of **neon dominoes** waiting to topple. Hit one too slow, and watch the chain reaction close behind you. Hit one just right, and ride the chaos to victory.

### Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `W` / `↑` / `Space` | 🚀 Accelerate |
| `S` / `↓` | 🛑 Brake / Reverse |
| `A` / `←` | ⬅️ Steer Left |
| `D` / `→` | ➡️ Steer Right |
| `Shift` | ⚡ **BOOST** (limited fuel!) |
| `R` | 🔄 Restart Race |
| `ESC` | ⏸️ Pause |
| `M` | 🔇 Mute Audio |

### Pro Tips

- 💥 **Topple for points** — every domino you knock down adds to your score, and chain reactions stack the multiplier!
- ⚡ **Boost wisely** — your fuel bar recharges slowly. Save it for straightaways or recoveries.
- 💚 **Watch your health** — fast-falling dominoes hurt. You start with 3 hits.
- 🏁 **Checkpoint every 500m** — wreck too badly and you'll respawn at the last one.
- 🏆 **Speed bonus** — finish under 60 seconds for a sweet point multiplier.

### Scoring

- ✅ **+1000** per checkpoint
- ⚡ **+10/sec** for fast times
- 🎲 **+5** per domino toppled
- 🔥 **Combo multiplier** on chain reactions
- 💎 **+500** flawless finish bonus
