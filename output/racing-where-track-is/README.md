# Resonant Velocity

## How to Play
You’re behind the wheel of a neon car on a road that literally moves to the music. Hit the beat, hit the boost, don’t hit the pits.

- Steer with **WASD** or **arrow keys**.
- Accelerate with **Up**, brake with **Down**.
- Hold **Space** to drift through sharp beats.
- Chase high **Amplitude** sections for a **Resonance Boost**.
- Survive your lap. The track is infinite, the music is not.
