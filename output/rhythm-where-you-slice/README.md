# RHYTHM SLICE: KATANA FRUIT

## A Rhythm Game Where Precision Meets Swordplay

**RHYTHM SLICE: KATANA FRUIT** is a high-octane rhythm game that fuses fast-paced sword combat with precise timing mechanics. Slash falling fruit in sync with the beat, chain combos, and avoid penalties—all while feeling the power of your katana!

---

## How to Play

1. **Control Your Katana** – Use your mouse to track the cursor and swing your blade. The katana follows your cursor with 1:1 precision.
2. **Time Your Slashes** – Each fruit falls on a beat. Hit them within the timing window for maximum points!
   - **Perfect (±50ms)** → 300 pts + 1.2× multiplier
   - **Good (±100ms)** → 100 pts
   - **Miss (>100ms)** → No points, combo resets
3. **Build Combos** – Chain multiple slices into one slash for bonus points (up to 5-fruit combo).
4. **Avoid Bombs** – Red "Bomb" fruits deduct 200 points and break your combo—dodge them or take the hit!
