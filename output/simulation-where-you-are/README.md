# 🌊 Lumina Keeper

## 🎮 How to Play

You're the lone lighthouse keeper on a stormy night. Ships are sailing toward your rocky island, and it's your job to guide them safely to harbor using your trusty lighthouse!

### The Basics:
- **Rotate the Beam:** Use your mouse to drag and aim the lighthouse beam, or hit the left/right arrow keys.
- **Match the Color:** Each ship needs a specific colored light — White (1), Red (2), or Green (3). Switch colors to match!
- **Flash the Pattern:** Some ships need special flash patterns — Continuous (C), Double-Flash (D), or Morse S.O.S. (M).
- **Guide Them In:** When a ship sees the right color and pattern, it'll change course toward the harbor.
- **Don't Crash:** If ships hit the rocks or sail away without guidance, they're lost!

### Power Moves:
- **Super Flash:** Hit SPACE for a burst that guides any nearby ship — but it costs energy!
- **Slow Mode:** Hold SHIFT to rotate your beam slowly for precise aiming.
- **Focus Lock:** Click on a ship to make your beam automatically track it for a few seconds.

### Survive the Night:
- Each wave brings more ships, faster speeds, and trickier patterns.
- Storms roll in, reducing visibility and adding wind drift.
- Manage your energy carefully — run out, and your light fades forever.

Guide as many ships as you can and become the legend of Lumina Island!
