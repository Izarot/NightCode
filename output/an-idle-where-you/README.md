# Assembly Line Optimizer

## How to Play

Welcome to **Assembly Line Optimizer** — the idle game where you become the mastermind behind the most efficient factory floor ever built!

### The Basics
- **Watch your assembly line come to life!** Items spawn at the start, travel along conveyors, pass through workstations, and get collected at the end.
- **Upgrade stations** to reduce processing time and boost throughput.
- **Adjust conveyor speeds** using the slider to fine-tune your flow.
- **Reposition stations** by dragging them to different slots to optimize your layout.
- **Earn currency** for every item completed and spend it on powerful upgrades.

### Getting Started
1. **Observe the Flow**: Watch how items move and identify bottlenecks.
2. **Upgrade Smart**: Focus on the slowest workstation first to maximize gains.
3. **Speed It Up**: Increase belt speed once your stations can keep up.
4. **Think Strategically**: Rearrange stations to balance your production line.
5. **Go Prestige**: Once you hit major milestones, reset for permanent multipliers and prestige points!

### Pro Tips
- Keep an eye out for **breakdowns** — they’ll halt production temporarily.
- Unlock the **research tree** for game-changing permanent bonuses.
- Don’t forget to **collect your offline earnings** when you return!
