# 🏎️ Neon Road Runner 🏎️

*A wild 2D side-scrolling racing game built on pure HTML5 Canvas!*

## 🎮 What Is This Thing?

Ever dreamed of cruising down an endless neon highway at breakneck speeds, dodging hot pink barriers and leaping over terrifying gaps? **Neon Road Runner** is an endless arcade-style auto-runner where YOU control a sleek cyan sports car tearing through a synthwave dreamscape. One button. Infinite road. How long can you survive?

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **Press `SPACE`** (or tap on mobile) to start your engine
2. Your car auto-runs down the infinite highway — no need to steer!
3. **`SPACE` / `↑` / `W` / Tap** to **JUMP** over obstacles
4. Time your hops to clear:
   - 🟥 **Barriers** — chunky blocks
   - 🔺 **Spikes** — pointy triangles
   - 📏 **Hurdles** — low but tricky
   - 🕳️ **Gaps** — MISSING ROAD (big jumps required!)
5. Rack up distance, dodge obstacles, and chase that high score!
6. Smash into anything and it's **GAME OVER** — press SPACE to go again

### Pro Tips 🛹
- **Coyote Time is real!** You can still jump ~100ms after driving off a ledge
- **Jump Buffering** lets you queue a jump before landing
- Your **speed increases** the longer you survive — your score multiplier grows too!
- Watch for the **NEW HIGH SCORE!** glory if you beat your best
