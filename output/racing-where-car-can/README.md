## \ud83d\udd79\ufe0f How to Play

1. **Press `SPACE`** (or tap on mobile) to start your engine
2. Your car auto-runs down the infinite highway \u2014 no need to steer!
3. **`SPACE` / `\u2191` / `W` / Tap** to **JUMP** over obstacles
4. Time your hops to clear:
   - \ud83d\udfe5 **Barriers** \u2014 chunky blocks
   - \ud83d\udd3a **Spikes** \u2014 pointy triangles
   - \ud83d\udccf **Hurdles** \u2014 low but tricky
   - \ud83d\udd73\ufe0f **Gaps** \u2014 MISSING ROAD (big jumps required!)
5. Rack up distance, dodge obstacles, and chase that high score!
6. Smash into anything and it's **GAME OVER** \u2014 press SPACE to go again

### Pro Tips \ud83d\udef9

- **Jump Buffering** lets you queue a jump before landing
- Your **speed increases** the longer you survive \u2014 your score multiplier grows too!
- Watch for the **NEW HIGH SCORE!** glory if you beat your best
