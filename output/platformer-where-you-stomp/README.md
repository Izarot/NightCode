# Stomp-Shift: The Platformer

Stomp enemies. Ride them. Don't fall when they wake up!

## How to Play

- **Move:** Arrow Keys / A,D
- **Jump:** Space / W / Up — hold for higher, release for a short hop
- **Stomp:** Land on an enemy while falling to turn it into a glowing platform for 5 seconds
- **Combo:** Chain stomps without taking damage to raise your multiplier
- **Survive:** You have 3 hearts. Platforms crack and collapse when their timer ends, so keep moving!
