# 🎵 ChromaBeat Symphony

A neon-soaked rhythm game where you're equal parts DJ and conductor. Match falling colored notes to build layers of drums, bass, melody, and effects—one perfect chain at a time.

## How to Play

- **Red** = Drums | **Blue** = Bass | **Green** = Melody | **Yellow** = FX
- Press **D, F, J, K** (or click/tap the lanes) to hit notes in time.
- Chain matching colors to lock in musical layers and grow your song.
- Don't miss! Breaking a chain pauses that instrument and resets your combo.
- Build the full symphony and watch your neon timeline fill with stacked color blocks.
