# 🚀 AstroDefender: Cosmic Shield  
**Defend the galaxy, one asteroid at a time!**  

## How to Play  
Command your space station in a chaotic battle against endless asteroid waves. Survive by strategically placing turrets, managing energy, and deploying shields. Can you last 20 waves?  

## Controls  
- **Move**: WASD or Arrow Keys  
- **Aim & Fire**: Turrets auto-target asteroids within their 180° firing arc.  
- **Abilities**: Click power-up icons (e.g., Laser Boost, Speed Surge) to activate.  

## Key Features  
- **Asteroid Mayhem**: Dodge Rocks (basic), split Meteors (split into smaller threats!), and flee Black Holes (they *suck*).  
- **Upgrade Your Arsenal**: Spend energy to boost turret damage, fire rate, or unlock Plasma Cannons.  
- **Survive the Storm**: Manage 1000 HP and deploy shields to tank damage.  
- **Cosmic Power-Ups**: Grab random boosts to turn the tide!  

## Progression  
Unlock new turrets, station skins, and climb the leaderboard. Survive 20 waves to win!
