# 🌌 BREAKOUT: PHASE SHIFT

> *Bricks don't break. They phase. Timing is everything.*

## 🎮 How to Play

### Controls
| Action | Keys |
|--------|------|
| Move Paddle | `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` or **Mouse** |
| Expand Paddle (Charged) | Hold `SPACE` |
| Phase Shift (Ghost Paddle) | Hold `SHIFT` |
| Pause | `P` or `ESC` |

### The Core Mechanic
Bricks cycle through **solid** ↔ **ghost** states on predictable timers. Hit them only during their **solid phase** — contact during ghost phase means they pass right through. Watch the thin progress bar above each brick to anticipate the shift.

### Pro Tips
- ⚡ **Phase Timing Bonus** — Smash a brick within the first 0.3s of its solid phase for +5 points
- 🔥 **Build Combos** — Chain hits without missing: 1× → 2× → 4× → 8× multiplier
- 🟣 **Phase Lock** power-up freezes all bricks solid — *chaos time*
- 🔴 **Shield** aura on your paddle saves you from one life loss
- 💎 **Earn 30 points** for top-row bricks (Fast Cycle, tricky timing)

### Levels
Clear all bricks across **5 levels** to win. Each level boosts ball speed by 10% and your score by 1.5×.
